Payment Institution License in Norway

A payment institution (betalingsforetak) licence in Norway lets you provide payment services from a Norwegian company. The Financial Supervisory Authority of Norway (Finanstilsynet) grants it under the Financial Institutions Act (finansforetaksloven). The application fee is NOK 30,000, and the file must be in Norwegian. A limited licence with somewhat lighter requirements covers money remittance up to an average of NOK 5 million a month.

Who needs a payment institution license in Norway?

Finanstilsynet says a company needs a licence to provide payment services as defined in finansavtaleloven 1-5. Those include account deposits and withdrawals, payment transactions, card-based payments, money remittance without an account agreement, and account information services. Whether you need one depends mainly on whether you hold or control other people's funds, initiate payments from another person's account, or retrieve account information for the account holder.

Finanstilsynet's page lists four forms of permission:

  • A full payment institution licence under finansforetaksloven 2-10, covering all payment services.
  • A payment initiation agent (betalingsfullmektig), with some application requirements waived and liability insurance or a guarantee required.
  • An account information agent (opplysningsfullmektig) under 2-10a. It is not a financial institution and has fewer requirements.
  • A limited licence for money remittance only, up to an average of NOK 5 million a month over 12 months, with the head office in Norway, no cross-border EEA activity and no agents.

If you issue stored value, see the EMI licence.

Payment institution license requirements in Norway

Capital and own funds. Initial capital is EUR 20,000, EUR 50,000 or EUR 125,000 depending on the services (PSD2 Art 7). Finanstilsynet's page does not give a separate capital figure for the limited licence, only that some requirements are lighter, so confirm it. Your application gives a plan for meeting initial capital and own funds.

Management and fit and proper. Board members, the managing director and key personnel get a suitability assessment on form KRT-1157, with a CV, criminal record certificates, diplomas and the company's own assessment of the board. Owners with qualifying holdings above 10% are assessed too.

Substance. The limited licence requires a head office in Norway. For a full licence, PSD2 Art 11(3) requires the head office in the same state as the registered office.

AML. The application has a risk assessment and AML procedures under hvitvaskingsloven 7 and 8.

Safeguarding. Customer funds must be safeguarded by the end of the business day after receipt, held separately or covered by an insurer or bank guarantee (finansforetaksloven 13-18). Payment initiation agents are exempt from this.

Professional indemnity. Payment initiation and account information agents need liability insurance or a guarantee, with a calculation of the minimum amount.

ICT and DORA. Governance and control systems are aligned with DORA.

How to get a payment institution license in Norway: process

  1. Choose the permission: full licence, payment initiation agent, account information agent or limited licence.
  2. Pay the NOK 30,000 fee and keep the receipt.
  3. Prepare the file in Norwegian: articles, a three-year organisation and business plan, owner information, suitability forms (KRT-1157), cover form KRT-1060, financial forecasts for three years, service and money flow descriptions, AML documents and the safeguarding procedures. A limited licence uses form KRT-1120, and the finansforetaksforskriften 3-2 form is not needed.
  4. File in Altinn, or by email to post@finanstilsynet.no.
  5. Finanstilsynet's normal processing time is about three months from payment of the fee. If the file is incomplete, the clock starts once everything is in (finansforetaksloven 3-2 fourth paragraph).

After you get the payment institution license

  • Finanstilsynet supervises you as a financial institution. An account information agent is not one.
  • Report incidents and keep safeguarding in place every business day.
  • Changes in the board or key roles need suitability assessment first, as the application forms show.
  • A full licence holder can serve other EEA states by notifying Finanstilsynet (PSD2 Art 28). The limited licence cannot.
  • Check the Financial Institutions Act for change of control rules before any share transfer. We did not read them for this page.

Payment institution in Norway FAQs

How much capital does a payment institution need in Norway?

EUR 20,000, EUR 50,000 or EUR 125,000 depending on the services (PSD2 Art 7; finansforetaksloven 3-4 second paragraph). Finanstilsynet's page gives no separate figure for the limited licence, so confirm it.

How long does Finanstilsynet take to decide?

About three months from payment of the fee. The clock starts when the application is complete.

How much does a Norwegian payment institution application cost?

NOK 30,000, including for a limited licence. Confirm the amount with Finanstilsynet because the page is undated.

What is the limited licence?

A licence under finansforetaksloven 2-10 fourth paragraph for money remittance without an account, if volume averages no more than NOK 5 million a month over 12 months. The head office must be in Norway, with no cross-border EEA activity and no agents.

What does a payment initiation agent need?

A betalingsfullmektig is exempt from some application requirements (finansforetaksloven 2-10 second paragraph). It needs professional indemnity insurance or a comparable guarantee that covers the areas where it operates (finansforetaksforskriften 2-17). The application includes the insurance form, a calculation of the minimum amount and the insurer's confirmation.

How fast must customer funds be safeguarded?

By the end of the business day after you receive them. The funds are held separately or covered by an insurer or bank guarantee (finansforetaksloven 13-18 first paragraph).

Do I need a licence to offer account information services only?

You apply as an account information agent (opplysningsfullmektig) under finansforetaksloven 2-10a. Finanstilsynet's page says it faces fewer requirements and is not a financial institution.

Can I file in English?

No. The application and attachments must be in Norwegian.

Does a Norwegian payment institution licence work in the rest of the EEA?

A full licence does, by notification (PSD2 Art 28). The limited licence does not.

How do I submit the application?

In Altinn, or by email to post@finanstilsynet.no, with the fee receipt attached.
