Crypto License in Norway

A MiCA crypto-asset service provider (CASP) licence in Norway comes from the Financial Supervisory Authority of Norway (Finanstilsynet). MiCA applies in Norway through the EEA Agreement, and the Crypto Asset Act (kryptoeiendelsloven) has been in force since 1 July 2025. The Norwegian transition ended on 1 July 2026, and the ESMA register shows six Norwegian entries.

Who needs a crypto license in Norway?

You need authorisation under MiCA Art 63, or a notification under Art 60, to provide crypto-asset services in Norway (MiCA Art 59, as Finanstilsynet explains). The Crypto Asset Act brings MiCA into Norwegian law, and Finanstilsynet says anyone offering these services in Norway needs a MiCA authorisation or a notification.

Other routes can fit better:

  • Art 60 notification for credit institutions, central securities depositories, investment firms, e-money institutions, fund managers and market operators. You may only offer services that match your existing licence, except credit institutions. An e-money institution may notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues. Notify through Altinn at least 40 working days before launch. AK Jensen Norway AS went this way and has been permitted since 2 February 2026.
  • Authorisation in another EEA state, then a passport into Norway.
  • An EMI licence as the base for issuing e-money tokens.

Crypto license requirements in Norway

Capital. Your minimum is EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000, depending on the services (MiCA Annex IV), or one quarter of fixed overheads if higher (Art 67).

Management and fit and proper. Board members, the managing director, actual managers and branch managers must be suitable, individually and collectively. Finanstilsynet asks for form KRT-1157, an ID copy, a CV, a police certificate and a collective competence matrix. The compliance officer and the AML officer also get a KRT-1157.

Substance. MiCA Art 59(2) requires a registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director. Finanstilsynet's page also mentions the EEA presence requirement. Ask what that means for your structure.

AML. The Crypto Asset Act came with an amendment to the Money Laundering Act (TFR II) that extends transfer information rules to CASPs and makes them subject to reporting duties.

Safeguarding. Client crypto-assets are not used for your own account, and client funds go to a credit institution or central bank by the end of the next business day (MiCA Art 70).

ICT and DORA. Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 applies, and MiCA Art 68 requires ICT and continuity arrangements under it.

How to get a crypto license in Norway: process

  1. Choose the services and capital class, or the Art 60 route if you hold another licence.
  2. Prepare the application form under delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/305 and 2025/306, and Finanstilsynet's checklist. The checklist explains how you meet MiCA and points to supporting documents by page number. Finanstilsynet says better quality shortens processing.
  3. Submit through Altinn with form KRT-3060. Finanstilsynet also refers to ESMA's Supervisory Briefing as practical guidance.
  4. Finanstilsynet acknowledges within 5 working days and checks completeness within 25 working days. It can ask for missing information with a deadline of 5 to 20 working days, and it can refuse incomplete applications.
  5. Once complete, the assessment period is 40 working days. Finanstilsynet may ask for more information within 20 working days. The decision is notified within 5 working days.
  6. Notify Finanstilsynet without undue delay if information in the application changes.

Finanstilsynet's CASP page does not say whether Norwegian is required or whether a pre-application meeting is offered. Ask both questions early.

After you get the crypto license

  • Finanstilsynet and ESMA list you in their registers.
  • Acquiring or increasing a qualifying holding (10% or more, or significant influence) needs prior approval. Crossing 20, 30 or 50 percent, or becoming a subsidiary, needs prior written notice. Disposals need prior written notice. Send notices to CASP@finanstilsynet.no.
  • Changes in the board or key roles need to be reported before new members take office.
  • To serve other EEA states, notify your home regulator under MiCA Art 65. Finanstilsynet accepts cross-border notices at post@finanstilsynet.no.

Is the Norwegian crypto transition over?

Yes. The Crypto Asset Act and MiCA applied from 1 July 2025. The Act's transition rule (section 19, second paragraph) first ran to 30 December 2025. Finanstilsynet extended it on 18 December 2025 to 30 June 2026 for exchange and custody providers that were registered under the Money Laundering Act. They could operate until 30 June 2026, or until they were granted or refused authorisation, whichever came first. Finanstilsynet's page says the arrangement ended on 1 July 2026, and unauthorised providers should wind down in an orderly way.

On 23 November 2026 the ESMA interim MiCA register lists six entries with Norway as the home state:

Crypto license in Norway FAQs

Does MiCA apply in Norway?

Yes. Norway is an EEA state. EEA Joint Committee Decision 41/2025 of 20 February 2025 incorporated Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 into the EEA Agreement, and the Crypto Asset Act has applied since 1 July 2025.

How much does a crypto license cost in Norway?

Capital is EUR 50,000, 125,000 or 150,000 by class, or a quarter of fixed overheads if higher (MiCA Art 67, Annex IV). We did not find a CASP application fee, so ask Finanstilsynet. Staff, custody, audit and legal work cost extra.

How long does Finanstilsynet take to decide?

25 working days to check completeness, then 40 working days to decide, with up to 20 working days of pause for questions (MiCA Art 63). The decision is notified within 5 working days.

How do I apply?

Through Altinn with form KRT-3060, the application form under delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/305 and 2025/306, and Finanstilsynet's checklist.

Is the Norwegian transition over?

Yes. It ended on 1 July 2026 after Finanstilsynet extended it from 30 December 2025 to 30 June 2026.

How many CASPs are authorised in Norway?

The ESMA interim register lists six Norwegian entries on 23 November 2026, one of them an Art 60 notification. Check the registers for the current list.

Can an EMI or investment firm offer crypto services without a full licence?

Yes, through an Art 60 notification at least 40 working days before launch, for services that match the existing licence. An EMI may notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues.

Does a Norwegian CASP licence work across the EEA?

Yes, by notification under MiCA Art 65.
