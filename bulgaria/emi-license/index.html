EMI License in Bulgaria

An electronic money institution (EMI) licence from the Bulgarian National Bank (BNB) lets a Bulgarian company issue e-money and provide payment services, then passport both across the EU and EEA. The BNB grants it under the Law on Payment Services and Payment Systems. The file goes to the BNB in Bulgarian, with translations, and that affects both your budget and your timetable.

Who needs an EMI license in Bulgaria?

You need one if you issue electronic money in Bulgaria: stored value held as a claim on you, issued against funds and accepted by people other than you. The ZPUPS requires a licence before issuing (Art 36(2)). Prepaid cards, wallets that hold a balance and some marketplace accounts fall in scope.

Another route can fit better in four cases.

  • You only move money and never hold stored value for customers. A payment institution licence in Bulgaria has lower capital, from EUR 20,000.
  • You are already an EMI in another EEA state. Passport into Bulgaria instead of applying again. Ordinance 16 Art 33 covers incoming EMIs.
  • You are small. The ZPUPS text published by the BNB contains no small EMI tier, although EMD2 Art 9 lets Member States offer one. Plan for full authorisation.
  • You only offer account information. That is a registration with the BNB, not a licence, with an application fee of EUR 2,500 (Ordinance 16 Art 70(4)).

An EMI may not issue e-money through agents (Ordinance 16 Art 32(1)). It can distribute and redeem e-money and provide payment services through agents (Art 32(2); EMD2 Art 3(4)).

EMI license requirements in Bulgaria

Legal form and substance

The applicant is, or is in the process of becoming, a limited liability company or a joint-stock company (ZPUPS Art 10(4)(1), applied by Art 37(2)). The registered office must be in Bulgaria and must match the head office in the Commercial Register and the place where management actually happens. You must carry out at least part of the activity in Bulgaria (Art 10(4)(4)). The BNB licenses only applicants with a Bulgarian registered office (Art 37(1)).

Capital and own funds

Initial capital is at least EUR 350,000, made up of items listed in Art 26(1)(a) to (e) of Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 (ZPUPS Art 38). It must be paid in when you obtain the licence. Own funds then stay at or above the higher of that figure and the calculated amount (Art 39). For e-money the calculation is at least 2% of average outstanding e-money (EMD2 Art 5(3)).

Fit and proper

Managers and qualifying shareholders go through the personal documents set out in Ordinance 16 (Arts 24 to 27 for EMIs). A person who wants to acquire or increase a qualifying holding needs the BNB's prior approval, and the BNB charges EUR 1,000 for that review (Ordinance 16 Art 70(6)). The origin of the capital must be transparent and lawful (ZPUPS Art 10(4)(3)).

Safeguarding

You protect funds received for e-money issued by segregation or by an insurance policy or guarantee (EMD2 Art 7(1); PSD2 Art 10(1)). Card-funded money must be safeguarded within 5 business days of issue (EMD2 Art 7(1)).

AML, governance and ICT

The file describes your organisational structure, risk procedures, internal control, business continuity and security policy, plus ICT arrangements under DORA, Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (PSD2 Art 5(1)). The AML manual covers your obligations under the Bulgarian anti-money laundering law. Our AML and compliance programme page explains what the BNB tests.

How to apply for an EMI license in Bulgaria

  1. Fix the product and the activities you will list, and check whether you also want payment services next to e-money. The ZPUPS lets you notify the BNB with the application that you will provide payment services as an additional activity (Art 37(4)).
  2. Set up the Bulgarian company, or start the incorporation, and pay in the EUR 350,000.
  3. Build the file listed in Ordinance 16 Art 23, using the BNB's EMI document list. The BNB publishes its licensing documents under the payment systems section of bnb.bg.
  4. Pay the EUR 5,000 fee to the BNB account shown on the BNB register page and attach the proof (Ordinance 16 Art 70(2)).
  5. File the application in writing to the BNB. Documents go in Bulgarian. Foreign-language documents go in the original with a Bulgarian translation, and formal documents are legalised. Copies go in on paper and on an electronic device (Ordinance 16 Art 3(3) and (5), applied to the EMI file by Art 23(2)).
  6. Answer BNB questions. The 3-month clock starts on receipt (ZPUPS Art 11(1)). If the file is incomplete, the BNB asks for the missing items, gives you up to 2 months to supply them, and then decides within 3 months of receiving them (Art 11(2) and (3)).
  7. After the licence, start business within 12 months or the BNB can withdraw it (ZPUPS Art 16(1)(1)).

A refused applicant can apply again no earlier than 6 months after the refusal takes effect (ZPUPS Art 15(2)).

The capital stays on your balance sheet. The BNB's annual supervision charges are not in Ordinance 16, so check them on bnb.bg before you budget.

Other cost lines, with no amounts here: the Bulgarian legal entity and registered office, local management and staff, certified translations and legalisation of documents, audit, the safeguarding account or insurance, AML and ICT systems, and legal and consulting support. After the BNB grants the license

  • The BNB enters you in its public register of licensed EMIs (ZPUPS Art 19). The register is published as a spreadsheet on bnb.bg, and its EMI file showed an update dated 23 November 2026 when we checked.
  • You report own funds, financial statements and changes to the BNB. Material changes to the file, including managers and qualifying holdings, need notification or approval.
  • Passport. Tell the BNB which states, services, agents or branches you intend to use. The home regulator forwards the notice within 1 month and the host assesses it within 1 month (PSD2 Art 28(2)). Agents abroad can distribute and redeem e-money and provide payment services, but cannot issue it (Ordinance 16 Art 32(1), (2), (5)).
  • Change of control. A person who acquires or increases a qualifying holding needs prior BNB approval (Ordinance 16 Art 70(6) sets the fee).

EMI license in Bulgaria FAQs

How much capital do you need for an EMI license in Bulgaria?

EUR 350,000 in eligible capital at the time of licensing (ZPUPS Art 38). Afterwards your own funds cover at least 2% of average outstanding e-money and never fall below the initial capital (Art 39; EMD2 Art 5).

How much does an EMI license cost in Bulgaria?

The BNB charges EUR 5,000 to examine the application (Ordinance 16 Art 70(2)). The larger items are the EUR 350,000 capital, a local entity, staff and advisers. our fee range for EMI licence in Bulgaria

How long does the BNB take to decide?

The law gives the BNB 3 months from receipt of the application. If the file is incomplete, it asks for the missing items, gives you up to 2 months to supply them, and then decides within 3 months of receiving them (ZPUPS Art 11). The time you spend preparing comes on top.

Can a foreign founder own a Bulgarian EMI?

Shareholders can be foreign. Each qualifying holder files documents, the origin of the capital must be lawful, and the company must be established in Bulgaria with actual management there (ZPUPS Art 10(4)).

Is there a small EMI licence in Bulgaria?

We found none in the ZPUPS text published by the BNB. EMD2 Art 9 makes the small EMI tier optional for Member States.

Can a Bulgarian EMI serve customers in other EU countries?

Yes. You notify the BNB and passport the licence under PSD2 Art 28, applied by EMD2 Art 3(1).

Do I need to submit documents in Bulgarian?

Yes. Ordinance 16 requires Bulgarian-language documents, with a Bulgarian translation for foreign-language ones and legalisation for formal documents (Art 3(3), applied to the EMI file by Art 23(2)).

Can a Bulgarian EMI also provide payment services?

An EMI can provide payment services as an additional activity if it notifies the BNB with the application (ZPUPS Art 37(4)). A payment institution licence you already hold is cancelled when the EMI licence is granted (Art 37(3)).
