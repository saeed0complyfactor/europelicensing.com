Payment Institution License in Bulgaria

A payment institution (PI) licence from the Bulgarian National Bank (BNB) lets a Bulgarian company provide payment services such as money remittance, acquiring, payment initiation or account services, and passport them across the EU and EEA. The BNB grants it under the Law on Payment Services and Payment Systems. Your capital need depends on the services you list, from EUR 20,000 up to EUR 125,000, and the BNB charges EUR 4,000 to examine the file.

Who needs a payment institution license in Bulgaria?

You need a licence before you provide any payment service in Art 4 items 1 to 7 of the ZPUPS as a business (Art 7(1)). A licence covers only the services listed in it (Art 7(2)). Typical cases are money remittance, merchant acquiring, issuing payment instruments, card and wallet processing, and payment initiation.

Other routes can fit better.

  • Account information only. You register with the BNB instead of getting a licence. You hold professional indemnity insurance and pay EUR 2,500 (ZPUPS Art 18; Ordinance 16 Art 70(4)).
  • You also hold e-money. An EMI licence in Bulgaria covers payment services too.
  • You are already authorised in another EEA state. Use the passport rather than a Bulgarian licence.
  • Your activity is a limited network or a similar exclusion. If your payment transactions in the preceding 12 months exceed EUR 1 million, you must notify the BNB and ask for entry in the register (ZPUPS Art 2(3)).
  • You are small. The ZPUPS text contains no small payment institution tier, although PSD2 Art 32 lets Member States offer one.

Payment institution license requirements in Bulgaria

Capital

The ZPUPS sets the initial capital by service group (Art 8). It must be in place when you obtain the licence.

Services Initial capital Basis
Money remittance only (Art 4 item 6) EUR 20,000 ZPUPS Art 8(1)
Payment initiation only (Art 4 item 7) EUR 50,000 ZPUPS Art 8(2)
Any of Art 4 items 1 to 5 EUR 125,000 ZPUPS Art 8(3)

Own funds must stay at or above the higher of the initial capital and the amount from the PSD2 Art 9 method the BNB applies (ZPUPS Art 9). A payment initiation provider also holds professional indemnity insurance or a comparable guarantee (ZPUPS Art 10).

Legal form and substance

The applicant is, or is being set up as, a limited liability company or a joint-stock company. Its registered office and head office in the Commercial Register match the place where management actually sits. It carries out at least part of its payment activity in Bulgaria (ZPUPS Art 10(4)).

Fit and proper and owners

The BNB checks managers and persons with qualifying holdings through the personal documents in Ordinance 16 Arts 4 to 7. The origin of the capital must be transparent and lawful (ZPUPS Art 10(4)(3)).

Safeguarding

Funds received from users for payment transactions go into a safeguarding account with a bank licensed in a Member State or with a central bank, if they have not reached the payee or another provider by the end of the next working day (ZPUPS Art 23(2), for services 1 to 6). This follows PSD2 Art 10.

Governance, ICT and AML

The file describes governance, internal controls, a security-incident procedure and ICT arrangements under DORA, Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, as required by PSD2 Art 5(1) and ZPUPS Art 10(4)(6). Your AML procedures are tested as part of the file. See our AML and compliance programme.

How to apply for a payment institution license in Bulgaria

  1. List the payment services you will provide. The licence covers only those, and the list decides your capital.
  2. Set up the Bulgarian company, or begin incorporation, and pay in the capital.
  3. Prepare the file in Ordinance 16 Art 3: programme of operations, business plan, governance, safeguarding, ICT, AML, and the personal documents for managers and qualifying holders. The BNB posts its licensing documents on its payment systems pages at bnb.bg.
  4. Pay the fee of EUR 4,000 to the BNB account shown on its registers page and attach the proof (Ordinance 16 Art 70(3)).
  5. File the application in writing, signed by the people who manage and represent the applicant. Documents are in Bulgarian. Foreign-language documents go in the original with a Bulgarian translation, and formal documents are legalised. Copies go in on paper and on an electronic device (Ordinance 16 Art 3(2) to (5)).
  6. The 3-month clock runs from receipt. If something is missing, the BNB asks for it, with up to 2 months to reply, and then decides within 3 months of receiving it (ZPUPS Art 11).
  7. Start business within 12 months of the licence or risk withdrawal (ZPUPS Art 16(1)(1)).

A refused applicant can apply again after 6 months (ZPUPS Art 15(2)). Agents are registered separately, and the BNB decides within 2 months (ZPUPS Art 28(4)).

Other cost lines, with no amounts here: the Bulgarian entity and registered office, local management, certified translations, audit, the safeguarding account, professional indemnity insurance for payment initiation and account information, AML and ICT systems, and advisers. After the BNB grants the license

  • The BNB lists you in its public register of payment institutions, with your branches and agents (ZPUPS Art 19). Its payment institution file showed an update dated 18 November 2026 when we checked.
  • You file financial statements and own-funds reports and notify the BNB of changes (ZPUPS Arts 24 and 25).
  • Passport. You notify the BNB of the Member State, services and whether you act through a branch, an agent or directly (ZPUPS Art 32). The home regulator forwards the notice within 1 month and the host assesses it within 1 month (PSD2 Art 28(2)).
  • Change of control. Acquiring or raising a qualifying holding needs prior BNB approval (EUR 1,000 review fee).

Bulgaria compared with other countries

If you will hold customer balances, the EMI licence in Bulgaria fits better. Crypto services sit with the FSC, so see the MiCA crypto licence in Bulgaria. For the same licence elsewhere, compare the payment institution licence in Lithuania, Ireland and Malta. The EU baseline is on the payment institution licence page.

Payment institution license in Bulgaria FAQs

How much capital do you need for a payment institution in Bulgaria?

EUR 20,000 for money remittance only, EUR 50,000 for payment initiation only, and EUR 125,000 if you provide any of the services in Art 4 items 1 to 5 (ZPUPS Art 8).

How much does a payment institution license cost in Bulgaria?

The BNB charges EUR 4,000 to examine the application (Ordinance 16 Art 70(3)). The rest is capital, a local entity, staff and advisers. our fee range for payment institution licence in Bulgaria

How long does the BNB take to decide on a payment institution?

The BNB has 3 months from receipt of the application. If it is incomplete, the BNB asks for the missing items with up to 2 months to reply and then decides within 3 months of receiving them (ZPUPS Art 11).

Is account information a licence or a registration in Bulgaria?

A registration. You apply to the BNB under ZPUPS Art 18, hold professional indemnity insurance, and pay EUR 2,500.

Is there a small payment institution in Bulgaria?

We found no small payment institution tier in the ZPUPS text published by the BNB. PSD2 Art 32 makes it optional for Member States.

Can a payment institution licensed in Bulgaria serve other EU countries?

Yes. You notify the BNB and passport the services you are licensed for (ZPUPS Art 32; PSD2 Art 28).

Does the application have to be in Bulgarian?

Yes. Ordinance 16 Art 3(3) requires Bulgarian-language documents, with translations of foreign ones and legalisation of formal documents.

Can a foreign-owned company get a Bulgarian payment institution licence?

Yes, if the company is established in Bulgaria with its management there, and the owners and capital source pass the BNB's checks (ZPUPS Art 10(4)).
