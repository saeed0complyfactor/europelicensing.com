EMI License in Liechtenstein

A Liechtenstein EMI licence lets you issue electronic money and provide payment services from Liechtenstein, an EEA member, and passport them across the EEA. The Financial Market Authority (FMA Liechtenstein) grants it under the E-Money Act (EGG). The application and business plan have to be filed in German.

Who needs an EMI license in Liechtenstein?

You need a licence to issue e-money in Liechtenstein as a business (EGG Art 4). If your product holds a balance for later spending, it is e-money. If it only moves money, the payment institution licence in Liechtenstein may be enough.

Other routes can fit:

  • A small e-money institution under EGG Art 30 is exempt from most licensing rules if it has its seat in Liechtenstein, operates only there, keeps average outstanding e-money to CHF 1,000,000 or less, has managers with clean records, and limits stored value to CHF 100 per instrument. It notifies the FMA before starting and cannot passport.
  • An EMI licensed in another EEA state can serve Liechtenstein under the EEA passport.
  • A Liechtenstein EMI that wants to custody or transfer its own e-money tokens notifies under MiCA Art 60(4). See the crypto licence in Liechtenstein.

Liechtenstein EMI license requirements

Capital and own funds

Initial capital is at least EUR 350,000 or the Swiss franc equivalent, paid in and freely available at authorisation. It can include share premium, reserves and retained earnings, and the FMA may require more in justified cases (FMA guidance, section 2.6; EGG Art 8). Initial capital is also the floor for own funds. For e-money issuance own funds are at least 2% of average outstanding e-money (EMD2 Art 5(3)).

Management and fit and proper

The FMA guidance requires a stock company (AG) or a European company (SE). Management must have at least two members with a combined workload of at least 200%, working under shared responsibility and not sitting on the board. The board must have at least three members (EGG Art 7(1)(i), per the guidance). Qualifying shareholders must meet the standard in EGG Art 9.

Substance

Seat and head office must be in Liechtenstein (EGG Art 7(1)(b)). The management workload rule above is the practical test of local substance.

AML officer

Plan for a named AML compliance officer and a business-wide risk assessment. See our AML and compliance programme.

Safeguarding

Customer funds are safeguarded under EGG Art 11 by reference to the ZDG methods: segregation in a separate account at a bank or another EEA credit institution, or a covering insurance or guarantee. The file has to show how you meet this.

ICT and DORA

The application follows EBA guidelines on authorisation (EBA/GL/2017/09) and PSD2 Art 5, including ICT arrangements under Regulation (EU) 2022/2554.

How to get an EMI license in Liechtenstein

  1. Set up a Liechtenstein AG or SE with the capital.
  2. Optionally send the FMA an informal draft of the application without original documents. The guidance calls this an informal pre-application.
  3. Build the file from the FMA guidance and its checklist: business model, three-year plan, capital evidence, governance, safeguarding, ICT, AML and qualifying holders.
  4. File in German or with certified translations. The application and business plan have to be in German. Other documents can be in English after you agree it with the FMA.
  5. Submit a qualified electronically signed application with the numbered checklist, or a hand-signed original by post to the FMA in Vaduz. Fit-and-proper forms go through the FMA e-service portal.
  6. The FMA examines the file formally and materially, and may order corrections.
  7. It decides within three months of a complete file. It rejects a file that is still incomplete twelve months after receipt (EGG Art 7(3)).

The time you spend preparing the file does not count toward these limits.

The authorisation fee covers the decision only. The annual levy comes from FMA Act Annex 2, as amended by LGBl. 2025 No. 94; check the consolidated FMA Act for any other surcharge that applies to you.

Other costs, for which we give no amounts here, include company formation, at least two managers and three board members, audit, a safeguarding account, German-language documents and certified translations, compliance tooling and advisers.

After you get the EMI license

  • The FMA enters you in its register of e-money institutions.
  • You report to the FMA and pay its supervision fees.
  • To serve other EEA states you notify the FMA. The passport steps are one month, one month and three months (PSD2 Art 28).
  • Material changes need notice. A change in qualifying holders needs the FMA's assessment.
  • A small e-money institution that outgrows the CHF 1,000,000 limit needs a full licence.

Liechtenstein EMI license vs other countries

Liechtenstein sits outside the EU but inside the EEA, so the passport reaches the same markets. Its file is in German and its management and board rules are written down. Compare the EMI licence in Lithuania, Malta and Ireland. In Liechtenstein, the sibling pages are the payment institution licence and the crypto licence. The EU-wide rules sit in our EMI licence guide.

EMI license in Liechtenstein FAQs

How much does an EMI license cost in Liechtenstein?

The FMA fee for granting or refusing the authorisation is CHF 30,000 (FMA Act, Annex 1). Capital is EUR 350,000 or the Swiss franc equivalent. The annual supervision levy starts at a CHF 20,000 base. Running costs come on top.

What are the Liechtenstein EMI license requirements?

An AG or SE with seat and head office in Liechtenstein, EUR 350,000 capital, management of at least two people with a combined 200% workload, a board of at least three, suitable shareholders, safeguarding, AML controls and ICT arrangements.

How long does an EMI license take in Liechtenstein?

The FMA decides within three months of a complete file and rejects a file still incomplete after twelve months (EGG Art 7(3)). Preparation sits outside that clock.

Can I apply in English?

Not fully. The application and business plan have to be in German. Other documents can be in English after you agree it with the FMA.

Is Liechtenstein in the EU, and does the passport work?

Liechtenstein is an EEA member, not an EU member. The EGG implements Directive 2009/110/EC, and a licensed EMI passports across the EEA by notification.

Is there a small EMI option in Liechtenstein?

Yes, under EGG Art 30, but only for firms operating in Liechtenstein alone, with average outstanding e-money up to CHF 1,000,000 and CHF 100 per instrument.

Can I run the EMI from abroad?

No. Seat and head office have to be in Liechtenstein, and the management must carry the combined 200% workload the guidance sets.

Do I need a separate crypto licence for e-money tokens?

Only EMIs and credit institutions may issue e-money tokens (MiCA Art 48(1)). Custody or transfer of your own tokens needs an Art 60 notification.
