Payment Institution License in Liechtenstein

A Liechtenstein payment institution licence lets you provide payment services from Liechtenstein, an EEA member, and passport them across the EEA. The Financial Market Authority (FMA Liechtenstein) grants it under the Payment Services Act (ZDG). The application and business plan must be in German.

Who needs a payment institution license in Liechtenstein?

You need a licence if you provide payment services in Liechtenstein as a business, such as accounts, transfers, card acquiring, remittance or payment initiation. If your product holds a balance for later spending, that is e-money and needs the EMI licence in Liechtenstein.

Other routes can fit:

  • Account information only is a registration, not a licence. The FMA fee for granting or refusing a registration is CHF 15,000, and you still need professional indemnity insurance (PSD2 Art 5(3), Art 33).
  • A payment institution licensed in another EEA state can serve Liechtenstein through a branch, an agent or cross-border services under the EEA passport.
  • The ZDG excludes some limited-network services from its scope (ZDG Art 3(1)(g)). Above CHF 1 million in twelve months you notify the FMA, which decides whether the exclusion applies. We found no small payment institution tier like the one in PSD2 Art 32.
  • If you also handle crypto-assets, see the crypto licence in Liechtenstein.

Liechtenstein payment institution license requirements

Capital by service

Services Initial capital Source
Money remittance only CHF 20,000 or the euro equivalent ZDG Art 10
Payment initiation only CHF 50,000 or the euro equivalent ZDG Art 10
Other payment services CHF 125,000 or the euro equivalent ZDG Art 10

PSD2 states these amounts in euro (PSD2 Art 7). The FMA guidance states them in Swiss francs or the euro equivalent, so agree the currency with the FMA. The FMA may order a higher amount in justified cases (ZDG Art 10(4)). Own funds must never fall below initial capital or the Art 19 calculation, whichever is higher (ZDG Art 18).

Management and fit and proper

The FMA guidance requires a stock company (AG) or a European company (SE). Management must have at least two members with a combined workload of at least 200%, working under shared responsibility and not sitting on the board. The board must have at least three members (ZDG Art 9(1)(i), per the guidance). Qualifying shareholders must meet the ZDG standard.

Substance

Seat and head office must be in Liechtenstein (ZDG Art 9(1)(b)). The management workload rule is the practical test of local substance.

AML officer

Plan for a named AML compliance officer and a business-wide risk assessment. See our AML and compliance programme.

Safeguarding

For the payment services listed in ZDG Art 20 (Art 2(2) letters a to c and f to h), you safeguard customer funds by one of the methods in Art 20(1), which follow PSD2 Art 10(1): segregation, secure low-risk assets, or insurance or a guarantee. The file has to show which method you use.

ICT and DORA

The ZDG file includes ICT and security arrangements under Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 and the guidance covers outsourcing of ICT functions.

How to get a payment institution license in Liechtenstein

  1. Map your services to PSD2 Annex I and fix your capital band.
  2. Set up a Liechtenstein AG or SE with the capital.
  3. Optionally send the FMA an informal draft of the application without original documents.
  4. Build the file from the FMA guidance and its checklist (Annex 2): business model, three-year budget, capital evidence, governance, safeguarding, ICT, AML and qualifying holders.
  5. File in German or with certified translations. The application and business plan have to be in German.
  6. Submit a qualified electronically signed application with the checklist, or a hand-signed original by post to the FMA in Vaduz. Fit-and-proper forms go through the FMA e-service portal.
  7. The FMA examines the file formally and materially, and may order corrections.
  8. It decides within three months of a complete file and rejects a file still incomplete after twelve months (ZDG Art 9(4)).

The time you spend preparing the file does not count toward these limits.

After you get the payment institution license

  • The FMA enters you in its register of payment institutions.
  • You report to the FMA and pay its supervision fees.
  • To serve other EEA states you notify the FMA of branches, agents or cross-border services. The passport steps are one month, one month and three months (PSD2 Art 28).
  • Changes in qualifying holders, management or outsourcing need notice to the FMA.

Liechtenstein payment institution license vs other countries

Liechtenstein sits outside the EU but inside the EEA, so the passport reaches the same markets. Its capital is set in Swiss francs, its fee is a flat CHF 30,000, and its file is in German. Compare the payment institution licence in Lithuania, Malta and Cyprus. In Liechtenstein, the sibling pages are the EMI licence and the crypto licence. The EU-wide rules sit in our payment institution licence guide.

Payment institution license in Liechtenstein FAQs

How much does a payment institution license cost in Liechtenstein?

The FMA fee for granting or refusing the authorisation is CHF 30,000 (FMA Act, Annex 1). Capital is CHF 20,000, 50,000 or 125,000 by service, or the euro equivalent. The annual supervision levy starts at a CHF 20,000 base. Running costs come on top.

What are the Liechtenstein payment institution license requirements?

An AG or SE with seat and head office in Liechtenstein, capital for your services, management of at least two people with a combined 200% workload, a board of at least three, suitable shareholders, safeguarding, AML controls and ICT arrangements.

How long does a payment institution license take in Liechtenstein?

The FMA decides within three months of a complete file and rejects a file still incomplete after twelve months (ZDG Art 9(4)). Preparation sits outside that clock.

Can I apply in English?

Not fully. The application and business plan have to be in German. Other documents can be in English after you agree it with the FMA.

Is Liechtenstein in the EU, and does the passport work?

Liechtenstein is an EEA member, not an EU member. The ZDG implements Directive (EU) 2015/2366, and a licensed payment institution passports across the EEA by notification.

Is there a small payment institution option in Liechtenstein?

We found none in the ZDG text we read. Account information providers register instead of applying for a full licence.

Do I need a licence for account information services only?

You register with the FMA, pay the CHF 15,000 registration fee and hold professional indemnity insurance.

Can I be an agent of a payment institution licensed elsewhere?

A firm licensed in another EEA state can use agents in Liechtenstein under the passport, notified through its home regulator. The licence sits with that firm.
