Crypto License in Liechtenstein

A crypto licence in Liechtenstein is an authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider (CASP) under MiCA, granted by the FMA Liechtenstein. It is for exchanges, custodians, brokers and transfer services that want an EEA passport from Liechtenstein. The old TVTG registration no longer covers MiCA services: it expired for those activities on 2 July 2026.

Who needs a crypto license in Liechtenstein?

You need CASP authorisation to provide any of the ten MiCA crypto-asset services as a business: custody, trading platform, exchange for funds or other crypto-assets, order execution, placing, order reception and transmission, advice, portfolio management and transfer services (MiCA Art 3(1)(16)). The applicant needs a registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (Art 59(2)).

Other routes can fit:

  • If you already hold a bank or EMI licence, you may notify the FMA under MiCA Art 60 instead of applying. An EMI may notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues. The ESMA register shows six Liechtenstein notifications, mostly banks. See the EMI licence in Liechtenstein.
  • If you are authorised in another EEA state, you passport into Liechtenstein under Art 65 and need no Liechtenstein licence.
  • If you offer token services that fall outside MiCA, such as non-fungible tokens, the Token and TT Service Provider Act (TVTG, the Blockchain Act) may still apply. The FMA says some providers need authorisation under both regimes.

Liechtenstein crypto license requirements

Capital by class

Class Minimum capital Services
Class 1 EUR 50,000 Order reception and transmission, advice, execution, placing, transfer, portfolio management
Class 2 EUR 125,000 Class 1 plus custody and exchange services
Class 3 EUR 150,000 Class 2 plus operating a trading platform

You hold the higher of the class minimum and one quarter of the preceding year's fixed overheads, using projected overheads in your first year (MiCA Art 67(1), (2)). Safeguards can be CET1 own funds, insurance or both (Art 67(4)).

Management and fit and proper

Directors need good repute, knowledge, skills, experience and enough time. Qualifying shareholders need good repute (MiCA Art 68(1), (2)). The FMA may attach conditions and requirements to the authorisation (EWR-MiCA-DG Art 3).

Substance

MiCA requires effective management in the EU and an EU-resident director (Art 59(2)). The FMA page does not address local presence, so ask in your informal discussion.

AML officer

Art 68(8) requires AML/CFT controls. Plan for a named AML compliance officer and a business-wide risk assessment. See our AML and compliance programme.

Client assets and custody

Client funds go to a credit institution or central bank by the end of the next business day, in separate accounts (MiCA Art 70). Custody needs a client agreement, a custody policy, a position register and statements at least quarterly (Art 75).

ICT and DORA

Your ICT systems must meet Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, with continuity and recovery plans (MiCA Art 68(7)).

How to get a crypto license in Liechtenstein

  1. Map your services to the ten crypto-asset services to fix your capital class.
  2. Ask for an informal preliminary discussion with the FMA Fintech Unit at casp@fma-li.li. The FMA then provides the documents you need for submission. It has offered these discussions since June 2024.
  3. Set up a legal entity with the capital and an EU-resident director.
  4. Build the MiCA Art 62(2) file: programme of operations, governance, fit and proper evidence, AML/CFT, ICT, custody and complaints policies.
  5. Submit your application. The FMA says the MiCAR deadlines are very strict and leave little room to build an application step by step, so it treats a complete, high-quality file as essential.
  6. The FMA acknowledges receipt within 5 working days and checks completeness within 25 working days (MiCA Art 63(1), (2)).
  7. It decides within 40 working days of a complete application. Questions raised by the 20th working day pause the clock for up to 20 working days (Art 63(9), (12)).
  8. Within 2 working days of a grant, ESMA is informed for its register (Art 63(13)).

The time you spend preparing the file does not count toward these limits.

After you get the crypto license

  • The FMA enters you in its register, and ESMA lists you in its MiCA register.
  • You meet the ongoing MiCA rules on capital, governance, custody, complaints and conflicts of interest, and pay the FMA's supervision fees.
  • To serve other EEA states, you notify the FMA, which forwards the notice within 10 working days. You may start on receipt or on the 15th calendar day at the latest (MiCA Art 65).
  • Adding services needs an extension of authorisation (Art 59(8)), which carries the fee shown above.
  • A change in qualifying holders needs regulator approval.

Is the Liechtenstein crypto transition over?

Yes. The MiCAR transition period under Art 143(3) ended on 1 July 2026, and TVTG registrations for activities that need MiCAR authorisation expired on 2 July 2026, according to the FMA's announcement of 7 July 2026. The FMA is updating its TVTG register entries to match.

MiCA reached Liechtenstein in two steps. The FMA says the EWR-MiCA-DG has been in force since 1 February 2025. The EEA Joint Committee adopted Decision 041/2025 on 20 February 2025, and the EFTA Secretariat lists its entry into force in the EEA as 24 June 2025.

Liechtenstein crypto license vs other countries

Liechtenstein has six full CASP authorisations and a published fee schedule that depends on the services you offer. See the MiCA licence in Lithuania, Malta and Cyprus. In Liechtenstein, the sibling pages are the EMI licence and the payment institution licence. The EU-wide rules sit in our MiCA CASP licence guide.

Crypto license in Liechtenstein FAQs

How much does a crypto license cost in Liechtenstein?

The FMA fee is CHF 30,000 for an authorisation that includes custody, a trading platform or exchange services, and CHF 10,000 for the other services only, plus any expert costs (FMA Act, Annex 1). Capital is EUR 50,000, 125,000 or 150,000 by class, or a quarter of fixed overheads if higher.

What are the Liechtenstein crypto license requirements?

A registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU, an EU-resident director, minimum capital for your class, fit and proper directors and shareholders, AML/CFT controls, DORA-compliant ICT, and custody and complaints policies (MiCA Art 59, 62, 67, 68).

How long does a MiCA license take in Liechtenstein?

The FMA has 25 working days to check completeness and 40 working days to decide once the file is complete (MiCA Art 63). Preparation and answering questions sit outside those limits.

Does MiCA apply in Liechtenstein if it is not in the EU?

Yes. MiCA is part of the EEA Agreement, and Liechtenstein applies it through the EWR-MiCA-DG. An authorisation from the FMA passports across the EEA.

Does the TVTG still matter after MiCA?

For services covered by MiCA, TVTG registrations expired on 2 July 2026. The FMA says the two regimes are meant to be mutually exclusive, but items outside MiCA, such as NFTs, may still fall under the TVTG.

Has the FMA authorised any crypto firms?

Yes. The ESMA register of 23 November 2026 lists six Art 63 authorisations and six Art 60 notifications for Liechtenstein, the first authorisation dated 1 June 2026.

Can I apply in English?

The FMA page does not state a filing language. Other FMA licensing guidance asks for German for the main application documents, so raise language in your informal discussion.

Do I need a licence to serve Liechtenstein clients from another country?

A CASP authorised in another EEA state passports into Liechtenstein under MiCA Art 65. A firm from outside the EEA cannot rely on the passport.
