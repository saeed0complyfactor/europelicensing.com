Crypto License in Romania

Romania has no confirmed MiCA authorisation route for crypto-asset service providers (CASPs) at our last check on 23 November 2026. ESMA's list of competent authorities shows Romania as "TBA", and the press quoted the Financial Supervisory Authority (ASF) in July 2026 as saying it was not designated. If you want a MiCA licence now, authorise in another member state and passport into Romania.

Who needs a MiCA licence in Romania?

You need CASP authorisation if you provide crypto-asset services to clients as a business: custody and administration, operating a trading platform, exchange, order execution, placing, order reception and transmission, advice, portfolio management or transfer services (MiCA Art 3(1)(16)). Since 1 July 2026 no transitional period covers an old national arrangement (Art 143(3)).

We found no Romanian route at our last check, so look at these options:

  • You want an EU-wide licence. Apply in a member state with a working authority and passport into Romania (Art 59(7) and Art 65). The application goes to the authority of your home member state, where your registered office is (Art 62).
  • You are a bank. MiCA Art 60 lets you notify your home regulator instead of applying, but a notification also needs a designated authority to receive it. An EMI may notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues (Art 60(4)), and a payment institution has no notification route and must apply in full. Ask BNR and ASF who receives a notification.
  • You already hold a MiCA authorisation elsewhere. Use the passport instead of waiting for Romania.
  • You are a non-EU firm. ESMA's statement of 23 June 2026 says you cannot serve or solicit EU clients outside reverse solicitation.

Requirements for a MiCA licence

These are the EU rules that apply wherever you authorise. Romania has published no national procedure that we could find.

Capital and prudential safeguards

Class Minimum capital Services covered
Class 1 EUR 50,000 Order reception and transmission, advice, execution, placing, transfer services, portfolio management
Class 2 EUR 125,000 Class 1 plus custody and administration, and exchange for funds or other crypto-assets
Class 3 EUR 150,000 Class 2 plus operating a trading platform

You hold the higher of that amount or one quarter of the previous year's fixed overheads (Art 67(1)).

Substance and governance

You need a registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (Art 59(2)). Directors need good repute, knowledge, skills and time, and qualifying shareholders need good repute (Art 68).

ICT and AML

Your ICT must meet DORA, Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (Art 68(7)). You also need AML/CFT controls (Art 68(8)). See our AML and compliance programme page.

How to get a MiCA licence for Romania

Because we found no Romanian procedure, the process below applies in the member state you choose.

  1. Map your product to the crypto-asset services and set your capital class.
  2. Choose a home member state with a designated authority and a published procedure.
  3. Set up a company with its registered office there, with effective management in the EU.
  4. File under MiCA Art 62. The authority acknowledges within 5 working days, checks completeness within 25 working days and decides within 40 working days of a complete application (Art 63).
  5. Notify the passport (Art 65) to serve customers in Romania.
  6. If you want a Romanian authorisation, ask ASF and BNR whether a procedure exists, and record the answer with a date.

How much does a MiCA licence cost?

Regulator fees

We found no Romanian application fee because we found no Romanian procedure. Fees come from the authority you apply to in another member state.

Capital

EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000 by class, or the overheads figure if higher (MiCA Annex IV and Art 67).

Other cost lines

No amounts here, because they depend on your model and the member state:

  • Company and registered office in the chosen member state
  • At least one EU-resident director and staff
  • Office space
  • Statutory audit
  • Insurance policy or own funds for prudential safeguards
  • Custody, segregation and trading technology
  • ICT, DORA and AML tools
  • Legal and policy drafting

After authorisation

  • Register: authorised CASPs appear in the ESMA register, which ESMA publishes as csv files updated weekly. The file we downloaded on 23 November 2026 has no entry with Romania as home member state.
  • Passport: the home authority forwards your notification within 10 working days, and you can start on that communication or on the 15th calendar day at the latest (MiCA Art 65).
  • Records: keep records for five years, up to seven if the regulator asks (Art 68(9)).

Status of the Romanian regime on 23 November 2026

What an official source confirms:

  • ESMA's list of competent authorities, last updated 17 February 2026, shows Romania with an asterisk and "TBA" in every column. The list says authorities for asterisked states have not yet been formally designated.
  • ESMA's file of grandfathering periods shows Romania at 18 months, with a note that some periods were communicated as expectations. MiCA capped every period at 1 July 2026 (Art 143(3)).
  • The ESMA register file we downloaded on 23 November 2026 lists no CASP with Romania as home member state.

What ASF and the press report, with dates:

  • On 1 July 2026 Ziarul Bursa quoted an ASF first vice-president saying that no authority in Romania is designated for applying MiCA. On 5 July 2026 StartupCafe quoted ASF saying it was not designated and had no applications.
  • On 2 July 2026 Business24 reported a draft emergency ordinance designating ASF, presented in first reading in the Government on 2 April 2026. It gave no adoption date.
  • OUG 10/2025, published in Monitorul Oficial 225 on 13 March 2025, amended the AML law (Law 129/2019). Some sources say it made ASF the main regulator for CASPs. A law firm note of 21 August 2026 says it does not designate ASF for authorisation.
  • ASF's Financial Innovation Strategy 2026 to 2028 lists MiCA and CASP authorisation as a strategic pillar. It does not cite a designating act.

We found no Monitorul Oficial act that designates ASF. ASF's own press release list, checked on 23 November 2026, has no notice on designation or on the July statements, and its only MiCA item relays ESMA's statement on the end of the transitional period. The sources disagree on OUG 10/2025, so we state only what ESMA's list and ASF's own statements support. Ask ASF in writing whether it is designated and whether it accepts applications, and record the date of the reply.

Romania vs other countries

Romania has no confirmed MiCA route, so firms look elsewhere. Compare the MiCA licence in Cyprus, the MiCA licence in Malta and the MiCA licence in Lithuania. In Romania, the EMI licence and the payment institution licence both come from BNR and are available.

Crypto license in Romania FAQs

Can I get a MiCA licence in Romania?

We could not confirm a working route. ESMA's list of competent authorities (17 February 2026) shows Romania as "TBA", and the press quoted ASF in July 2026 as saying it was not designated. Ask ASF whether that has changed.

Which authority would grant it?

ASF is the authority press sources expect, and a draft ordinance would designate it. No confirmed act does so in what we found. Check with ASF.

Does OUG 10/2025 designate ASF?

Sources disagree. The ordinance amended the AML law, and a law firm note of 21 August 2026 says it does not designate ASF for CASP authorisation. The press quoted ASF in July 2026 as saying it was not designated.

Can a Romanian firm get a MiCA licence somewhere else?

Yes. Apply in a member state with a working authority, with your registered office there, and passport into Romania (Art 59(7), 62 and 65).

Has the Romanian transitional period ended?

Yes. No transitional period could run past 1 July 2026 (Art 143(3)). ESMA's statement of 23 June 2026 says unauthorised CASPs must stop onboarding EU clients and wind down.

How much capital do you need?

EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000 by class, or one quarter of fixed overheads if higher (MiCA Annex IV, Art 67).

How long would authorisation take?

MiCA sets 25 working days for completeness and 40 working days to decide from a complete application (Art 63), once an authority can receive it. We do not quote a timeline.

Where can I check the status?

Ask ASF, and check ESMA's list of competent authorities and the ESMA register. Date every answer you rely on.
