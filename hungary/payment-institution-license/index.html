Payment Institution License in Hungary

A payment institution (PI, or pénzforgalmi intézmény) licence from the Magyar Nemzeti Bank (MNB) lets a Hungarian company run payment accounts, execute transfers, acquire cards, send remittances or initiate payments, and passport those services across the EU and EEA. Hungarian law sets capital in forint: HUF 37.5 million for the full set of services, with lower tiers for cash transfers only and payment initiation only. The MNB fee is HUF 2.7 million for procedures started on or after 1 August 2026.

Who needs a payment institution license in Hungary?

You need an MNB activity licence before you provide payment services in Hungary as a business: payment accounts, execution of transfers and direct debits, card issuing or acquiring, money remittance or payment initiation (PSD2 Annex I). The MNB guide lists these as the payment services in the Credit Institutions Act.

Other routes can fit better.

  • You only provide account information. The MNB runs no licensing procedure for it. You register, with a HUF 2,000,000 fee under the 2026 decree and no minimum capital.
  • You hold stored value. An EMI licence in Hungary covers e-money and payment services together.
  • You are already authorised in another EEA state. Passport into Hungary under PSD2 Art 28.
  • You are small. The MNB guide describes no volume-based exemption, only lower capital for narrow services. PSD2 Art 32 lets Member States set one, so check with the MNB whether Hungary uses it.

Payment institution license requirements in Hungary

Capital and own funds

Services Initial capital Basis
Payment services generally HUF 37,500,000 Act CCXXXV s 11(1)
Payment initiation only HUF 15,000,000 s 11(3)
Cash transfers only HUF 6,000,000 s 11(2)
Account information only None MNB Q&A

Capital is paid in cash only and held in a credit institution that is not part of the foundation and has no ownership link with the founder, until operations begin (s 11(6)). You prove the full amount with the application. Own funds then follow PSD2 Arts 8 and 9.

Substance and legal form

A PI is a joint stock company, a limited liability company, a cooperative or a branch of an EEA payment institution (s 10(1)). You declare that governance takes place at the principal office in Hungary (s 17(4)).

Management and owners

Qualifying owners must be independent of influences that harm prudent operation, with good reputation and a transparent structure. Owners with a qualifying holding supply identification data, proof of the legal origin of funds and clean records. Senior executives supply the documents the guide lists under s 29.

Safeguarding

You submit contracts with an EEA credit institution for the custody account that holds funds received for payment transactions, or describe their placement in safe, low-risk instruments, together with an IT description and auditor confirmation for the client fund register (PSD2 Art 10(1); MNB guide).

AML, governance and ICT

The file includes AML/CFT policies, internal control, business continuity, security-incident and sensitive-data procedures. Payment initiation and account information need professional liability insurance or a comparable guarantee (s 13/A). Institutions that keep payment accounts need an IT security certificate from an external expert (s 12/A). See our AML and compliance programme.

How to apply for a payment institution license in Hungary

  1. Map your services to the PSD2 Annex I list. That sets your capital tier.
  2. Ask the MNB for a personal consultation before you file, if you want one. The statutory deadline keeps running even if you consult first.
  3. Register for the ERA e-administration service. Legal entities may file only through ERA, using the form "Application for licensing the activity of payment institutions". Account-information-only firms use a separate registration form.
  4. Pay the HUF 2,700,000 fee when the procedure starts. The MNB examines the application on the merits only after payment.
  5. Upload the annexes the MNB guide lists under s 17: programme of operations, three-year business plan, proof of capital, safeguarding, governance, AML policies and owner documents.
  6. Non-resident applicants appoint an agent for service of process and attach official Hungarian translations of foreign-language documents.
  7. The administration deadline is 3 months. If the MNB asks you to complete the file, it counts from the date you submit everything, and it can be extended once by up to 3 months.

Payment institution license cost in Hungary

The MNB charges an administrative service fee of HUF 2,700,000 for a payment institution activity licence under Decree 23/2026 (VI.30.), in force from 1 August 2026 for procedures started after that date. Registering an account-information-only firm costs HUF 2,000,000. The fee is payable when the procedure starts, and the MNB does not refund it if the application is refused or you withdraw. The MNB guide still states HUF 1,900,000 and its Q&A states HUF 1,100,000, both from before the new decree.

After the MNB grants the license

  • Register. Confirm with the MNB how and when your entry appears on its public register.
  • Reporting and supervision. The MNB supervises you under the Payment Service Providers Act. We did not find an annual supervision fee in the guide, so check the MNB fee notice.
  • Passport. You notify the MNB of the states, services, agents and branches. It forwards the notice within 1 month and the host assesses it within 1 month (PSD2 Art 28(2)).
  • Change of control. Changes in qualifying holdings go to the MNB. Confirm the route and fee before any share transfer.

Payment institution license in Hungary FAQs

How much does a payment institution license cost in Hungary?

The MNB charges HUF 2,700,000 for the activity licence procedure (Decree 23/2026 s 3(1)), and you need HUF 37.5 million, 15 million or 6 million of capital by service. Our fee starts at EUR 60,000.

How much capital do you need for a payment institution in Hungary?

HUF 37,500,000 for payment services generally, HUF 15,000,000 for payment initiation only and HUF 6,000,000 for cash transfers only. Account information needs none (MNB guide; Act CCXXXV s 11).

How long does the MNB take to decide?

The administration deadline is 3 months, counted from the full submission if the MNB asks you to complete the file, and extendable once by up to 3 months in justified cases.

How do I get a payment institution license in Hungary?

You set up a Hungarian company, pay the fee and file the application and annexes through ERA, then answer the MNB's questions until the decision.

Do I need an office in Hungary?

You declare that governance takes place at a principal office in Hungary (Act CCXXXV s 17(4)), so a Hungarian authorisation means a Hungarian company with its head office there, or a branch of an EEA institution.

Does the application have to be in Hungarian?

The MNB Q&A requires official Hungarian translations of foreign-language documents from non-resident applicants. Confirm with the MNB which documents it accepts in English.

Do I need a licence for account information services only?

Registration with the MNB is enough, with a HUF 2,000,000 fee and no minimum capital. Professional liability insurance is required (Act CCXXXV s 13/A).

Can a Hungarian payment institution serve customers in other EU countries?

Yes, by notification under PSD2 Art 28.
