EMI License in Hungary

An electronic money institution (EMI, or elektronikuspénz-kibocsátó intézmény) licence from the Magyar Nemzeti Bank (MNB) lets a Hungarian company issue e-money and provide payment services, and passport both across the EU and EEA. The national capital figure is HUF 100 million, and the MNB's fee for the procedure is HUF 2.7 million for procedures started on or after 1 August 2026. The MNB's older guides still show lower fees, so use the 2026 decree.

Who needs an EMI license in Hungary?

You need one if you issue electronic money in Hungary as a business, for example prepaid cards, wallets with a stored balance or accounts holding funds redeemable at par. The MNB says an EMI may be licensed for all payment services, so you do not need a separate payment institution licence for them. A Hungarian EMI can also be a branch of an EMI with its registered office in another EEA state (Act CCXXXV s 10).

Other routes can fit better.

  • You only transmit money and hold no stored value. The payment institution licence in Hungary has lower capital.
  • You are already an EMI in another EEA state. Passport into Hungary under PSD2 Art 28.
  • You only provide account information. That is a registration with the MNB, with its own fee.
  • You want crypto services as well. See the crypto licence in Hungary.

EMI license requirements in Hungary

Capital and own funds

The minimum start-up capital is HUF 100 million for e-money issuance (Act CCXXXV s 11(4)). It is paid in cash only and held in a credit institution that is not part of the foundation and has no ownership link with the founder, until operations begin (s 11(6)). You prove the full amount with the application. Own funds then follow EMD2 Art 5(3), at least 2% of average e-money outstanding.

Substance and legal form

The EMI is a joint stock company, a limited liability company, a cooperative or a branch of an EEA EMI (s 10(1)). You declare that governance takes place at the principal office in Hungary (s 17(4)). The MNB guide does not require a foundation licence, only the activity licence.

Management and owners

Qualifying owners must be independent of influences that harm prudent operation, have a good business reputation and have transparent structures (s 13). The file includes identification data for senior executives and proof they meet the requirements of s 29. Owners with a qualifying holding, defined as 10% or more of capital or votes, supply their own documents, including the legal origin of funds.

Safeguarding

You submit contracts with an EEA credit institution for the custody account holding funds received for e-money, or describe their placement in safe, low-risk instruments (s 51; EMD2 Art 7). You also describe the IT system that keeps a separate register of client funds, with an auditor's confirmation of its suitability.

AML, governance and ICT

The file includes AML/CFT policies, internal control, business continuity, security-incident procedures and a risk assessment under the EBA authorisation guidelines. Institutions that keep payment accounts need an IT security certificate from an external expert (s 12/A). The auditor must have the financial institution qualification and no ownership link with the EMI (s 35). See our AML and compliance programme.

How to apply for an EMI license in Hungary

  1. Define the business model and the payment services you will add to e-money issuance.
  2. Ask the MNB for a personal consultation before you file, if you want one. Contact the Money and Capital Markets Licensing Department at ptef@mnb.hu. The statutory deadline keeps running even if you consult first.
  3. Register for the ERA e-administration service. Legal entities may file only through ERA, using the form "Application for licensing the activity of electronic money institutions".
  4. Pay the HUF 2,700,000 administrative service fee when the procedure starts. The MNB examines the application on the merits only after payment (MNB notice of 29 July 2026).
  5. Upload the annexes listed in s 17 of Act CCXXXV: programme of operations, three-year business plan, proof of capital, safeguarding, governance, AML policies, owner documents and the other items in the MNB guide.
  6. Non-resident applicants appoint an agent for service of process and attach official Hungarian translations of foreign-language documents (MNB Q&A).
  7. The administration deadline is 3 months. If the MNB asks you to complete the file, it counts from the date you submit everything. It can be extended once by up to 3 months in justified cases.

EMI license cost in Hungary

The MNB's administrative service fee for an EMI activity licence is HUF 2,700,000 under Decree 23/2026 (VI.30.), which entered into force on 1 August 2026 and applies to procedures started after that date. It is payable when the procedure starts. The MNB does not refund it if the application is refused, or if the procedure ends because you withdraw. The MNB EMI guide still states HUF 1,900,000 and its Q&A states HUF 1,100,000, but both predate the new decree.

The MNB's annual supervision fees are outside the guide we used, so check its fee notice. Our own fee: from EUR 60,000.

After the MNB grants the license

  • Register. Confirm with the MNB how and when your entry appears on its public register, because the guide we used does not describe it.
  • Reporting and supervision. The MNB supervises you as an EMI. The EMI guide sets rules for the auditor contract and reports, which go to the MNB (Act CCXXXV s 37).
  • Passport. You notify the MNB of the states and services. It forwards the notice within 1 month and the host assesses it within 1 month (PSD2 Art 28(2)). E-money distribution abroad goes through distributors, not agents (EMD2 Art 3(4), (5)).
  • Change of control. Changes in qualifying holdings go to the MNB. Confirm the route and fee with it before any share transfer, because the guide we used does not cover it in detail.

See passporting and post-licence support.

Hungary compared with other countries

If you only provide payments, see the payment institution licence in Hungary. For crypto services, see the crypto licence in Hungary. For the same licence elsewhere, compare the EMI licence in Lithuania, the Czech Republic and Slovakia. The EU baseline is on the EMI licence page.

EMI license in Hungary FAQs

How much does an EMI license cost in Hungary?

The MNB charges HUF 2,700,000 for the activity licence procedure (Decree 23/2026 s 3(1)), and you need HUF 100 million of start-up capital. Our fee starts at EUR 60,000.

How much capital do you need for an EMI license in Hungary?

HUF 100 million for e-money issuance, paid in cash (Act CCXXXV s 11(4), (6)). The EU baseline in EMD2 Art 4 is EUR 350,000.

How long does the MNB take to decide?

The administration deadline is 3 months. If the MNB asks you to complete the file, it counts from the full submission, and it can be extended once by up to 3 months in justified cases.

How do I get an EMI license in Hungary?

You set up a Hungarian company, pay the fee and file the application and annexes through ERA, then answer the MNB's questions until the decision.

Can a non-resident founder hold a Hungarian EMI?

Yes, but non-resident applicants must appoint an agent for service of process and attach official Hungarian translations of foreign-language documents. Governance must take place at a principal office in Hungary.

Does the application have to be in Hungarian?

The MNB Q&A requires official Hungarian translations of foreign-language documents from non-resident applicants. Confirm with the MNB which documents it accepts in English.

Can a Hungarian EMI serve customers in other EU countries?

Yes, by notification under PSD2 Art 28, applied by EMD2 Art 3(1).

Is there a small EMI licence in Hungary?

The MNB EMI guide describes none.
