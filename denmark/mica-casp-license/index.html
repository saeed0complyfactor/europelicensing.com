Crypto License in Denmark

A crypto licence in Denmark is an authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider (CASP) under MiCA, granted by Finanstilsynet, the Danish Financial Supervisory Authority. It is for exchanges, custodians, brokers, transfer services and other crypto firms that want an EU passport from Denmark. The MiCA transition has ended. ESMA's register lists seven Danish entries, and Finanstilsynet has refused at least one application.

Who needs a crypto license in Denmark?

You need CASP authorisation to provide any of the ten MiCA crypto-asset services as a business: custody, trading platform, exchange for funds or other crypto-assets, order execution, placing, order reception and transmission, advice, portfolio management and transfer services (MiCA Art 3(1)(16)). The applicant needs a registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (Art 59(2)).

Other routes can fit:

  • If you already hold a Danish EMI licence, you may notify Finanstilsynet instead of applying, at least 40 working days before you start (MiCA Art 60(4)). The notification covers only custody and transfer of the e-money tokens you issue. Art 60 gives payment institutions no notification route, so a payment institution applies for full authorisation. See the EMI licence in Denmark.
  • If you hold a bank, securities firm or other Art 60 licence, you may notify for the services your licence covers.
  • If you are authorised in another member state, you passport into Denmark under Art 65 and need no Danish licence.
  • If you handle e-money tokens in ways that count as payment services, you may also need payment institution authorisation. See the payment institution licence in Denmark.

Denmark crypto license requirements

Capital by class

Class Minimum capital Services
Class 1 EUR 50,000 Order reception and transmission, advice, execution, placing, transfer, portfolio management
Class 2 EUR 125,000 Class 1 plus custody and exchange services
Class 3 EUR 150,000 Class 2 plus operating a trading platform

You hold the higher of the class minimum and one quarter of the preceding year's fixed overheads, using projected overheads in your first year (MiCA Art 67(1), (2)). Safeguards can be CET1 own funds, insurance or both (Art 67(4)).

Management and fit and proper

Directors need good repute, knowledge, skills, experience and enough time. Qualifying shareholders need good repute (MiCA Art 68(1), (2)). The regulator must refuse if they fail the test (Art 63(10)).

Substance

MiCA requires a registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU and an EU-resident director (Art 59(2)). Finanstilsynet's pages state no separate Danish presence rule. Its refusal of Kittrow Finance ApS, as summarised in a search extract of the notice, turned on too little staff and management presence in Denmark. Plan for real local substance and agree it with the authority before you apply.

AML officer

Art 68(8) requires AML/CFT controls. Plan for a named AML compliance officer and a business-wide risk assessment. See our AML and compliance programme.

Client assets and custody

Client funds go to a credit institution or central bank by the end of the next business day, in separate accounts (MiCA Art 70). Custody needs a client agreement, a custody policy, a position register and statements at least quarterly (Art 75).

ICT and DORA

Your ICT systems must meet Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, with continuity and recovery plans (MiCA Art 68(7)).

How to get a crypto license in Denmark

  1. Map your services to the ten crypto-asset services to fix your capital class.
  2. Check the Finanstilsynet crypto page and contact its fintech office at fintech@ftnet.dk with questions.
  3. Set up a legal entity with the capital and an EU-resident director.
  4. Build the MiCA Art 62(2) file: programme of operations, governance, fit and proper evidence, AML/CFT, ICT, custody and complaints policies.
  5. Submit through the application or notification form on virk.dk.
  6. The authority acknowledges receipt within 5 working days and checks completeness within 25 working days (MiCA Art 63(1), (2)).
  7. It decides within 40 working days of a complete application. Questions raised by the 20th working day pause the clock for up to 20 working days (Art 63(9), (12)).
  8. Within 2 working days of a grant, ESMA is informed for its register (Art 63(13)).

These are statutory limits. Preparation sits outside them.

After you get the crypto license

  • Finanstilsynet records the authorisation, and ESMA lists you in its MiCA register.
  • You meet the ongoing MiCA rules on capital, governance, custody, complaints and conflicts of interest, and pay the annual fee, invoiced in early December.
  • To serve other EU states, you notify Finanstilsynet, which forwards the notice within 10 working days. You may start on receipt of that notice or on the 15th calendar day at the latest (MiCA Art 65).
  • Adding services later needs an extension of authorisation (Art 59(8)).
  • A change in qualifying holders needs regulator approval.

Is the Danish crypto transition over?

Yes for practical purposes. Finanstilsynet's page says firms that were already active and applied by 30 December 2024 could continue for up to 18 months unless the authority refused the application first. Eighteen months from that date is the end of June 2026. The page gives no fixed calendar date, so that end date is our own calculation. EU-wide, ESMA says the MiCA transition ended on 1 July 2026 (MiCA Art 143(3)), and unauthorised firms must stop onboarding EU clients and wind down.

Finanstilsynet has refused applications. On 21 August 2025 it refused the application of Kittrow Finance ApS, and on 10 November 2025 the Business Appeals Board ruled that the firm's appeal does not suspend the decision, so the firm cannot offer its services while the appeal runs. These facts come from a search extract of Finanstilsynet's notice, because the page returned an error when we fetched it.

Denmark crypto license vs other countries

Denmark has a short list of authorised firms and a regulator that has refused applications. See the MiCA licence in Lithuania, Malta and Ireland. In Denmark, the sibling pages are the EMI licence and the payment institution licence. The EU-wide rules sit in our MiCA CASP licence guide.

Crypto license in Denmark FAQs

How much does a crypto license cost in Denmark?

Capital of EUR 50,000, 125,000 or 150,000 by class, or a quarter of fixed overheads if higher (MiCA Art 67, Annex IV). Finanstilsynet's pages state no application fee, so we cannot quote one. The annual fee is 12.5 per mille of salary, commission and bonus costs with a minimum of DKK 20,000. Running costs come on top.

What are the Denmark crypto license requirements?

A registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU, an EU-resident director, minimum capital for your class, fit and proper directors and shareholders, AML/CFT controls, DORA-compliant ICT, custody and complaints policies (MiCA Art 59, 62, 67, 68).

How long does a MiCA license take in Denmark?

The authority decides within 40 working days of a complete application, after up to 25 working days to check completeness (MiCA Art 63). Requests for information can pause the clock for up to 20 working days. Preparation comes on top.

Do I need a physical presence in Denmark?

MiCA requires effective management in the EU and an EU-resident director. Finanstilsynet's pages state no further rule, but a search extract of its Kittrow Finance notice shows a refusal for too little staff and management presence in Denmark. Plan for local staff and agree the set-up with the authority before you file.

Is the Danish MiCA transition period over?

Yes. Finanstilsynet allowed up to 18 months from 30 December 2024 for firms that applied in time, and ESMA says the EU-wide transition ended on 1 July 2026. Unauthorised firms must wind down.

Which Danish firms hold a MiCA authorisation?

ESMA's register listed seven Danish entries on 23 November 2026, including Coinify ApS, Lunar Block A/S and Northstake ApS. See the status section for the full list.

Can I notify instead of applying?

Yes, if you are a Danish bank, central securities depository, securities firm, market operator, EMI, investment management company or AIFM, for the services your licence allows. You notify at least 40 working days before you start (MiCA Art 60). An EMI may notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues. A payment institution cannot notify.

Can I passport a Danish crypto licence?

Yes. An authorised CASP notifies Finanstilsynet and can serve any EU member state without a local presence (MiCA Art 59(7); Art 65).
