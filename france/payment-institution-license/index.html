Payment Institution License in France

A payment institution (établissement de paiement, EP) licence from the ACPR lets a French company provide payment services and passport them across the EU and EEA. The ACPR decides within 3 months of a complete file, after an opinion from the Banque de France on the security of payment means. France also has a simplified authorisation for firms up to an average of EUR 3 million a month, with no passport and no money transmission.

Who needs a payment institution license in France?

You need an agrément as an établissement de paiement if you provide payment services, as defined in CMF L. 314-1, as a business in France. Typical cases are money remittance, payment accounts, card acquiring, payment initiation and account information.

Some firms need a different route.

  • You only provide account information. That is a registration with the ACPR, not an authorisation, and it has its own annex (instruction 2019-I-22, annex 3). See PSD2 Art 33.
  • You are small. The simplified authorisation in CMF L. 522-11-1 applies if your expected average monthly payment volume stays at or below EUR 3 million and you plan no money transmission service. It gives no European passport.
  • You qualify for an exemption (exemption d'agrément) under CMF L. 521-3. The ACPR decides within 3 months of a complete file (instruction 2022-I-22, annex 2), and exempt firms send annual declarations.
  • You are already a payment institution in another EEA state. You passport into France instead (PSD2 Art 28).
  • You hold stored value. Then you need the EMI licence in France.

Payment institution license requirements in France

Capital and own funds

The ACPR page says it checks for sufficient initial capital and prudential own funds, and gives no amount. The EU floors are EUR 125,000 for most services, EUR 50,000 for payment initiation only and EUR 20,000 for money remittance only (PSD2 Art 7). Own funds follow one of three methods (PSD2 Arts 8 and 9). A French decree sets the national figures, so confirm them with the ACPR before you fix your capital plan. The simplified authorisation adapts the capital rules.

Management and fit and proper

The effective managers (dirigeants effectifs) must meet honorability, knowledge, experience and competence requirements. The ACPR page gives no minimum number of managers.

Substance

The central administration (administration centrale) must be on the same national territory as the registered office (siège statutaire).

Insurance and safeguarding

If you provide payment initiation or account information, you need professional civil liability insurance. Funds received from users are safeguarded by segregation or by insurance or a guarantee (PSD2 Art 10).

AML, governance and ICT

The file covers governance, internal control, business continuity, security incident handling and ICT arrangements (PSD2 Art 5(1), with DORA applying to ICT risk). The AML programme is part of it. See our AML and compliance programme.

How to apply for a payment institution license in France

  1. Contact the ACPR's authorisations secretariat at contactautorisations@acpr.banque-france.fr to present the project and go through the timetable.
  2. Choose the route: full authorisation, simplified authorisation, AISP registration or exemption. Each has its own annex to instruction 2019-I-22 or 2022-I-22.
  3. Prepare the file. Annex 1 to instruction 2019-I-22 covers the full authorisation, annex 2 the simplified one and annex 3 the AISP registration.
  4. File on the ACPR portal at acpr-portail.banque-france.fr with the supporting documents.
  5. Answer requests for information. The 3-month period runs from receipt of a complete file, and a request for more information extends the review.
  6. The ACPR issues the agrément after the Banque de France's opinion on the security of payment means.

If you later add services, an extension of the authorisation is decided within 2 months of a complete file. The ACPR pages we read do not state the language of the file. Ask in the pre-application meeting whether English annexes are accepted.

Payment institution license cost in France

The ACPR procedure page states no application fee amount and points to a separate page on supervisory contributions. That page sets annual charges for banking-sector entities on the REGAFI register at 0.66 per mille of capital requirements or minimum capital, with a EUR 500 minimum (CMF L. 612-20). It does not say which category covers payment institutions, so ask contribution@acpr.banque-france.fr what applies to you.

After the ACPR grants the license

  • Register. Authorised entities appear on the ACPR's REGAFI register.
  • Supervision charge. You pay the annual ACPR contribution under the rules above.
  • Passport. You notify the ACPR of the states and services. It forwards the notice within 1 month and the host state assesses it within 1 month (PSD2 Art 28). A simplified EP cannot passport.
  • Growth. If a simplified EP passes the volume ceiling, it needs full authorisation (PSD2 Art 32 gives 30 calendar days to apply once the conditions are breached).
  • Change of control. The ACPR has a separate procedure page for changes in situation. Confirm the route with the ACPR before any share transfer.

France compared with other countries

If you will hold stored value, see the EMI licence in France. For crypto services you go to the AMF, covered in the MiCA crypto licence in France. For the same licence elsewhere, compare the payment institution licence in Spain, Lithuania and Italy. The EU baseline is on the payment institution licence page.

Payment institution license in France FAQs

How much does a payment institution license cost in France?

The ACPR procedure page states no application fee amount. You need EUR 20,000, EUR 50,000 or EUR 125,000 of initial capital depending on the services (PSD2 Art 7) and a French entity with local management. Our fee starts at EUR 60,000.

How much capital do you need for a payment institution in France?

The EU floors are EUR 125,000 for most services, EUR 50,000 for payment initiation only and EUR 20,000 for money remittance only (PSD2 Art 7). The simplified authorisation adapts the capital rules.

How long does the ACPR take to decide?

The ACPR issues the agrément within 3 months of receiving a complete file. A request for more information extends the review. Your preparation time comes before the period starts.

What is the simplified payment institution in France?

The agrément simplifié in CMF L. 522-11-1 applies if average monthly payment volume stays at or below EUR 3 million and you offer no money transmission service. It gives no passport.

Do I need to register as an AISP or get a licence?

If account information is your only service, you register with the ACPR instead of applying for a licence. It is a separate procedure under PSD2 Art 33 with its own annex.

Do I need an office in France?

The central administration must be on the same national territory as the registered office. The ACPR page describes no minimum headcount.

Does the application have to be in French?

The ACPR pages we read do not say. They are published in French. Ask at the pre-application meeting.

Can a French payment institution serve customers in other EU countries?

Yes, if fully authorised, by notification under PSD2 Art 28. The simplified authorisation does not carry the passport.
