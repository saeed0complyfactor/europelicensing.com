Crypto License in France

A crypto licence in France means authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider (CASP, or prestataire de services sur crypto-actifs, PSCA) from the Autorité des marchés financiers (AMF) under MiCA. The old French PSAN regime ended on 1 July 2026, and it never carried an EU passport. The AMF charges EUR 10,000 at authorisation and EUR 10,000 a year, per its 2025 contributions guide.

Who needs a crypto license (agrément PSCA) in France?

You need MiCA authorisation to provide crypto-asset services as a business from France or to French clients: custody, exchange, trading platforms, order execution, placing, transfer, advice, portfolio management, and reception and transmission of orders. Since 2 July 2026 the PSAN route is closed, and only providers authorised under MiCA may operate in France (AMF).

Some firms take a different route.

  • You are a bank, investment firm, EMI or other listed financial entity. You can notify under MiCA Art 60 at least 40 working days before starting, instead of applying. An EMI may notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues.
  • You are authorised in another EU state. You passport into France and need no AMF licence.
  • You issue asset-referenced or e-money tokens. The ACPR authorises those (AMF).
  • You hold or transfer e-money tokens for clients. This can also touch payment licensing. See the payment institution licence in France.

Crypto license requirements in France

Capital by class

Class Services Minimum capital
1 Order execution, placing, reception and transmission of orders, advice, portfolio management, transfer EUR 50,000
2 Class 1 plus custody and administration, exchange for funds or for other crypto-assets EUR 125,000
3 Class 2 plus operating a trading platform EUR 150,000

Prudential safeguards are the higher of that capital and one quarter of the previous year's fixed overheads, with a forecast for firms under a year old (MiCA Art 67).

Legal form and substance

MiCA needs a registered office in a Member State, effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (Art 59(2)). A French company is the usual route for an AMF application.

Governance, ICT and AML

The management body needs good repute and the right skills, and qualifying shareholders are vetted (MiCA Art 68). The AMF lists the common rules a provider must meet: AML and counter-terrorist financing, cyber security, good repute and competence, conduct, governance, conflicts of interest and custody of funds where applicable. DORA applies to your ICT risk management.

Client safeguards

Client funds go to a credit institution or central bank by the end of the next business day. Custody needs an agreement, a custody policy and segregation (MiCA Arts 70 and 75). Our MiCA CASP licence page lists each rule.

How to apply for a crypto license in France

  1. Choose the class from the services you will offer.
  2. Set up the French company, or plan the conversion of an existing one.
  3. Prepare the MiCA Art 62 file with the technical-standard annexes. The AMF notes that first versions of files are often incomplete, and that questions or structural changes add delay.
  4. File with the AMF. The AMF's MiCA page gave a dedicated email mailbox for applications when we read it, so confirm the current channel before sending.
  5. The AMF acknowledges within 5 working days, checks completeness within 25 working days and decides within 40 working days of a complete application. It can suspend the clock for up to 20 working days to ask for information (MiCA Art 63). The AMF says ESMA guidance allows up to four months for the review once the file is complete.
  6. After the decision, the AMF sends an invoice for the EUR 10,000 contribution. It falls due on the day of authorisation and the AMF guide says to pay within six months of the grant in the first year.

The AMF's earlier simplified procedure for PSANs with an enhanced registration or an agrément was tied to the transition and has no use now that the transition is over.

The AMF contributions guide (2025 edition, section 1.18) sets the amounts for PSCAs authorised from 30 December 2024. It lists no separate filing fee for PSCAs.

Other cost lines, with no amounts here: the French company and registered office, directors and staff, audited accounts if you convert an existing company, French translation, custody and safeguarding arrangements, professional indemnity or own-funds cover, AML and ICT systems, and advisers. After the AMF grants the license

  • Register. You appear in the ESMA interim MiCA register, with the AMF as competent authority.
  • Supervision. The AMF supervises PSCAs' compliance with professional and conduct obligations (AMF release of 6 July 2026) and collects the annual contribution.
  • Passport. You notify the AMF of the services and states. It forwards the notice within 10 working days, and you may start on receipt of that communication or on the 15th calendar day (MiCA Art 65).
  • Changes. Material changes to the file go to the AMF. Confirm the notice route for share transfers with it.

PSAN to PSCA: status in France on 23 November 2026

  • The French national regime for digital-asset service providers (prestataires de services sur actifs numériques, PSAN) came from the Pacte law, with a simple registration, an enhanced registration and an optional agrément. None of them gave an EU passport (AMF, 2 August 2024).
  • Firms that provided services before MiCA could continue until 1 July 2026, or until the AMF granted or refused authorisation, whichever came first (AMF).
  • The AMF said the transition was over from 2 July 2026. Under its list of struck-off PSANs, those with a MiCA authorisation left the PSAN list on the date of that authorisation, and those without one were removed automatically on 2 July 2026.
  • Firms without authorisation had to follow an orderly wind-down plan and return clients' assets. The AMF notice says unauthorised provision after 1 July 2026 carries up to two years' imprisonment and a EUR 30,000 fine, and the AMF may publish a list of unauthorised providers and ask courts to block their websites.
  • On 6 July 2026 the AMF said 31 French actors were authorised, with some authorisations still being finalised, against 283 in the EU.
  • The ESMA interim MiCA register file we downloaded on 23 November 2026 lists 36 entities with France as home state, all under the AMF. They include CACEIS Bank, Banque Delubac, Bpifrance Investissement and Société Générale Forge, and crypto-native firms such as Circle Internet Financial Europe, Coinhouse, Paymium, Woorton, Flowdesk Europe and Bitstack Digital Assets. The 36 and the AMF's 31 are not the same count. The AMF figure is dated 6 July, and the register marks 6 of the 36 as Art 60 notifications only and 3 as both a notification and an authorisation.

France compared with other countries

For the same MiCA licence elsewhere, see the crypto licence in Spain, Lithuania and Italy. Capital under MiCA is the same in all of them. Fees, language and local substance rules differ. In France, the EMI and payment institution licences come from the ACPR, a different regulator from the AMF.

Crypto license in France FAQs

How much does a crypto license cost in France?

The AMF guide sets EUR 10,000 at authorisation and EUR 10,000 a year, plus 0.0094 per mille of assets under custody for custodians (minimum EUR 1,500). You also need capital of EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000 depending on the class. Our fee starts at EUR 60,000.

What is a PSAN and what replaced it?

A PSAN was a digital-asset service provider under the French Pacte regime. MiCA replaced it, and the authorisation is now the PSCA.

Can I keep operating as a PSAN?

No. The transition ended on 1 July 2026 and PSANs without a MiCA authorisation were struck off on 2 July 2026.

Did a PSAN registration give an EU passport?

No. The AMF said prior registration or optional authorisation gave no European passport during the transition. A MiCA authorisation does.

Which regulator grants a crypto license in France?

The AMF. The ACPR authorises asset-referenced and e-money token issuers.

How long does the AMF take to decide?

MiCA Art 63 gives 25 working days for completeness and 40 working days to decide on a complete application, with suspensions for questions. The AMF says ESMA guidance allows up to four months for the review of a complete file.

How many crypto firms are authorised in France?

The AMF counted 31 on 6 July 2026. The ESMA interim register file we downloaded on 23 November 2026 lists 36 entities with France as home state, including Art 60 notifiers.

Does a French CASP license work in other EU countries?

Yes. You notify the AMF and can start in the host state on receipt of the communication or on the 15th calendar day (MiCA Art 65).
