EMI License in France

An electronic money institution (EMI, or établissement de monnaie électronique, EME) licence from the ACPR lets a French company issue e-money and, if you add them, provide payment services. The ACPR decides within 3 months of a complete file, after an opinion from the Banque de France on the security of payment means. The ACPR pages we checked state no capital figure, no fee and no filing language, so those points go on your list for the pre-application meeting.

Who needs an EMI license in France?

You need an agrément as an EME if you issue electronic money in France as a business. The CMF defines e-money in L. 315-1, and the ACPR page is built around that definition. A wallet or prepaid product with a spendable balance usually falls inside it.

An EME can also provide payment services, with or without e-money. If you add payment services later, the ACPR page says you communicate it three months before you start (CMF L. 526-2, items 1 and 2).

Some firms need a different route.

  • You are small. The simplified authorisation in CMF L. 526-19 applies if average e-money outstanding stays at or below EUR 5 million a month and e-money units stay at or below EUR 150. It gives no European passport.
  • Your use of e-money is narrow enough for an exemption (exemption d'agrément) under CMF L. 525-5. The ACPR has a separate file for it (instruction 2022-I-22, annex 1) and exempt firms send an annual declaration.
  • You only transmit money and hold no stored value. The payment institution licence in France carries lower capital.
  • You are already an EMI in another EEA state. You passport into France instead (PSD2 Art 28).

EMI license requirements in France

Capital and own funds

The ACPR page says it checks for sufficient initial capital and prudential own funds, and gives no amount. The EU floor is EUR 350,000 of initial capital (EMD2 Art 4). Own funds then stay at or above 2% of average outstanding e-money (EMD2 Art 5(3)). A French decree sets the national figure, so confirm it with the ACPR before you fix your capital plan. The simplified authorisation adapts the capital and own funds rules.

Management and fit and proper

The effective managers (dirigeants effectifs) must meet honorability, knowledge, experience and competence requirements. In a hybrid entity, which runs e-money alongside another business, the same applies to the person responsible for the e-money activity. The ACPR page gives no minimum number of managers.

Substance

The central administration (administration centrale) must sit in the same national territory as the registered office (siège statutaire). Plan for real management in France.

Safeguarding

Funds received for issued e-money are safeguarded by segregation or by insurance or a guarantee (EMD2 Art 7). The file has to describe the arrangement (PSD2 Art 5(1)).

AML, governance and ICT

The file covers governance, internal control, business continuity, security and ICT arrangements (PSD2 Art 5(1), with DORA applying to ICT risk). The anti-money-laundering programme is part of it. See our AML and compliance programme.

How to apply for an EMI license in France

  1. Contact the ACPR's authorisations secretariat (Secrétariat de la Direction des autorisations) at contactautorisations@acpr.banque-france.fr to present the project and go through the timetable. The ACPR invites this before filing.
  2. Choose the route: full authorisation, simplified authorisation (L. 526-19), or exemption (L. 525-5). Each has its own annex.
  3. Prepare the file on the ACPR template. For the EME the ACPR points to instruction 2013-I-09, annex 1, and annex 2 for the simplified route. Adding payment services uses the annex to instruction 2014-I-05.
  4. File on the ACPR portal at acpr-portail.banque-france.fr with the supporting documents.
  5. Answer requests for information. The 3-month period runs from receipt of a complete file, and a request for more information extends the review.
  6. The ACPR issues the agrément after the Banque de France's opinion on the security of payment means.

The ACPR pages we read do not state the language of the file. Ask in the pre-application meeting whether English annexes are accepted, and budget for French versions of the corporate documents.

EMI license cost in France

The ACPR EME page states no application fee amount, and it points to a separate page on supervisory contributions. That page sets annual charges for banking-sector entities on the REGAFI register at 0.66 per mille of capital requirements or minimum capital, with a EUR 500 minimum (CMF L. 612-20). It does not say which category covers EMEs, so ask contribution@acpr.banque-france.fr what applies to you.

Our own fee: from EUR 60,000.

After the ACPR grants the license

  • Register. Authorised entities appear on the ACPR's REGAFI register.
  • Supervision charge. You pay the annual ACPR contribution under the rules above.
  • Passport. You notify the ACPR of the states and services. It forwards the notice within 1 month and the host state assesses it within 1 month (PSD2 Art 28). A simplified EME cannot passport.
  • Change of control and changes in situation. The ACPR has a separate procedure page for changes. Confirm the route with the ACPR before any share transfer.

See passporting and post-licence support.

France compared with other countries

If you only provide payments, see the payment institution licence in France. For crypto services you go to the AMF instead, covered in the MiCA crypto licence in France. For the same licence elsewhere, compare the EMI licence in Spain, Lithuania and Italy. The EU baseline is on the EMI licence page.

EMI license in France FAQs

How much does an EMI license cost in France?

The ACPR procedure page states no application fee amount. You need at least EUR 350,000 of initial capital (EMD2 Art 4) and a French entity with local management. Our fee starts at EUR 60,000.

How much capital do you need for an EMI license in France?

The EU minimum is EUR 350,000 (EMD2 Art 4). Own funds then stay at or above 2% of average outstanding e-money (EMD2 Art 5(3)). The simplified authorisation adapts these rules.

How long does the ACPR take to decide?

The ACPR issues the agrément within 3 months of receiving a complete file. A request for more information extends the review. Your preparation time comes before the period starts.

What is the simplified EME authorisation?

CMF L. 526-19 allows a lighter authorisation if average e-money outstanding stays at or below EUR 5 million a month and units stay at or below EUR 150. It gives no European passport.

Do I need an office in France?

The central administration must be on the same national territory as the registered office. The ACPR page does not describe a minimum headcount.

Does the application have to be in French?

The ACPR pages we read do not say. They are published in French. Ask at the pre-application meeting.

Can a French EMI serve customers in other EU countries?

Yes, if fully authorised, by notification under PSD2 Art 28 applied through EMD2 Art 3(1). The simplified authorisation does not carry the passport.

Can I issue e-money without an agrément?

Only under the exemption in CMF L. 525-5, which has its own file and annual declaration.
