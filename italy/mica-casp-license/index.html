Crypto License in Italy

A crypto licence in Italy means authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider (CASP) from Consob under MiCA, decided with the opinion of the Banca d'Italia. Consob charges a EUR 20,000 supervisory contribution when you file. The Italian transitional period for firms on the old OAM register ended on 1 July 2026.

Who needs a crypto license in Italy?

You need MiCA authorisation to provide crypto-asset services as a business from Italy or to Italian clients: custody, exchange, trading platforms, order execution, placing, transfer, advice, portfolio management, and reception and transmission of orders.

Some firms take a different route.

  • You are a bank, SIM, SGR, EMI or other listed financial entity. You notify the authority that authorised you under MiCA Art 60, at least 40 working days before starting, for notifiable services. An EMI may notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues. Other services still need authorisation. Banks, EMIs and SGRs notify the Banca d'Italia by PEC.
  • You are an EMI or payment institution adding crypto services. The Banca d'Italia authorises EMIs for non-notifiable services and payment institutions for CASP services, after consulting Consob.
  • You are authorised in another EU state. You passport into Italy and need no Consob licence.
  • You were on the old OAM register of virtual-asset providers. That route has ended, as the status section below explains.

Crypto license requirements in Italy

Capital by class

Class Services Minimum capital
1 Order execution, placing, reception and transmission of orders, advice, portfolio management, transfer EUR 50,000
2 Class 1 plus custody and administration, exchange for funds or for other crypto-assets EUR 125,000
3 Class 2 plus operating a trading platform EUR 150,000

Prudential safeguards are the higher of that capital and one quarter of the previous year's fixed overheads, with a forecast for firms under a year old (MiCA Art 67).

Legal form and substance

MiCA needs a registered office in a Member State, effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (Art 59(2)). The Consob page and the Banca d'Italia page we read state no further Italian form or office rule.

Governance, ICT and AML

The management body needs good repute and the right skills, and qualifying shareholders are vetted (MiCA Art 68). DORA applies to your ICT risk management, and you need an AML programme that fits your services.

Client safeguards

Client funds go to a credit institution or central bank by the end of the next business day. Custody needs an agreement, a custody policy and segregation (MiCA Arts 70 and 75). Our MiCA CASP licence page lists each rule.

How to apply for a crypto license in Italy

  1. Choose the class from the services you will offer.
  2. Decide the legal form and set up the Italian company, or plan the conversion of an existing one.
  3. Prepare the MiCA Art 62 file with the technical-standard annexes.
  4. File with Consob and pay the EUR 20,000 supervisory contribution at filing (Resolution 23700/2025, which sets the payment terms in Art 4).
  5. Authorities acknowledge the file, and check completeness within 25 working days. If the file is incomplete, you get a deadline to supply the rest, and the application may be archived if it stays incomplete.
  6. The authorities can request more information by the 20th working day, and the assessment is suspended for up to 20 working days while the request is pending.
  7. The decision comes within 40 working days of a complete application, and the final decision is notified within 5 working days after the assessment ends.
  8. Start operating within 12 months of authorisation.

For an Art 60 notification, you file at least 40 working days before you start. The authority checks completeness within 20 working days, and you cannot provide the services while the notification is incomplete.

Crypto license cost in Italy

Consob charges a EUR 20,000 supervisory contribution at filing, under Resolution No. 23700/2025, published on 1 December 2025. Its Art 4 sets how to pay. The Banca d'Italia CASP page says nothing on contributions for its route.

After Consob grants the license

  • Register. You appear in the ESMA interim MiCA register, with Consob as competent authority.
  • Start of business. You start operating within 12 months of authorisation.
  • Passport. You notify Consob of the services and states. It forwards the notice within 10 working days, and you may start on receipt of that communication or on the 15th calendar day (MiCA Art 65).
  • Changes. Material changes to the file go to the authority. Confirm the notice route for share transfers with Consob.

Crypto license status in Italy on 23 November 2026

  • The MiCAR transitional period ended on 1 July 2026. Consob and the Banca d'Italia announced it in a joint press release dated 30 June 2026: Consob had authorised 8 CASPs, in close coordination with the Banca d'Italia, and Banca Sella had notified the Banca d'Italia, 9 entities in all.
  • Firms on the old OAM register could continue until the authorities granted or refused authorisation and no later than 30 June 2026. Firms that did not plan to apply were asked to stop operating in Italy. We could read Consob Communication 16/25 only through search extracts, so check the exact wording with Consob.
  • The ESMA interim MiCA register file we downloaded on 23 November 2026 lists 10 entities with Italy as home state, all under Consob: Checksig, Olliv Italia, Riv-Digital, Conio, Banca Sella, Cryptosmart, Young Platform, Hodlie, Hercle and YouHodler Italy.
  • Consob's own page points to the ESMA register for the list of CASPs.

Italy compared with other countries

For the same MiCA licence elsewhere, see the crypto licence in France, Spain and Lithuania. Capital under MiCA is the same in all of them. Fees, language and local substance rules differ. In Italy, the EMI and payment institution licences come from the Banca d'Italia, which also takes part in CASP procedures.

Crypto license in Italy FAQs

How much does a crypto license cost in Italy?

Consob charges a EUR 20,000 supervisory contribution at filing (Resolution 23700/2025). You also need capital of EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000 depending on the class. our fee range for CASP licence in Italy

Which regulator grants a crypto license in Italy?

Consob for specialised CASPs, with the Banca d'Italia consulted. The Banca d'Italia authorises EMIs and payment institutions that add crypto services, after consulting Consob.

Who needs a MiCA license in Italy?

Anyone providing MiCA crypto-asset services as a business in Italy who is not an authorised CASP elsewhere in the EU or a listed financial firm using the Art 60 notification.

How long does Consob take to decide?

MiCA Art 63 gives 25 working days for completeness and 40 working days to decide on a complete application. The assessment can be suspended for up to 20 working days while the authorities wait for information.

How many crypto firms are authorised in Italy?

The ESMA interim MiCA register file we downloaded on 23 November 2026 lists 10 entities with Italy as home state, including Banca Sella. The joint Consob and Banca d'Italia release of 30 June 2026 counted 9 entities, 8 authorised and 1 notified.

Can I keep operating on the old OAM registration?

No. The transitional period ended on 1 July 2026.

Can a bank or EMI add crypto services without a Consob licence?

For notifiable services, yes. You notify the authority that authorised you under Art 60, at least 40 working days before you start. Other services need authorisation.

Does an Italian CASP license work in other EU countries?

Yes. You notify Consob and can start in the host state on receipt of the communication or on the 15th calendar day (MiCA Art 65).
