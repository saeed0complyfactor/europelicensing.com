Payment Institution License in Italy

A payment institution (istituto di pagamento, IP) licence from the Banca d'Italia lets an Italian company provide payment services and passport them across the EU and EEA. Paid-in capital runs from EUR 20,000 to EUR 125,000 depending on the services, and the procedure takes 90 days from a complete and regular application. The registered office and head office must be in Italy.

Who needs a payment institution license in Italy?

You need authorisation as an istituto di pagamento if you provide payment services in Italy as a business. Typical cases are money remittance, payment accounts, card acquiring, payment initiation and account information.

Some firms need a different route.

  • You hold stored value. Then you need the EMI licence in Italy.
  • You only provide account information. PSD2 Art 33 makes that a registration, not a licence. The Banca d'Italia pages we read do not describe the Italian route, so confirm it with the Banca d'Italia. The Italian page does require liability insurance for account information providers.
  • You are already a payment institution in another EEA state. You passport into Italy (PSD2 Art 28).
  • You are small and only do money remittance. Chapter IX of the Disposizioni di vigilanza (consolidated text to 22 February 2022) covers the istituto di pagamento a operatività limitata: a monthly average of payment transactions up to EUR 3 million over the previous twelve months, money remittance only, no activity in other countries and no credit. It still keeps own funds at or above the minimum initial capital. If you pass the threshold, you tell the Banca d'Italia within 30 days and have 60 more days to meet the full rules or stop.
  • You offer crypto services as well. A payment institution can seek authorisation for crypto services from the Banca d'Italia after Consob is consulted. See the MiCA crypto licence in Italy.

Payment institution license requirements in Italy

Capital and own funds

Initial capital is paid in: EUR 125,000 for services 1 to 5, EUR 50,000 for payment initiation and EUR 20,000 for money remittance only (PSD2 Art 7; Banca d'Italia page). The page gives no separate figure for an account-information-only firm. Own funds follow one of three methods (PSD2 Arts 8 and 9).

Legal form and substance

The IP must be a società per azioni, a società in accomandita per azioni, a società a responsabilità limitata or a cooperative. The registered office and head office must be in Italy, and at least part of the payment services activity is carried out there.

Fit and proper

Qualifying shareholders and corporate officers must meet the fit-and-proper rules, and there must be no impediment to effective supervision, including through close links.

Insurance and safeguarding

If you provide payment initiation or account information, you need a civil liability insurance policy or equivalent guarantee. The register shows insurance details for payment initiation providers. User funds are safeguarded by segregation or by insurance or a guarantee (PSD2 Art 10).

AML, governance and ICT

The programme of operations, capital sustainability plan and organisational structure report are part of the file, together with ICT and DORA arrangements (PSD2 Art 5(1)). See our AML and compliance programme.

How to apply for a payment institution license in Italy

  1. Define the services and map them to TUB art. 1(2)(h-septies.1), which decides your capital.
  2. Ask for a pre-filing meeting if you want one. It is optional and does not change the time limits. Write to Servizio.Riv.Costituzioni@bancaditalia.it or riv@pec.bancaditalia.it with your contact details.
  3. Prepare the documents the Disposizioni di vigilanza list: the deed of incorporation, by-laws, a capital sustainability programme and an organisational structure report, among others. Items d, g and h must be dated within 6 months of filing.
  4. File by PEC. Under the Disposizioni (consolidated text to 22 February 2022), the administrators file after the deed of incorporation and before registration in the companies register.
  5. The 90 days run from a complete and regular application. If it has defects, you get a notice listing them and the procedure does not start. The Banca d'Italia can ask for information, inspect, or take opinions from other authorities, and suspension lasts at most 180 days.
  6. If the outcome looks negative, you get a notice of grounds for refusal and 10 days to send written observations.
  7. After authorisation, send the companies-register certificate. You are entered in the register under TUB art. 114-septies with an identification code, you join an out-of-court dispute scheme (TUB art. 128-bis), and you notify the start of operations within 12 months of registration.

The pages we read do not state the language of the file. Ask in the pre-filing meeting.

Payment institution license cost in Italy

The Banca d'Italia pages state no application fee. Ask the Banca d'Italia whether any supervisory charge applies to payment institutions before you budget.

After the Banca d'Italia grants the license

  • Register. The register under TUB art. 114-septies shows the services you may provide and, for payment initiation, your insurance details.
  • Start of business. Notify the Banca d'Italia within 12 months of registration.
  • Passport. You notify the Banca d'Italia of the states and services. It forwards the notice within 1 month and the host state assesses it within 1 month (PSD2 Art 28).
  • Change of control. Confirm the notice route with the Banca d'Italia before any share transfer.

Italy compared with other countries

If you will hold stored value, see the EMI licence in Italy. For crypto services the authority is Consob, covered in the MiCA crypto licence in Italy. For the same licence elsewhere, compare the payment institution licence in France, Spain and Lithuania. The EU baseline is on the payment institution licence page.

Payment institution license in Italy FAQs

How much does a payment institution license cost in Italy?

The Banca d'Italia pages we read state no application fee. You need EUR 20,000, EUR 50,000 or EUR 125,000 of paid-in capital depending on the services, and an Italian entity with its head office in Italy. our fee range for payment institution licence in Italy

How much capital do you need for a payment institution in Italy?

EUR 125,000 for services 1 to 5, EUR 50,000 for payment initiation and EUR 20,000 for money remittance only, all paid in (PSD2 Art 7; Banca d'Italia).

How long does the Banca d'Italia take to decide?

The procedure runs 90 days from a complete and regular application. Suspension lasts at most 180 days. Your preparation time comes before the clock starts.

How do I get a payment institution license in Italy?

You form an Italian company, prepare the documents the Disposizioni list, file by PEC, answer information requests, and enter the register under TUB art. 114-septies after authorisation.

Is there a small payment institution in Italy?

Yes, for money remittance only. The Disposizioni di vigilanza provide a limited-operation payment institution for a monthly average up to EUR 3 million. It cannot operate in other countries.

Do I need an office in Italy?

Yes. The registered office and the head office must be in Italy.

Does the application have to be in Italian?

The pages we read do not say. Ask at the pre-filing meeting.

Can an Italian payment institution serve customers in other EU countries?

Yes, by notification under PSD2 Art 28.
