Crypto License in Cyprus

A Cypriot crypto licence is a MiCA authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider (CASP), granted by the Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission (CySEC). It covers services such as custody, exchange, trading platforms, transfers and advice, and can be passported across the EU and EEA. The Central Bank of Cyprus may also require a separate licence if you handle e-money tokens for clients.

Who needs a crypto licence in Cyprus?

You need authorisation if you provide crypto-asset services to clients in the EU from a Cypriot base, or you want Cyprus as the home state for an EU passport. MiCA Art 59 bars any firm from providing crypto-asset services in the EU unless it is an authorised CASP or an entity allowed to provide them under Art 60.

Other routes can fit better:

  • Notification under MiCA Art 60. Credit institutions, investment firms, central securities depositories, market operators, UCITS management companies and AIF managers can notify instead of applying, at least 40 working days before starting. CySEC charges EUR 10,000 for that notification when it comes from a Cypriot CIF, depository, market operator, management company or AIF. An EMI can notify for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues. See our EMI licence in Cyprus.
  • Authorisation elsewhere. A CASP authorised in another member state can serve Cypriot clients through a passport. The old Cypriot national regime for existing providers ended on 1 July 2026 (CBC note on MiCA Art 143(3)).
  • A separate Central Bank licence. Where you transfer or hold e-money tokens for clients, the Central Bank of Cyprus treats that as a payment service. CySEC authorisation does not replace it. See below and our payment institution page.
  • Token issuance. Offering tokens, as opposed to providing services, falls under the MiCA white paper and issuer rules, with their own CySEC fees. This page covers service providers.

Requirements for a MiCA licence in Cyprus

Legal form and presence

A legal person with a registered office in a Member State, the place of effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (MiCA Art 59(2)). Authorisation lets you serve every Member State without a physical presence there.

Capital and prudential safeguards

Minimum capital is EUR 50,000 for class 1 services, EUR 125,000 for class 2 (custody, exchange) and EUR 150,000 for class 3 (trading platform). You hold the higher of that figure and a quarter of the preceding year's fixed overheads, in CET1 own funds and, or, an insurance policy (MiCA Art 67). A firm under a year old uses projected overheads.

Management and fit and proper

Management body members need good repute, knowledge, skills, experience and enough time. Qualifying shareholders need good repute (MiCA Art 68).

Governance, ICT and AML

You need ICT and business continuity arrangements under DORA, Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, an AML and counter-terrorist financing framework, and records kept for five years (MiCA Art 68). Complaints, conflict-of-interest, outsourcing and custody rules apply under Art 71 to 75.

Client assets

Client crypto-assets are kept separate from your own and not used for your own account. Client funds are placed with a credit institution or central bank by the end of the next business day (MiCA Art 70).

How to apply for a MiCA licence in Cyprus

  1. Map your services to the MiCA classes to fix capital and fees. Services are priced one by one in Cyprus.
  2. Set up a company with an EU place of effective management and at least one EU-resident director.
  3. Prepare the MiCA Art 62 file, covering business plan, governance, ICT, AML, custody and complaints policies, and the people and shareholders.
  4. Submit it to CySEC with the application charge for each service you ask for. CySEC publishes its MiCA material in the Crypto-Assets Entities (MiCAR) section of its site.
  5. CySEC acknowledges receipt within 5 working days and checks completeness within 25 working days (MiCA Art 63(1)-(2)).
  6. It decides within 40 working days of a complete application. It can ask for more information by the 20th working day, and the clock pauses for up to 20 working days while you reply (Art 63(9) and 63(12)).
  7. On authorisation, pay the annual fee when due and file passport notifications.

The statutory clock starts from a complete application. File preparation and question rounds come on top.

EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000 by class, or a quarter of fixed overheads if higher (MiCA Art 67).

After authorisation

  • Register: authorised CASPs appear in ESMA's interim MiCA register and in CySEC's MiCAR register.
  • Reporting and fees: you pay the annual fee within four months of the financial year end.
  • Passport: you send CySEC the member states, services and start date. It forwards the notification within 10 working days, and you may start on receipt of that communication or by the 15th calendar day (MiCA Art 65).
  • Changes: notify CySEC of every change to the management body (EUR 2,000 each) and before an acquisition of your firm (EUR 8,000).
  • Central Bank licence: if you hold or move e-money tokens for clients and use no authorised payment partner, a CBC licence is required, with a passport process under Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/2055.

Status of the Cypriot regime on 23 November 2026

The Central Bank of Cyprus says the national regime for existing providers ran until 1 July 2026 under MiCA Art 143(3). ESMA's public statement of 23 June 2026 says unauthorised CASPs must stop onboarding EU clients and wind down.

ESMA's interim MiCA register lists 26 entries with Cyprus as home Member State, in the file downloaded on 23 November 2026, one of them with an authorisation end date. Names include eToro (Europe), Revolut Digital Assets (Europe), Trading 212 Markets, XTB, Unlimit Crypto and Solidgate's Cypriot entity. Check the live register before you quote a figure.

The Central Bank of Cyprus has a separate track for CASPs: if you transfer or hold e-money tokens for clients, it requires PI authorisation, based on the EBA's no-action letter of 10 June 2025. Its deadline for existing CASPs to apply was 20 February 2026.

Cyprus vs other countries

The MiCA rules are the same everywhere, but fees and the supervisor differ. Compare the crypto licence in Malta, where the MFSA charges EUR 10,000, 20,000 or 25,000 by class, with the CASP authorisation in Lithuania and Ireland. In Cyprus, the EMI licence and payment institution licence sit alongside this one. The general framework is in our MiCA CASP licence guide.

Crypto licence in Cyprus FAQs

Who grants a crypto licence in Cyprus?

CySEC authorises CASPs under MiCA Art 63. The Central Bank of Cyprus is a separate authority for EMIs and PIs, and may require its own licence if you transfer or hold e-money tokens for clients.

How much is the CySEC application fee for a CASP?

It depends on the service: EUR 5,000 for exchange or transfer services, EUR 8,000 for execution, placing, order reception, advice or portfolio management, EUR 10,000 for custody and EUR 30,000 for a trading platform (CySEC Directive DI 73-2009-04, Annex II). The directive's English text is unofficial.

How much capital does a Cypriot CASP need?

EUR 50,000 for class 1, EUR 125,000 for class 2 and EUR 150,000 for class 3, or a quarter of the previous year's fixed overheads if higher (MiCA Art 67).

How long does MiCA authorisation take in Cyprus?

CySEC has 25 working days to check completeness and 40 working days to decide (MiCA Art 63). Information requests pause the clock for up to 20 working days. Preparation time comes on top, and we do not quote a timeline.

Can I still use the old Cyprus transitional regime?

No. The Central Bank of Cyprus records that the national regime ended on 1 July 2026, and ESMA's statement of 23 June 2026 says unauthorised CASPs must wind down.

Do you need a physical office in Cyprus?

MiCA needs a registered office in a Member State, effective management in the EU and an EU-resident director (Art 59(2)). We have not confirmed any CySEC substance expectation beyond those rules.

Do I need both a CySEC and a Central Bank licence?

Only if your services include transferring or holding e-money tokens for clients. The Central Bank says that counts as a payment service needing PI authorisation, unless you use an authorised payment partner.

Can a Cypriot CASP serve other EU countries?

Yes. MiCA authorisation is valid across the EU, and CySEC informs host authorities within 10 working days of your notification (MiCA Art 65).
