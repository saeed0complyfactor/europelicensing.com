Payment Institution License in Sweden

A payment institution (PI) licence from Finansinspektionen (FI), the Swedish Financial Supervisory Authority, lets you provide payment services such as transfers, card acquiring and money remittance from Sweden and passport them across the EEA. It is for payment processors, remittance firms and payment initiation providers. FI's application fee is SEK 405,000, and Sweden's only small-firm route is a registered status for account information services.

Who needs a payment institution license in Sweden?

You need authorisation before you provide a PSD2 Annex I payment service in or from Sweden, unless you already hold a bank, EMI or other PSD2 licence (PSD2 Art 11(1); Lag 2010:751, 2 kap. 2 section). The services are cash placement and withdrawal, execution of transfers, direct debits and card payments, issuing or acquiring payment instruments, money remittance, payment initiation and account information.

Pick the route that matches your business:

  • A full licence passports into the EEA and carries the capital tier for your services.
  • If you provide only account information services, you can apply to be a registered payment service provider instead. FI must grant the exemption when the conditions are met, including suitable management and liability insurance or a comparable guarantee (Lag 2010:751, 2 kap. 3 section; PSD2 Art 33).
  • An agent of a payment institution has no licence of its own. The principal registers the agent with FI, and FI charges no fee for that (Lag 2010:751, 3 kap. 17 section).
  • An agent of an authorised PI in another EEA state needs no Swedish licence.
  • If customers keep a balance with you for later spending, that is e-money and you need an EMI licence in Sweden.
  • If you hold payment institution status already and want more Annex I services, you apply to extend the licence. The ordinance lists SEK 52,500 for that application.

Payment institution license requirements in Sweden

Capital and own funds

Services (PSD2 Annex I) Initial capital Source
Money remittance only EUR 20,000 Lag 2010:751, 3 kap. 1 section; PSD2 Art 7(a)
Payment initiation EUR 50,000 Lag 2010:751, 3 kap. 1 section; PSD2 Art 7(b)
Services 1 to 5 EUR 125,000 Lag 2010:751, 3 kap. 1 section; PSD2 Art 7(c)
Account information only None; liability insurance or guarantee Lag 2010:751, 2 kap. 3 section; PSD2 Art 33

Own funds must then stay at or above the higher of the initial capital and the Method A, B or C amount FI selects (PSD2 Art 8(1), Art 9).

Legal form and substance

FI can authorise only a Swedish aktiebolag or a Swedish ekonomisk förening (Lag 2010:751, 2 kap. 1 section). A legal entity must have its head office in Sweden (3 kap. 11 section). The text we read has no separate rule that management must sit in Sweden, and FI's page sets no minimum headcount, so agree your local team with FI before you file.

Management and fit and proper

Board members, the CEO and the persons responsible for payment services need enough experience and suitability. Qualifying shareholders must be suitable (PSD2 Art 5(1)(m), (n); Art 11(6)). FI's regulations FFFS 2010:3 set the application contents.

AML officer and controls

The file describes internal controls under Directive (EU) 2015/849 (PSD2 Art 5(1)(k)). Appoint a named AML compliance officer. See our AML and compliance programme.

Safeguarding

For services 1 to 6 you segregate customer funds in a credit institution or central bank account or in secure low-risk assets, or cover them by insurance or a guarantee from outside your group (PSD2 Art 10(1), as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/886).

ICT and DORA

The file covers ICT arrangements under Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, incident handling, business continuity and a security policy (PSD2 Art 5(1)(e), (f), (h), (j)). Payment initiation and account information providers also hold professional indemnity insurance or a comparable guarantee (Art 5(2), (3)).

How to get a payment institution license in Sweden

  1. Map your services to PSD2 Annex I to fix your capital tier, and decide between a licence and, for account information only, registered status.
  2. Set up a Swedish aktiebolag or ekonomisk förening and pay in the capital.
  3. Prepare the file from FI's payment services page and its application form, following FFFS 2010:3.
  4. Submit the application. FI registers it and issues a reference number.
  5. Wait for FI's administrator letter with the fee and payment details. FI tells you not to pay before you have the letter and reference number.
  6. Pay the fee. FI starts processing only when payment arrives, and it may reject the application if payment does not arrive within 30 days (Forordning 2001:911, section 6).
  7. FI decides within three months of a complete application and the fee (Lag 2010:751, 2 kap. 9 section). The statute text we read adds that the licence is deemed granted if FI does not decide in time. Do not plan around that rule, and confirm how FI applies it.

These are statutory limits. Preparation time and question rounds come on top, and we give you a dated plan after scoping. FI's page does not state an application language, so ask FI which language it accepts.

Professional indemnity insurance applies where you provide payment initiation or account information services.

After you get the payment institution license

  • FI enters you in its company register, which you can search by main business, including payment services companies.
  • You report to FI under the payment-services rules and keep own funds above the required level.
  • You pay the annual supervision fee.
  • To use agents, you register each one with FI before it starts, and FI decides within two months of the notification (Lag 2010:751, 3 kap. 17 section).
  • To serve another EEA state, you notify FI, which forwards the notice within 1 month. The host state assesses it within 1 month (PSD2 Art 28(1) and (2)).
  • A change of qualifying holding needs FI's permission, and FI's fee for that permission is SEK 33,000.

Sweden payment institution license vs other countries

Sweden charges SEK 405,000 to apply and offers no small PI tier, only registered status for account information. To compare, see the payment institution licence in Denmark, Lithuania and Ireland. Within Sweden, the sibling pages are the EMI licence and the MiCA CASP licence. For the EU rules behind all of them, read our payment institution licence in Europe guide.

Payment institution license in Sweden FAQs

How much capital does a payment institution need in Sweden?

EUR 125,000 for services 1 to 5, EUR 50,000 for payment initiation and EUR 20,000 for money remittance (Lag 2010:751, 3 kap. 1 section; PSD2 Art 7).

How much does a payment institution licence cost in Sweden?

FI's application fee is SEK 405,000, and the annual supervision fee shown by FI is SEK 150,000. Add the capital and the running costs of a regulated firm: local people, audit, safeguarding, ICT and advisers.

How long does it take to get a payment institution licence in Sweden?

FI decides within three months of a complete application and payment of the fee (Lag 2010:751, 2 kap. 9 section). Preparation and question rounds come on top, and we do not quote a real-world average.

What is a registered payment service provider in Sweden?

A firm that provides only account information services and has applied for exemption from the licence requirement. FI's fee is SEK 232,500 for a legal person, and the annual supervision fee is SEK 50,000.

Is there a small payment institution licence in Sweden?

No. The only reduced route is registered status for account information services. Other payment services need a full licence with capital from EUR 20,000.

Do I need a Swedish company?

Yes. FI can authorise only a Swedish aktiebolag or ekonomisk förening, and a legal entity must have its head office in Sweden.

Can I passport a Swedish payment institution licence?

Yes, by notifying FI (PSD2 Art 28).
