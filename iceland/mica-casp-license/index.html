Crypto License in Iceland

MiCA applies in Iceland, even though Iceland is outside the EU. As an EEA state it took MiCA in through EEA Joint Committee Decision 41/2025, and Act 101/2025 has applied since 1 January 2026. The Central Bank of Iceland (Seðlabanki Íslands) is the competent authority, and the ESMA register lists one Icelandic crypto-asset service provider.

Who needs a crypto license in Iceland?

You need authorisation, or an Art 60 notification, to provide crypto-asset services in Iceland (MiCA Art 59, applied through Act 101/2025). The ten services are defined in MiCA Art 3(1)(16) and include custody, operating a trading platform, exchange for funds, order execution, advice, portfolio management and transfer services.

Other routes can fit better:

  • Art 60 notification for credit institutions, investment firms, EMIs and other regulated firms, at least 40 working days before they start (MiCA Art 60). The scope follows your existing licence. An EMI may notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues.
  • Authorisation in another EEA state, then a passport into Iceland.
  • An EMI licence as the base for issuing e-money tokens.

Crypto license requirements in Iceland

Capital. Your minimum is EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000, depending on the services (MiCA Annex IV), or one quarter of fixed overheads if higher (Art 67).

Management and fit and proper. Directors need good repute, knowledge, skills, experience and time, and qualifying shareholders need good repute (MiCA Art 68).

Substance. MiCA Art 59(2) requires a registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director. As an EEA state, Iceland applies this through the EEA adaptations in Decision 41/2025. We did not read the adaptations, so ask the Central Bank how they apply to an Icelandic or EU structure.

AML. AML/CFT controls are part of the application and the governance rules.

Safeguarding. Client crypto-assets are not used for your own account, and client funds go to a credit institution or central bank by the end of the next business day (MiCA Art 70).

ICT and DORA. Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 applies, and MiCA Art 68 requires ICT and continuity arrangements under it.

Implementing rules. The Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs issued Regulation No 420 on 20 April 2026 under the Act. The Central Bank issued Rules No 714 on 9 June 2026, which replaced its earlier Rules No 332/2026 and cover technical points such as crypto-asset white paper templates. We read only their headings and entry-into-force clauses.

How to get a crypto license in Iceland: process

  1. Choose the services and capital class, or the Art 60 route if you hold another licence.
  2. Prepare the application under MiCA Art 62 and the delegated rules, with a programme of operations, governance, custody and complaints policies, and a financial plan.
  3. Apply to the Central Bank. We did not find its CASP application page, so ask the Central Bank for the form, the filing channel and the language. Its payment institution checklist asks applicants to request a meeting before filing, so ask for one here too.
  4. The Central Bank acknowledges within 5 working days and checks completeness within 25 working days (MiCA Art 63).
  5. It decides within 40 working days of a complete application. Questions pause the clock for up to 20 working days.
  6. ESMA is informed within 2 working days of the decision, and the register is updated.

The statutory periods cover the regulator's assessment. Your preparation and answers come on top.

Other cost lines, without amounts: an EU entity, an EU-resident director and local staff, an AML officer, custody and safeguarding arrangements, audit, ICT security, insurance where relevant, and legal work. Our fee depends on scope: our fee range for a MiCA CASP licence in Iceland.

After you get the crypto license

  • The Central Bank and ESMA list you in their registers.
  • To serve other EEA states, notify the Central Bank under MiCA Art 65. It forwards the notice within 10 working days.
  • Report changes to the information in your application to the Central Bank.
  • Pay the supervisory fees. Under Act 101/2025 Art 15 the Minister sets them by regulation.
  • Check MiCA's qualifying holding rules and the Central Bank's guidance before any share transfer.

Is MiCA in force in Iceland?

Yes. Act 101/2025 was passed on 24 December 2025 and took effect on 1 January 2026 (Art 16). Art 1 says MiCA applies through EEA Joint Committee Decision 41/2025, which the EEA Joint Committee adopted on 20 February 2025. Art 3 makes the Central Bank the competent authority. Art 16 also reads the dates in MiCA Art 143 as 1 January 2026 and 1 February 2026 in place of 30 December 2024 and 30 July 2024.

ESMA's list of national grandfathering periods, in its May 2026 version, shows 18 months for Iceland, though its layout is ambiguous and it warns that some periods were not yet in national law. MiCA Art 143(3) caps any transition at 1 July 2026, and ESMA's statement of 23 June 2026 says the transitional period ends on that date. We did not confirm what happened to Icelandic virtual asset firms registered under the older AML regime.

On 23 November 2026 the ESMA interim MiCA register lists one entry with Iceland as the home state:

Firm Date in the register
Myntkaup ehf. 25 June 2026

The file does not mark the entry as an Art 63 authorisation or an Art 60 notification. Check the ESMA CASPS file and the Central Bank's register for the current list.

Crypto license in Iceland FAQs

Is Iceland part of MiCA?

Yes. Iceland is in the EEA. EEA Joint Committee Decision 41/2025 incorporated Regulation (EU) 2023/1114, and Act 101/2025 applies it in Icelandic law from 1 January 2026.

Who regulates crypto in Iceland?

The Central Bank of Iceland is the competent authority (Act 101/2025 Art 3).

How much does a crypto license cost in Iceland?

The Central Bank's cost page lists an ISK 1,280,000 processing fee for crypto-asset service providers. Capital is EUR 50,000, 125,000 or 150,000 by class, or a quarter of fixed overheads if higher (MiCA Art 67, Annex IV). Staff, custody, audit and legal work cost extra.

How long does the Central Bank take to decide?

25 working days to check completeness and 40 working days to decide, with up to 20 working days of pause for questions (MiCA Art 63).

How many CASPs are authorised in Iceland?

The ESMA interim register lists one Icelandic entry on 23 November 2026, Myntkaup ehf. Check the registers for the current list.

Can an EMI or investment firm offer crypto services without a full licence?

Yes, through an Art 60 notification at least 40 working days before launch, for services that match the existing licence. An EMI may notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues.

Does an Icelandic CASP licence work across the EEA?

Yes, by notification under MiCA Art 65.

Which language do I file in?

We did not find this. Ask the Central Bank before you prepare translations.
