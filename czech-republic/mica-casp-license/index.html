Crypto License in Czech Republic

A Czech crypto licence is now a MiCA authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider (CASP), granted by the Czech National Bank (ČNB). The old trade licence regime ended on 1 July 2026 at the latest, and nothing converted automatically. By 1 July 2026 the ČNB had assessed 251 applications and authorised 11 firms.

Who needs a crypto license in the Czech Republic?

You need CASP authorisation if you provide crypto-asset services as a business to clients in the Czech Republic or the EU: custody, a trading platform, exchange for funds or other crypto-assets, order execution, placing, order transmission, advice, portfolio management or transfers. Under Act 31/2025 the ČNB is the competent authority (Sec 9).

Other routes can fit better:

  • MiCA Art 60 notification, if you are already a bank, investment firm, EMI or another listed financial entity. An EMI may notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues. The ČNB assessed these notifications alongside applications. The ESMA register shows Partners Banka, a.s. notifying on 29 July 2026.
  • A CASP authorised in another EU member state. The ČNB confirms such firms may serve the Czech market.
  • An EMI licence, if your product is an e-money token. Only credit institutions and EMIs may offer them (MiCA Art 48(1)).

Czech crypto license requirements

Capital

MiCA sets the minimum at EUR 50,000 for class 1 services, EUR 125,000 for class 2 (adding custody and exchange) and EUR 150,000 for class 3 (adding a trading platform). You hold the higher of that amount or a quarter of the previous year's fixed overheads, using projected overheads in your first year (MiCA Art 67(1)-(2), Annex IV). The sources checked show no extra national capital figure.

Management, shareholders and substance

Directors must have good repute, knowledge, skills, experience and enough time, and qualifying shareholders must have good repute (MiCA Art 68(1)-(2)). You need a registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (Art 59(2)). MiCA authorises only a legal person or other undertaking as a CASP (Art 59(1)(a)), so you apply through a company.

The ČNB's 1 July 2026 release lists what went wrong: many applicants had no verifiable track record, used virtual addresses for registered offices or planned to operate mainly abroad, and only a very small share had a realistic and transparent business model, ownership structure and financing.

Policies, ICT and AML

The file covers custody, complaints, conflicts, outsourcing, business continuity, AML/CFT controls and ICT under Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA), as listed in MiCA Art 62(2) and 68. The ČNB says it assesses risk management, internal controls, organisation and capital. It also reminds authorised CASPs to notify changes to the management body before new members act (Art 69).

How to get a crypto license in the Czech Republic

  1. Map your services to MiCA's ten crypto-asset services and fix your capital class.
  2. Form the Czech company and prepare the Art 62(2) file, using the content rules in Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/305 and the forms in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/306.
  3. Submit through the ČNB contact point described in the PDF on its crypto licensing page.
  4. Pay the CZK 20,000 administrative fee.
  5. The ČNB acknowledges within 5 working days and confirms completeness within 25 working days (MiCA Art 63(1)-(2)).
  6. It decides within 40 working days of a complete application. Information requests by the 20th working day can pause the clock for up to 20 working days (Art 63(9), (12)).
  7. The decision becomes final before the ČNB adds you to its lists. ESMA then lists you in its register.

In a July 2025 reply to an information request, the ČNB said it did not offer pre-licence consultations. Its pages show no changed policy. It publishes a recommendations document for applicants on its licensing page. The ČNB said on 11 February 2026 that it follows the binding MiCA deadlines and that they can restart when you submit more information. It gives no typical duration.

After authorisation

The ČNB adds authorised CASPs to its "Lists of regulated institutions and registered financial market entities" after the decision becomes final, and ESMA lists them in its MiCA register. You then notify the ČNB of management changes (MiCA Art 69). To serve other member states you send your home regulator a passport notice, which it forwards within 10 working days. You can start when you receive its communication or on the 15th calendar day at the latest (Art 65). The ČNB can fine you, suspend or ban services or marketing, and order immediate cessation. Acquisitions of qualifying holdings in a CASP follow Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/414.

Status of the Czech crypto market on 23 November 2026

Under Sec 26 of Act 31/2025, a firm that held a trade licence before 30 December 2024 could keep operating only if it applied under MiCA Art 63 by 31 July 2025, and then only until the decision became final and no later than 1 July 2026. A later application gave no cover.

Crypto license in the Czech Republic compared

The MiCA capital classes and the 40-working-day clock are the same in every member state. Fees, language and supervisory practice differ. Compare the MiCA licence in Lithuania, Malta and Estonia. In the Czech Republic you can also read the EMI licence and payment institution licence pages. The EU-wide rules are in our MiCA CASP guide.

Crypto license in Czech Republic FAQs

How much does a Czech crypto license cost?

The ČNB charges CZK 20,000 for the application (Act 634/2004, annex Item 65 point 4(g)). Capital is EUR 50,000, 125,000 or 150,000 by class, or a quarter of fixed overheads if higher. Staff, office, audit, ICT and advisers come on top, and our fee depends on scope: our fee range for a MiCA CASP application in the Czech Republic.

Can I still use a Czech trade licence for crypto services?

No. Under Sec 26 of Act 31/2025 the cover lasted until a decision on a timely MiCA application and no later than 1 July 2026. The ČNB ordered firms still operating without authorisation to stop.

How many firms has the ČNB authorised?

11 domestic CASPs as of the ČNB's 19 October 2026 release. The ESMA register file downloaded on 23 November 2026 shows 12 Czech entries, 11 of them authorisations and 1 an Art 60 notification.

How long does the ČNB take?

MiCA gives 25 working days to confirm completeness and 40 working days to decide (Art 63(2), (9)), and information requests can pause the clock. The ČNB has published no average duration.

Is the Czech Republic an easy place to get a crypto license?

The ČNB assessed 251 applications and authorised 11. It said a significant share were wholly insufficient in content and many showed no verifiable track record.

Can I get a Czech licence with a virtual office?

The ČNB named virtual addresses for registered offices as a weakness in the applications it saw. MiCA also requires effective management in the EU and an EU-resident director (Art 59(2)).

Does a Czech CASP licence work across the EU?

Yes. A passport notice to the ČNB lets you start in another member state on receiving its communication or on the 15th calendar day at the latest (Art 65).

Can Binance or other firms refused elsewhere serve Czech clients?

The ČNB says Binance, which applied through the Greek regulator, did not obtain authorisation before the deadline and must stop crypto-asset services across the EU including the Czech Republic.
