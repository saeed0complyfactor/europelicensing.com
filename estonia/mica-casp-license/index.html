Crypto License in Estonia

A crypto licence in Estonia now means a MiCA authorisation from Finantsinspektsioon (FI), the Estonian financial supervision authority. The old virtual currency licences from the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) stopped counting on 1 July 2026, and FI's page names a EUR 3,000 processing fee for CASP applications. Many websites still describe the FIU licence regime, so check any figure against the dates below.

Who needs a crypto license in Estonia?

Any firm providing crypto-asset services in Estonia on a professional basis needs authorisation from FI or from another EEA supervisor under MiCA. FI's notice of 30 June 2026 says this has applied since 1 July 2026. The services are custody, trading platforms, exchange, execution, placing, order reception, advice, portfolio management and transfers. See the MiCA licence guide for the full list.

Routes that may fit better:

  • Another EEA authorisation. A CASP authorised elsewhere can serve Estonia after the Art 65 notification. The ESMA register lists more than 100 entries that name Estonia as a state of service.
  • Art 60 notification. Credit institutions, EMIs, investment firms and others can notify instead of applying. An EMI may notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues. FI's notice says AS LHV Pank and Lightyear Europe AS can provide crypto-asset services in Estonia.
  • Holders of an old FIU licence. You need a new application. See the section on the FIU licences below.

Shareholders Qualifying shareholders must prove good repute (Art 68(2))

FI's page lists 18 items under Art 62(2) and ESMA's technical standards, including company details, the programme of operations, prudential safeguards, governance, risk and AML controls, ICT, client asset segregation and service-specific policies. FI does not state a share capital figure beyond MiCA, or a local office rule, on that page. Ask FI before you set your Estonian board.

How to get a crypto license in Estonia: process

  1. Map your services. Set your capital class from the service list.
  2. Use the FI portal. From 18 March 2026 FI takes crypto licence applications only through taotlus.fi.ee.
  3. Prepare the file. Follow the 18 items of Art 62(2) and the form in Annex VI of the ESMA technical standards. Submit manager details through FI's fit and proper form.
  4. Language. File in Estonian. English documents are allowed if the application says so.
  5. Pay the processing fee. FI's page names EUR 3,000 for a CASP application.
  6. Completeness. FI checks the file within 25 working days.
  7. Decision. FI assesses it within 40 working days from a complete file. If FI has questions, the clock can pause for up to 20 working days. FI informs you of the decision within five working days.
  8. Passport. Notify FI before you serve another state. There is no processing fee for the notification.

The statutory periods cover FI's decision. Preparing the file and answering questions come on top of them.

After authorisation

ESMA lists you in its register, and FI supervises you. You report to FI, pay its fees and notify it of changes. Passport notifications go through FI (Art 65), with no FI processing fee.

Estonia crypto license: what happened to the FIU licences?

Before MiCA, the FIU issued virtual currency service licences under Estonian anti-money laundering law. These are the dated facts from the FIU and FI notices of 30 June 2026.

Date Event Source
Mid-2021 641 valid FIU virtual currency licences when the FIU began clearing the sector FI
Start of 2026 36 valid licences Same
30 June 2026 FI and FIU announce the end of the transition Same; FIU
1 July 2026 FIU cancels the register data of the licences. Only firms authorised by FI or another EEA supervisor under MiCA may provide crypto-asset services in Estonia Same
20 July 2026 Deadline for firms that provided virtual currency services in Q2, including those whose licences were cancelled, to report through the Eesti Pank portal Same

The FIU licences were not converted into MiCA authorisations. FI's notice says firms without authorisation cannot accept new clients, open accounts or actively market to clients in the EEA, and that applicants awaiting a decision must limit their activity until FI decides.

ESMA's interim MiCA register, read on 23 November 2026, lists three entries with Estonia as the home state: Lightyear Europe AS, AS LHV Pank and Lightspark Payments Europe AS. FI's notice of 30 June 2026 names Lightspark Payments Europe AS as the only CASP FI had authorised at that date. We did not find an FI statement dated after 1 July 2026 with a new count, so treat the register as the current source.

Estonia vs Lithuania crypto license

Both countries are EEA states, so a CASP authorised in either one can serve the other by notification. The differences you can verify from official sources:

Point Estonia Lithuania
Old regime FIU virtual currency licences: 641 in mid-2021, 36 at the start of 2026, cancelled 1 July 2026 See the Lithuania page
ESMA register entries, home state, 23 November 2026 3 7 (4 Article 63 authorisations, 3 Article 60 notifications)
Regulator fee for a CASP application EUR 3,000 (FI) See the Lithuania page
Application language Estonian; English documents allowed if the application says so See the Lithuania page
Decision clock MiCA Art 63: 25 and 40 working days Same

The statutory decision periods are the same in both countries. Fees and language differ. A Polish firm that needs a home state can use either, as the Poland crypto page explains. For other options, see Malta and Cyprus. In Estonia itself, the sibling licences are the EMI licence and the payment institution licence.

Crypto license in Estonia FAQs

How much does a crypto license cost in Estonia?

FI's page names a EUR 3,000 processing fee for a CASP application. Capital is EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000 by class, or a quarter of fixed overheads if higher (MiCA Art 67). Other costs include the Estonian company, staff, audit and legal work. We found no published FI supervision fee.

Is my old Estonian FIU crypto licence still valid?

No. The FIU cancelled the register data on 1 July 2026, and only firms authorised under MiCA may provide crypto-asset services in Estonia.

How long does the Estonian crypto license take?

FI checks completeness within 25 working days and assesses within 40 working days of a complete file. The clock can pause for up to 20 working days if FI asks questions.

Which regulator grants a crypto license in Estonia?

Finantsinspektsioon. Applications go through taotlus.fi.ee.

Which language do I apply in?

Estonian. English documents are allowed if the application says so.

How many crypto firms are authorised in Estonia?

ESMA's register lists three entries with Estonia as the home state on 23 November 2026: Lightyear Europe AS, AS LHV Pank and Lightspark Payments Europe AS. Only Lightspark was FI's own CASP authorisation at the end of June 2026.

Is Estonia or Lithuania better for a crypto licence?

The MiCA clocks are the same. The ESMA register lists 3 Estonian and 7 Lithuanian home-state entries, and the fees and language differ. Compare them against your product and team.

Can I serve Estonian clients with a licence from another EU country?

Yes, after your home regulator forwards the Art 65 notification. The ESMA register names Estonia as a state of service for more than 100 entries.
