Payment Institution License in Spain

A payment institution (entidad de pago) licence from the Banco de España lets a Spanish company provide payment services and passport them across the EU and EEA. The capital depends on the services, from EUR 20,000 to EUR 125,000, and the Banco de España charges no application fee. Spain also has a lighter low-volume registration, but it comes with no passport and no payment initiation or account information services.

Who needs a payment institution license in Spain?

You need authorisation if you provide payment services on a professional basis in Spain and are not a bank or another type of authorised provider. The Banco de España lists money transfer operators, firms that offer payment accounts, issue or acquire card transactions, initiate payments or aggregate account information (guide 1.2).

Other routes can fit better.

  • Low-volume registration. If your projected average payment volume over the first twelve months stays at or below EUR 3 million a month, you can register under RDL 19/2018 Art 14. The conditions: no payment initiation or account information services, central administration or residence in Spain, no use of the EU passport, and a clean record for the managers (guide section 4). The decision period is 20 days, and silence counts as approval (guide 1.8).
  • Account information only. You register, rather than get a licence. The decision period is 3 months (guide 1.8).
  • You hold stored value for customers. The EMI licence in Spain covers payment services too.
  • You are already authorised in another EEA state. Passport into Spain.
  • You are a crypto-asset service provider that transfers or holds e-money tokens. The CNMV Q&A says that, under the EBA no-action letter of 10 June 2025, you need a PSD2 authorisation from the Banco de España or a partnership with an authorised firm (CNMV FAQ 1.1). The transitional period for that ended on 1 March 2026.

Payment institution license requirements in Spain

Capital

Services Initial capital
Money remittance only EUR 20,000
Payment initiation only EUR 50,000
Any service in Art 1.2(a) to (e) of RDL 19/2018, with or without others EUR 125,000

The Banco de España stresses that initial capital is not the same as share capital (guide 2.5). Own funds must stay at or above the higher of the initial capital and the amount from the method set for you under PSD2 Art 9, which the Banco de España applies (guide 2.3). Payment initiation and account information providers also hold professional indemnity insurance or a comparable guarantee (PSD2 Art 5(2), (3)).

Substance in Spain

The registered office, central administration and actual management must be in Spain (guide 2.1). You enter the Banco de España's Special Registry before you start, and an unused authorisation can be revoked after 12 months (guide 1.10).

Fit and proper and owners

Managers, directors and qualifying shareholders file the documents listed in the Payment Institution Authorisation Form, which follows EBA/GL/2017/09 and RD 736/2019 Art 2 (guide section 2). The SEPBLAC Executive Service reports on your AML framework (guide 1.7).

Safeguarding

For services 1 to 6, you segregate user funds in a separate account at a credit institution or central bank, or cover them with insurance or a guarantee (PSD2 Art 10(1)).

Governance, ICT and AML

The file includes governance, internal controls, incident handling, business continuity and ICT arrangements under DORA (PSD2 Art 5(1)). Your AML manual and framework contract must be in Spanish (guide 1.5). See our AML and compliance programme.

How to apply for a payment institution license in Spain

  1. Map your services to Art 1.2 of RDL 19/2018 and decide between full authorisation, account information registration and low-volume registration.
  2. Ask for a meeting before filing, if you want one, using the pre-application form in the "Tramitación" section of the Banco de España's payment institution page (guide 1.3).
  3. Prepare the Payment Institution Authorisation Form with its annexes. Spanish is required for the application, by-laws, framework contract and terms, ombudsman rules and AML manual. Other documents can be in English (guide 1.5).
  4. File through the Electronic Registry, in person at calle Alcalá 48, at a branch, or by post (guide 1.4).
  5. Answer requests for information. The 3-month clock starts when the application is complete, can be suspended while you correct gaps, and for mandatory reports for up to 3 months (guide 1.8).
  6. If approved, meet any conditions, finish the Mercantile Register steps and apply for Special Registry entry (guide 1.9, 1.10).

Payment institution license cost in Spain

The application and procedure are free, and no deposit is needed (guide 1.4).

After the Banco de España grants the license

  • Special Registry entry before you start (guide 1.10).
  • Reporting. Notify the Banco de España of any change that affects the information you filed, without delay (guide 1.5).
  • Passport. Authorised institutions notify the Banco de España of states, services, agents and branches. The home regulator forwards within 1 month and the host assesses within 1 month (PSD2 Art 28(2)).
  • Low-volume registrants cannot use the passport. If you grow, you need full authorisation (PSD2 Art 32(5) gives 30 calendar days to apply once the conditions are breached).
  • Change of control. Confirm the notification route with the Banco de España before share transfers.

Spain compared with other countries

For stored value, see the EMI licence in Spain. Crypto services go to the CNMV, covered in the MiCA crypto licence in Spain. For the same licence elsewhere, compare the payment institution licence in Lithuania, Ireland and Bulgaria. The EU baseline is on the payment institution licence page.

Payment institution license in Spain FAQs

How do I get a payment institution license in Spain?

You set up a Spanish company, fill in the Banco de España's payment institution authorisation form, file it electronically or in person, answer its questions within the 3-month clock, then enter the Special Registry before starting (guide 1.4 to 1.10).

How much capital does a payment institution need in Spain?

EUR 20,000 for money remittance only, EUR 50,000 for payment initiation only, and EUR 125,000 if you provide any of the services in Art 1.2(a) to (e) of RDL 19/2018 (guide 2.5).

How much does a payment institution license cost in Spain?

The Banco de España charges no application fee (guide 1.4). The costs are capital, a local entity, staff and advisers. our fee range for payment institution licence in Spain

How long does the Banco de España take to decide?

Three months from a complete application, with suspensions for corrections and reports. If it passes without an express decision, the application is deemed denied (guide 1.8).

What is the low-volume payment institution in Spain?

A registration under RDL 19/2018 Art 14 for firms whose projected payment volume averages no more than EUR 3 million a month over the first twelve months. It excludes payment initiation and account information, and it gives no EU passport. The decision period is 20 days (guide section 4, 1.8).

Can a foreign company apply for a Spanish payment institution?

It must establish a Spanish company with its registered office, central administration and actual management in Spain (guide 2.1).

Do I need to apply in Spanish?

The application, by-laws, framework contract and terms, ombudsman rules and AML manual are in Spanish. The rest may be in English (guide 1.5).

Can a Spanish payment institution passport into other EU countries?

Yes, if it is fully authorised. Low-volume registrants cannot (guide section 4; PSD2 Art 28).
