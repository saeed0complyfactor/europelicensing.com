Payment Institution License in the UK

A payment institution (PI) provides payment services such as money remittance, payment accounts or payment initiation under the Payment Services Regulations 2017 (PSRs 2017). The Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) authorises or registers you. You choose between three statuses: small PI, authorised PI, or registered account information service provider (RAISP).

Which UK payment institution status fits?

Status Fits when Capital
Small payment institution (SPI) Monthly average payment transactions up to EUR 3m over the previous 12 months, and no account information or payment initiation services (reg 14) No figure in Schedule 3, which covers authorised PIs
Authorised payment institution (API) Volumes above that, or you offer payment initiation EUR 20,000, EUR 50,000 or EUR 125,000 by service
Registered AISP (RAISP) Account information is your only service Professional indemnity insurance or comparable guarantee (reg 18)
Agent of another UK PI or EMI You act for a firm that already holds the licence None of your own; the principal registers you

A new firm can use projected volumes to test the small PI limit. If your product holds balances that customers can redeem as e-money, look at the UK EMI licence instead. A firm that offers only account information registers as a RAISP and does not need a PI licence.

FCA payment institution requirements

Capital

An authorised PI's initial capital depends on the services: EUR 20,000 for money remittance, EUR 50,000 for payment initiation and EUR 125,000 for the services in Schedule 1 paragraph 1(a) to (e). If you provide several, the greater amount applies (PSRs Sch 3 Part 1).

People and governance

The FCA asks for forms for directors, managers and controllers, evidence that the people involved know payment services, and a main contact who works at the firm. Policies must be written for your business.

Safeguarding

An authorised PI must safeguard relevant funds by segregating them, or by an insurance policy or comparable guarantee (PSRs reg 23). A small PI may opt in. The FCA's supplementary safeguarding regime (PS25/12) has applied since 7 May 2026, and the FCA's application page lists CASS 15 among the safeguarding rules to read. PS25/12 says a CASS-style end state needs further legislation, so read the current text of each rule before you design the account structure.

Agents

An authorised PI, small PI or RAISP may provide payment services through an agent only if the agent is on the FCA register. The application names the agent, its directors and managers, and the services it will provide, and the FCA must decide a complete application within 2 months (PSRs reg 34(1), (3), (11)). The PI must make sure the agent tells customers about the agency arrangement (reg 34(16)).

AML

You need AML controls under the Money Laundering Regulations 2017, a nominated officer and risk-based procedures.

How to apply for a payment institution license in the UK

  1. Choose small PI, authorised PI or RAISP and confirm capital or insurance.
  2. Request a pre-application meeting through the FCA's PASS service, open to PI, EMI and RAISP applicants.
  3. Register on Connect and submit the application there.
  4. Pay the non-refundable fee. A wrong fee means rejection without assessment.
  5. The FCA allocates a case officer within a maximum of 20 working days.
  6. Answer the case officer's questions.
  7. Decision within 3 months of a complete application, or within 12 months of receipt of an incomplete one (PSRs reg 9).

The FCA lists what to submit on its application page. The FCA pages do not state an application language.

After authorisation

  • Register entry on the Financial Services Register.
  • Agent registration before any agent acts, and prompt notice of changes.
  • Safeguarding and reporting under the rules in force since 7 May 2026.
  • Periodic FCA fees under the annual fee schedule.
  • Change of control approvals for qualifying holdings.
  • No passport: serving EEA clients needs an EU or EEA licence.

UK payment institution compared with other options

For EEA access under PSD2 see the payment institution licence in Lithuania or in Ireland. In the UK, the sibling pages are the EMI licence and FCA crypto authorisation. The overview is FCA authorisation.

Payment institution in the UK FAQs

What is a small payment institution?

A firm whose monthly average payment transactions over the previous 12 months are up to EUR 3 million and that offers no account information or payment initiation services (PSRs reg 14).

What is an authorised payment institution?

A PI authorised by the FCA under the PSRs 2017 with the initial capital for its services: EUR 20,000, 50,000 or 125,000.

How much does an FCA payment institution application cost?

GBP 1,130 for a small PI, GBP 2,820 or GBP 5,640 for an authorised PI depending on the services, per the fee page updated on 19 November 2026.

How long does the FCA take to decide?

3 months from a complete application and up to 12 months from receipt of an incomplete one (PSRs reg 9).

Can a UK PI passport into the EU?

No. Passporting ended on 31 December 2020.

Can I use agents?

Yes, once each agent is on the FCA register (PSRs reg 34).

Do small PIs have to safeguard?

Safeguarding is mandatory for authorised PIs. Small PIs may opt in (PSRs reg 23).

Do I need a PI licence for account information only?

No. You need RAISP registration, with professional indemnity insurance or a comparable guarantee (PSRs reg 18).
