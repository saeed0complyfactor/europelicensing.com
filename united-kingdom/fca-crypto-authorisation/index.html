Crypto License in the UK

The UK sits outside MiCA, and an EU CASP authorisation gives no right to serve UK customers. Cryptoasset firms are moving from registration under the Money Laundering Regulations 2017 (MLRs) to authorisation by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (FSMA). The FCA expects the new regime to start on 25 October 2027, and the application window closes on 28 February 2027.

Do you need FCA crypto authorisation?

Today, a cryptoasset exchange provider or custodian wallet provider must be on the FCA register under the MLRs before it acts (MLRs reg 56(1)(f), (g)). From 25 October 2027, a firm that carries on any of the new cryptoasset regulated activities needs FSMA authorisation.

Which route applies depends on what you already hold:

  • New firm with no FCA status: apply for authorisation through Connect.
  • Existing FSMA firm: apply to vary your permissions.
  • EMI or payment institution: the FCA states you also need FSMA authorisation for these activities.
  • MLR-registered firm: the FCA does not convert registrations, and being registered does not guarantee authorisation.
  • Firm that does not apply: it must run off its UK cryptoasset business before the regime starts.

A MiCA authorisation from an EU regulator is a different licence. For EU access see the MiCA CASP licence in Lithuania or in Ireland.

FCA cryptoasset authorisation requirements

The FCA tells firms to run a gap analysis against the FSMA requirements, agree a plan at board level and apply early in the window (what you need to do). FSMA authorisation covers the usual Part 4A areas: threshold conditions, fit and proper individuals, financial resources, systems and controls, and AML.

The application asks for permissions matched to your activities. The fee categories below show how the FCA groups them:

Activities in the fee schedule Category Fee
Arranging with a view to transactions, arranging deals, dealing as agent, arranging staking 4 GBP 2,820
Dealing as principal, safeguarding or arranging safeguarding, issuing a qualifying stablecoin 6 GBP 11,260
Operating a qualifying cryptoasset trading platform 7 GBP 28,150

A firm that applies for several activities pays the highest applicable fee.

How to apply for a UK crypto license

  1. Run the gap analysis and agree a plan at board level.
  2. Decide whether you need authorisation or a variation of existing permissions.
  3. Optional: a pre-application meeting. The FCA calls these optional and free of charge.
  4. Submit through Connect during the window, 30 September 2026 to 28 February 2027.
  5. Pay the fee for your highest-category activity.
  6. Answer FCA questions.
  7. Decision. FSMA section 55V sets 6 months for a complete application and 12 months for an incomplete one. For in-window applications the FCA says it expects to decide before the regime commences.

Firms that apply after the window but before commencement enter the transitional provision by operation of law if they are not yet authorised when the regime starts. The FCA says it will not expedite assessment to make up for a late submission.

UK crypto authorisation compared with MiCA

MiCA gives EEA passporting, and the UK regime does not. See the MiCA CASP licence in Lithuania and in Ireland. The UK sibling pages are the EMI licence and the payment institution licence. The overview is FCA authorisation.

Crypto license in the UK FAQs

How much does a UK crypto license cost?

FCA application fees are GBP 2,820, GBP 11,260 or GBP 28,150 depending on the highest-category activity, plus your own setup, staff and adviser costs. Our fee: our fee range for a crypto license in the UK.

Do I need a crypto license in the UK?

A cryptoasset exchange provider or custodian wallet provider needs MLR registration today (MLRs reg 56), and any firm needs FSMA authorisation for the new regulated activities from 25 October 2027.

What is the deadline to apply?

The application window closes on 28 February 2027. Firms that apply later but before commencement enter the transitional provision, though the FCA will not expedite their assessment.

Do I need a license for a crypto exchange in the UK?

Operating a qualifying cryptoasset trading platform is a Category 7 activity at GBP 28,150 in the fee schedule.

Does a MiCA license work in the UK?

No. The UK is outside MiCA and EEA passporting.

Is my MLR registration enough?

No. The FCA does not convert registrations, and registration does not guarantee authorisation.

How long does a decision take?

Section 55V of FSMA sets 6 months for a complete application and 12 months for an incomplete one. The FCA says it expects to decide in-window applications before the regime is due to start on 25 October 2027.

Can an EMI or payment institution offer crypto?

Not on that authorisation alone. The FCA states these firms also need FSMA authorisation.
