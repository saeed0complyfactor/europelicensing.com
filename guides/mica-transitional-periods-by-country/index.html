MiCA Transitional Periods by Country (Tracker)

MiCA let member states keep a national regime for existing crypto firms until 1 July 2026 at the latest (Art 143(3)). States could shorten it, and ten of the 30 EU and EEA states ended it in 2025: the dates below run from 30 June 2025 to 1 July 2026.

Written by Jan Hagen, regulatory compliance expert. Jan has supported authorisations with the FCA and DNB and has held senior compliance and MLRO roles.

Last checked against official sources: 23 November 2026

MiCA transitional periods in short

Fact Detail Source
EU-wide limit A national transitional regime could run until 1 July 2026 at the latest, or until the authority granted or refused authorisation, whichever came first MiCA Art 143(3)
Who could shorten it Each member state, and the EEA states through their own acts MiCA Art 143(3)
Status today The transitional period has ended in every state we cover. ESMA's statement says unauthorised CASPs must stop onboarding EU clients and wind down ESMA, 23 June 2026

How to read the dates

  • Two dates appear for the same country in some sources. Belgium's FSMA press release gave firms "until 30 June 2026 at the latest", while its CASP page says "until 1 July 2026", so we show 30 June as the safe date. For Italy we follow the joint release of 30 June 2026, which says the period ends from 1 July. Consob Communication 16/25 reportedly allowed activity no later than 30 June 2026, but we could not read it directly.
  • "At the latest" means the national text also ended the cover on an earlier decision under Art 63, as in Austria and Germany.
  • Dates marked as our calculation or not confirmed are the limits of what the official sources say. Denmark's Finanstilsynet gives 18 months and no calendar date, and for Iceland we did not confirm what happened to firms registered under the older AML regime.
  • ESMA's list of national periods is a guide only. The list itself warns that some periods were communicated as expectations and may not be in national law, so use the national source in the table.

What this means for you

  • If you ran a crypto business under an old national registration, that registration does not count as authorisation today. Authorities such as the Central Bank of Ireland and the Banco de Portugal say prior registration gives no simplified route and does not exempt a firm from the MiCA procedure.
  • A firm that is not authorised cannot rely on the old cover to onboard clients. The ESMA statement of 23 June 2026 says unauthorised CASPs must stop onboarding EU clients and wind down.
  • Pick a home state whose authority can accept your file. Poland and Romania are special cases: the UKNF says no Polish authority is designated for CASPs, and we found no act designating Romania's ASF. See the Poland page and the Romania page, and ask the authority in writing.
  • Check the register count on the authorised CASPs tracker before you rely on a state's practice, and read what became of the old registers in the VASP register guide.
  • For the full process, see the MiCA CASP licence guide.

MiCA transitional period FAQs

When did the MiCA transitional period end?

MiCA Art 143(3) allowed a national regime until 1 July 2026 at the latest. National end dates ranged from 30 June 2025 to 1 July 2026, as the table shows.

Which countries had the shortest MiCA transition?

Finland, Latvia and the Netherlands ended it on 30 June 2025, and Hungary and Slovenia on 1 July 2025. ESMA's list records 6 months for Finland, the Netherlands and Slovenia.

Can a firm with a pending application keep operating?

Only where the national rule allowed it and only until the decision or the end date. The ESMA statement says unauthorised CASPs must stop onboarding EU clients. Estonia's FI also says applicants awaiting a decision must limit their activities.

Does an old VASP registration still count?

No, not as a MiCA authorisation. See the VASP register guide for what each state did with its register.

Does MiCA apply in Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein?

Yes, through the EEA Agreement. See MiCA in the EEA.

What about the UK?

The UK is outside MiCA. The FCA expects its own cryptoasset regime to start on 25 October 2027. See the UK crypto page.

Where do I check the current position?

On the national regulator's page and on the ESMA interim MiCA register. The country pages link both.
