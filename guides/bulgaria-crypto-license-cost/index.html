Bulgaria Crypto License Cost in 2026

The Financial Supervision Commission (FSC) charges EUR 5,112.92, EUR 10,225.84 or EUR 30,677.51 for a class 1, 2 or 3 MiCA licence, then an annual supervision fee that has a fixed part per service and a variable part of 0.03% of annual revenue. On top of that you need EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000 in capital, a Bulgarian company and Bulgarian-language documents.

FSC fees for a Bulgarian crypto license

Ordinance No 76 sets the fees. The version we read is amended up to State Gazette 65 of 17 July 2026.

Fee item Amount Basis
CASP licence, class 1 EUR 5,112.92 Art 16 point 1
CASP licence, class 2 EUR 10,225.84 Art 16 point 2
CASP licence, class 3 EUR 30,677.51 Art 16 point 3
Extending a licence from class 1 to 2 EUR 6,135.50 Art 16 point 4
Extending a licence from class 2 to 3 EUR 18,917.80 Art 16 point 5
Other permissions and approvals under MiCA EUR 2,556.46 Art 16 point 19
Acquiring a qualifying holding below 50% EUR 1,500 per direct acquirer, EUR 800 per indirect Art 16 point 12
Acquiring 50% or more EUR 2,900 per direct acquirer, EUR 800 per indirect Art 16 point 13
Art 60 notification by an already-licensed firm The class fee, by the services you notify Art 16 point 15
Annual supervision, fixed part EUR 204.52 (reception and transmission of orders) to EUR 6,135.50 (trading platform); EUR 5,112.92 for transfer; EUR 409.03 for custody; added up for each service in your licence Art 43(1)
Annual supervision, variable part 0.03% of total annual revenue Art 43(6)

Class 1 covers order execution, placing, reception and transmission of orders, advice, portfolio management and transfer. Class 2 adds custody and exchange. Class 3 adds a trading platform. The FSC uses these three classes in its licences and its fee table.

Is the FSC fee EUR 2,556 or EUR 5,112.92?

Both figures circulate on vendor sites. In the FSC's own ordinance, EUR 5,112.92 is the class 1 licence fee and EUR 2,556.46 is the fee for other permissions and approvals under MiCA. We found no official EUR 2,556 figure for a licence. We have not verified why vendors quote one or the other.

Worked examples: FSC fees for three business types

These sums add the licence fee to one year of the fixed supervision part, from the table above. They exclude the 0.03% revenue element, which depends on your turnover, and they exclude capital and every other cost. Ask the FSC in which year the first annual fee falls due.

Business Licence fee Fixed annual part Total
Order reception and transmission only (class 1) EUR 5,112.92 EUR 204.52 EUR 5,317.44
Custody only (class 2) EUR 10,225.84 EUR 409.03 EUR 10,634.87
Trading platform (class 3) EUR 30,677.51 EUR 6,135.50 EUR 36,813.01

For scale, 0.03% of EUR 1,000,000 of annual revenue is EUR 300. The figure only illustrates the percentage.

A business with several services adds the fixed amount for each one. The licence fee depends on the highest class your services require.

Capital and other costs beyond the FSC fee

Capital stays in your company. Under MiCA Art 67 you hold the higher of the class minimum and one quarter of the preceding year's fixed overheads, using a forecast if you are under a year old. It can be own funds or insurance (Art 67(4)).

Class Minimum capital FSC licence fee
1 EUR 50,000 EUR 5,112.92
2 EUR 125,000 EUR 10,225.84
3 EUR 150,000 EUR 30,677.51

The FSC FAQ sets Bulgarian requirements that add cost beyond the fee. It says a CASP must have its registered office in Bulgaria. It describes at least one executive officer who resides in Bulgaria and is fully dedicated to the business, part of the staff working in Bulgaria and part of the clients being Bulgarian persons (FAQ Q7). The application, documents and declarations go in Bulgarian, and declarations signed by foreigners are bilingual. Ask the FSC how it applies these points to your structure before you commit to a team.

Other cost lines, with no amounts here because they depend on your model: the Bulgarian company, the executive officer and staff, translation into Bulgarian, audit, custody and safeguarding arrangements, professional indemnity where relevant, AML and ICT systems, and advisers. our fee range for CASP licence in Bulgaria

What the comparison shows:

  • Romania has no route today, so a Romanian-based founder applies in another state and passports back. See the Romania crypto page.
  • We found no published HCMC fee or fee schedule for CASP applications, and we could not open the HCMC's site, so ask the HCMC before you budget. See the Greece crypto page.
  • Croatia prices by service. A custody-only firm pays EUR 900 in service fees, plus EUR 1,400 for each management board member's consent. A trading platform pays EUR 10,200. The cap is EUR 12,700. See the Croatia crypto page.
  • Bulgaria's class 1 fee of EUR 5,112.92 is higher than the Croatian fee for any single class 1 service, and lower than Croatia's cap. Its class 3 fee of EUR 30,677.51 is more than twice the Croatian cap.

Capital is the same in all four, because MiCA sets it. For the countries beyond this region, see the cheapest country guide and the MiCA license cost guide.

Is Bulgaria still a cheap place to get a crypto license?

It depends on your services. Bulgaria's class 1 fee of EUR 5,112.92 is above Croatia's fee for any single class 1 service and below Malta's EUR 10,000 class 1 fee. The annual fee has a fixed part and a revenue part, and Bulgaria wants a local office, a resident executive officer and documents in Bulgarian, which all add running cost.

  • For a class 1 firm with few services, the FSC licence fee is about a tenth of the EUR 50,000 minimum capital.
  • For a class 3 trading platform, the licence fee is EUR 30,677.51. Croatia charges EUR 10,200 for the trading-platform service, with all service fees capped at EUR 12,700. Malta charges EUR 25,000 for class 3, and Cyprus EUR 30,000 for the trading platform before its other per-service fees (see the Malta and Cyprus crypto pages).
  • For a firm that wants to run from outside Bulgaria with a thin local team, the FSC FAQ's local requirements need discussion before you apply.

Status on 23 November 2026:

  • The MiCA transitional period for Bulgarian VASPs ended on 1 July 2026. An entry in the old register no longer authorises activity.
  • The FSC asked applicants to file by 16 February 2026, and said preliminary meetings with new companies would stop after that date. Confirm with the FSC whether it still takes them.
  • The ESMA interim MiCA register lists five entities with Bulgaria as home state: four CASPs authorised under Art 63 (Belayer OOD, Altcoins BG EOOD, Digital Assist OOD and FF Digital EOOD) and one Art 60 notification (Alaric Securities OOD). The earliest entry is dated 22 December 2025.

With four authorised firms, a new applicant has little precedent to go on, and the register does not show how long any firm waited.

What this means for you

  1. Set your class first. It fixes the FSC licence fee and the capital.
  2. List your services, because the annual fixed part adds up for each one.
  3. Budget for Bulgarian-language documents and a Bulgarian company and officer, using the FSC FAQ as your checklist.
  4. Compare the same services against Croatia, Greece and other states in the MiCA license cost guide.
  5. Check the current ordinance text on fsc.bg before you pay.

Bulgaria crypto license cost: FAQs

How much does a crypto license in Bulgaria cost?

The FSC charges EUR 5,112.92, EUR 10,225.84 or EUR 30,677.51 for a class 1, 2 or 3 licence (Ordinance 76 Art 16). You also need capital of EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000 and pay an annual supervision fee. our fee range for CASP licence in Bulgaria

What is the annual FSC fee for a crypto firm?

It has a fixed part, which adds up the amount for each service in your licence (EUR 204.52 to EUR 6,135.50, with EUR 5,112.92 for transfer and EUR 409.03 for custody), plus 0.03% of total annual revenue (Ordinance 76 Art 43).

Who needs a crypto license in Bulgaria?

Anyone providing MiCA crypto-asset services as a business who is not an authorised CASP elsewhere in the EU or a listed financial firm using the Art 60 notification. An e-money token issuer deals with the BNB, not the FSC.

Do I need an office in Bulgaria?

The FSC FAQ says the registered office must be in Bulgaria, and it describes an executive officer residing in Bulgaria, part of the staff working there and part of the clients being Bulgarian. Check the current position with the FSC.

How long does the FSC take to decide?

MiCA Art 63 gives 25 working days for the completeness check and 40 working days to decide on a complete application. Preparation and information requests add time.

Is the Bulgarian crypto license valid in other EU countries?

Yes. You notify the FSC, which forwards the notice within 10 working days, and you can start in the host state on receipt of the communication or on the 15th calendar day (MiCA Art 65).

Can I operate in Bulgaria with a licence from another EU state?

Yes, after your home regulator forwards your Art 65 notification. You do not need an FSC licence.
