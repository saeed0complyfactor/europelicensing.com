Authorised CASPs by Country (Tracker)

ESMA's interim MiCA register lists crypto-asset service providers (CASPs) by home state, and the counts on 23 November 2026 range from none in Poland and Romania to 131 in Germany. The counts mix full Art 63 authorisations with Art 60 notifications from banks and other regulated firms, so read each figure with its caveat.

Written by Jan Hagen, regulatory compliance expert. Jan has supported authorisations with the FCA and DNB and has held senior compliance and MLRO roles.

Article 63 authorisation against Article 60 notification

An Art 63 entry means the home regulator authorised the firm after a full application, with the 25 and 40 working day clocks in Art 63. An Art 60 entry means a bank, investment firm, EMI or similar regulated entity notified its regulator that it will provide crypto-asset services that match its existing licence. An EMI is limited to custody and transfer for the e-money tokens it issues (Art 60(4)), and a payment institution has no Art 60 route.

Keep the split in mind when you compare states:

  • Germany shows 131 entries, but 123 are Art 60 notifications and 8 are Art 63 authorisations.
  • Spain's 17 include banks that notified under Art 60, such as BBVA and CaixaBank.
  • Belgium shows two banks and no firm authorised by the FSMA.
  • Where the legal basis column is blank, as for Croatia, Luxembourg, Slovenia, Latvia and Belgium, we cannot split the two types.
  • The Estonian register shows three entries, but FI named only Lightspark as its own CASP authorisation on 30 June 2026. FI's notice says AS LHV Pank and Lightyear Europe AS can also provide crypto-asset services in Estonia.

Where the file gives a split, the Art 63 counts are: Austria 13, the Netherlands 22, the Czech Republic 11, Slovakia 6, Liechtenstein 6, Bulgaria 4, Lithuania 4 and Germany 8.

Why the counts differ from regulator figures

  • Dates differ. The AMF counted 31 French actors on 6 July 2026, and the register file of 23 November 2026 lists 36. Consob and the Banca d'Italia counted 9 on 30 June 2026 against 10 on the register. The NBS counted 6 licensed Slovak CASPs on 29 July 2026 against 7 entries, one of them a notification.
  • Methods differ. Austria and Germany were counted from an automated read of the file, and Malta is counted as 24 distinct entities.
  • The register is updated weekly, and each regulator updates it on its own schedule. Latvijas Banka's own count reached ten on 8 July 2026, and the register dates match that sequence.
  • ESMA's register is an interim one. Check the live file before you quote a number.

What this means for you

  • A high count shows that the authority has processed applications, but it does not show how fast it works or how it treats your business model.
  • A low count means the authority has handled few files. Denmark has refused at least one application, and the UK FCA had received no crypto authorisation applications under its new regime in the data to 13 November 2026.
  • The home-state count does not limit where a firm serves. A CASP authorised in one state can serve the others by notification under Art 65, and the ESMA register lists more than 100 entries that can serve Estonia or Poland.
  • Compare the transition dates in the transitional periods tracker and the fee and clock details on each country page before you pick a home state.

Authorised CASPs FAQs

How many CASPs are authorised in the EU?

ESMA's interim register lists about 400 entries in total, with the home-state counts in the table. Many are Art 60 notifications from banks and other regulated firms, so the count of full authorisations is lower.

Which country has the most authorised CASPs?

Germany has the most entries, 131, but 123 of them are Art 60 notifications. Among states that show a split, the Netherlands has 22 Art 63 authorisations.

Where is the ESMA MiCA register?

ESMA publishes the interim MiCA register as CSV files, including the CASPS file, updated weekly.

What is the difference between Art 63 and Art 60?

Art 63 covers authorisation on application. Art 60 covers notification by already-regulated firms, such as banks and investment firms, for services that match their licence. An EMI may notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues, and a payment institution cannot use Art 60.

Does a country's count include firms from other states?

No. The counts here are for entries with that country as home member state. The register separately lists states where services may be provided.

Are there authorised CASPs in the UK?

The UK is outside MiCA. The FCA's new cryptoasset regime is expected to start on 25 October 2027, and the FCA reported no authorisation applications in its data to 13 November 2026.

Why does my figure differ from a regulator's press release?

The press release is dated, and the register moves weekly. For example the AMF counted 31 on 6 July 2026 and the register lists 36 on 23 November 2026.
