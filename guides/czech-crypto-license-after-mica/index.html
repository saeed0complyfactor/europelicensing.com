Czech Crypto License After MiCA

A Czech crypto licence is now a MiCA authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider (CASP), granted by the Czech National Bank (ČNB). The old trade-licence regime ended on 1 July 2026 at the latest, and by then the ČNB had assessed 251 applications and authorised 11 firms. The application fee is CZK 20,000.

What happened to the Czech trade licence for crypto?

Before MiCA, Czech crypto firms worked under trade licences. Act 31/2025 let a firm that held one before 30 December 2024 keep operating only if it applied under MiCA Art 63 by 31 July 2025. Cover then lasted until the ČNB's decision became final and no later than 1 July 2026. A later application gave no cover, and nothing converted automatically.

The ČNB's 1 July 2026 release says firms without authorisation must stop onboarding clients and wind down. On 19 October 2026 it said firms that kept operating were ordered to stop and that it has enforcement powers. It also said that Binance, which applied through the Greek regulator, must stop crypto-asset services across the EU, including in the Czech Republic.

Eleven authorisations out of 251 is about 4%. The 251 mixes applications with notifications from existing financial firms, so read the ratio as a rough guide.

Why did most Czech applications fail?

In its 1 July 2026 release the ČNB said a significant share of the applications were wholly insufficient in content. Many applicants had no verifiable track record, used virtual addresses for registered offices or planned to operate mainly abroad. Only a very small share had a realistic and transparent business model, ownership structure and financing.

The ČNB says it assesses risk management, internal controls, organisation and capital (release of 11 February 2026). In July 2025 the ČNB also said it did not offer pre-licence consultations. Its pages show no changed policy, and it publishes a recommendations document for applicants on its licensing page.

What does a Czech CASP application need?

You apply through a company, because MiCA authorises only a legal person or other undertaking as a CASP (Art 59(1)(a)). You need a registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (Art 59(2)). Directors need good repute, knowledge, skills, experience and enough time, and qualifying shareholders need good repute (Art 68).

Class Services Minimum capital
1 Order execution, placing, order transmission, advice, portfolio management, transfers EUR 50,000
2 Class 1 plus custody and administration, exchange EUR 125,000
3 Class 2 plus operating a trading platform EUR 150,000

Capital is the higher of that amount and a quarter of the previous year's fixed overheads, using projected overheads in the first year (Art 67). The sources we checked show no extra national capital figure. The file also covers custody, complaints, conflicts, outsourcing, business continuity, AML controls and ICT under DORA. Content follows Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/305 and the forms follow Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/306.

How do you apply to the ČNB?

  1. Map your services to MiCA's crypto-asset services and fix your capital class.
  2. Form the Czech company and prepare the Art 62 file.
  3. Submit it through the ČNB contact point described in the PDF on its crypto licensing page.
  4. Pay the CZK 20,000 fee.
  5. The ČNB acknowledges within 5 working days and confirms completeness within 25 working days (Art 63).
  6. It decides within 40 working days of a complete application. Information requests by the 20th working day can pause the clock for up to 20 working days.
  7. After the decision becomes final, the ČNB adds you to its lists, and ESMA lists you in its register.

The ČNB said on 11 February 2026 that it follows the binding MiCA deadlines and that they can restart when you submit more information. It has published no average duration.

How does the Czech fee compare with other countries?

The capital classes and the clock are the same in every member state (MiCA Annex IV, Art 63). Application fees are not.

The fee is a small part of the budget. See Lithuania, Malta and Estonia for the other home states Czech founders compare.

What this means for you

If you ran a crypto business under a Czech trade licence and did not apply by 31 July 2025, you have no cover now. The ČNB has ordered firms in that position to stop. Talk to it about the wind-down.

If you want a Czech licence now, treat the ČNB's list of weaknesses as a checklist. Show a verifiable track record, a real office rather than a virtual address, and a realistic, transparent business model, ownership structure and financing.

If you are already a bank or investment firm, use the Art 60 notification instead of an application. An EMI can notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues (Art 60(4)). The ESMA register shows Partners Banka, a.s. notifying on 29 July 2026.

If you only want to serve Czech clients, a CASP authorised in another member state can do that, as the ČNB confirms.

Czech crypto license FAQs

How much does a Czech crypto license cost?

The ČNB charges CZK 20,000 for the application. Capital is EUR 50,000, 125,000 or 150,000 by class, or a quarter of fixed overheads if higher. Staff, office, audit, ICT and advisers come on top.

Can I still use a Czech trade licence for crypto services?

No. Under Sec 26 of Act 31/2025 the cover lasted until a decision on a timely MiCA application and no later than 1 July 2026. The ČNB ordered firms still operating without authorisation to stop.

How many firms has the ČNB authorised?

Eleven domestic CASPs as of its 19 October 2026 release. The ESMA register file read on 23 November 2026 shows 12 Czech entries, 11 of them authorisations and 1 an Art 60 notification.

How long does the ČNB take?

MiCA gives 25 working days to confirm completeness and 40 working days to decide, and information requests can pause the clock. The ČNB has published no average duration.

Is the Czech Republic an easy place to get a crypto license?

The ČNB assessed 251 applications and authorised 11. It said a significant share were wholly insufficient in content and many showed no verifiable track record.

Can I get a Czech licence with a virtual office?

The ČNB named virtual addresses for registered offices as a weakness. MiCA also requires effective management in the EU and an EU-resident director.

Does a Czech CASP licence work across the EU?

Yes. After a passport notice, you can start in another member state on receipt of your home regulator's communication or on the 15th calendar day at the latest (Art 65).
