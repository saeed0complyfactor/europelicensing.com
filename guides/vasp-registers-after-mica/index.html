What Happened to National VASP Registers After MiCA

Before MiCA, each country ran its own registration or licence for virtual asset service providers (VASPs): AML registrations, trade licences, the French PSAN register, Italy's OAM list, Malta's VFA licences and Liechtenstein's TVTG registrations. After the transitional periods ended, none of them counts as a MiCA authorisation, and we found no country that converted its old register automatically.

What each country did with its old register (checked 23 November 2026)

The table covers the states where we found an official statement. We found no official statement on the old register in Cyprus, Denmark, Greece, Iceland and Romania, so the country pages for those states cover the transition only.

What the pattern looks like

  • Several old registrations were AML registrations, as in Lithuania, Poland, Ireland, Norway and Bulgaria. The Central Bank of Ireland says VASP registration does not lead to a simplified CASP assessment.
  • Three states kept the old list but gave it a new role. Spain keeps the Banco de España register for verification, Poland keeps the Katowice AML registration, and the UK keeps MLR registration until its own regime starts.
  • Some states removed entries on a fixed day: Estonia on 1 July 2026, France and Liechtenstein on 2 July 2026, and Malta on 2 July 2026 or the MiCA decision.
  • Two states offered former licensees a simplified route. Malta gave reduced fees and a simplified procedure to former VFA licensees for applications lodged by 1 July 2026, and Germany allowed existing KWG institutions a procedure built on a BaFin gap analysis.
  • Where figures exist, few old registrants ended with a MiCA authorisation. Estonia had 36 valid FIU licences at the start of 2026, and FI's notice names one CASP it had authorised by 30 June 2026. HANFA counted 17 VASPs on its register and five CASP licences before the deadline. The ČNB authorised 11 of 251 assessed applications, a figure that mixes applications and notifications.

What about the UK registers?

The UK is outside MiCA. Cryptoasset exchange providers and custodian wallet providers are registered with the FCA under the Money Laundering Regulations 2017 until the new FSMA regime starts, which the FCA expects on 25 October 2027. The FCA says firms should not apply for MLR registration after 31 July 2027. See the UK crypto page.

What this means for you

  • If you hold an old registration, treat it as history: it gives you no licence today. You need a MiCA authorisation from a home regulator, or an Art 60 notification if you are already a bank, investment firm or other listed entity. An EMI may notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues (Art 60(4)), and a payment institution has no Art 60 route.
  • An old registration does not shorten the decision clock. MiCA Art 63 sets 25 working days to check completeness and 40 working days to decide.
  • Check whether your home regulator can accept a file. Poland has no designated authority for CASPs, and we found no act designating Romania's ASF. See the Poland page and Romania page.
  • Firms that stopped must follow the wind-down rules in the country notices, for example returning client assets in Croatia and telling customers how to sell, move or close holdings in Sweden. The French AMF notice names up to two years' imprisonment and a EUR 30,000 fine for unauthorised provision after 1 July 2026.
  • Read the transitional periods tracker for the dates and the authorised CASPs tracker for the current register counts. The full process is in the MiCA CASP licence guide.

National VASP register FAQs

Is my old VASP registration still valid after MiCA?

Not as authorisation to provide crypto-asset services. National regimes ended by 1 July 2026 at the latest. Check the table for your country and its end date.

What happened to the French PSAN register?

The AMF said the transition was over from 2 July 2026. PSANs with a MiCA authorisation left the PSAN list on the date of authorisation, and those without one were removed on 2 July 2026. The AMF notice says neither the registration nor the optional agrément gave an EU passport.

What happened to Maltese VFA licences?

They were cancelled on 2 July 2026 or on the MiCA decision, if earlier (VFA Act Art 66(2), inserted by Cap. 647 Art 63). The simplified procedure and reduced fees for former VFA licensees applied only to applications lodged by 1 July 2026.

Does the Estonian FIU licence still count?

No. The FIU cancelled the register data on 1 July 2026, and only firms authorised under MiCA by FI or another EEA supervisor may provide crypto-asset services in Estonia.

Does the Italian OAM register still matter?

Not for authorisation. The joint Consob and Banca d'Italia release says the transitional period ends from 1 July 2026. Consob Communication 16/25, as reported, let firms on the OAM register continue until a decision and no later than 30 June 2026. Consob points to the ESMA register for the CASP list.

Does the Liechtenstein TVTG still apply?

For services covered by MiCA, TVTG registrations expired on 2 July 2026. Items outside MiCA, such as NFTs, may still fall under the TVTG.

Can a former VASP get a simplified MiCA authorisation?

Only where a state offered one for a set window, such as Malta for applications lodged by 1 July 2026 or Germany for existing KWG institutions. The Central Bank of Ireland says VASP registration does not lead to a simplified assessment.
