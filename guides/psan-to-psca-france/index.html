From PSAN to PSCA: France After 1 July 2026

The French PSAN regime for digital-asset service providers ended on 1 July 2026. Only providers with a MiCA authorisation, called PSCA in French, may now provide crypto-asset services in France. The agrément comes from the Autorité des marchés financiers (AMF) and, unlike a PSAN registration, carries an EU passport.

What changed between PSAN and PSCA?

A PSAN, prestataire de services sur actifs numériques, was a provider under the French Pacte law. The regime had a simple registration, an enhanced registration and an optional agrément. The AMF said on 2 August 2024 that none of them gave a European passport. MiCA replaced it from 30 December 2024, and the national transition let existing PSANs continue until 1 July 2026 at the latest, or until the AMF granted or refused authorisation.

Legal basis French Pacte law, registered or approved by the AMF Regulation (EU) 2023/1114, authorised by the AMF

What does the AMF say about firms that did not make the deadline?

The AMF notice tells PSANs without an authorisation to follow an orderly wind-down plan and to return clients' assets. After 1 July 2026, providing the services without authorisation carries up to two years' imprisonment and a EUR 30,000 fine. The AMF may publish a list of unauthorised providers and ask courts to block their websites.

On 6 July 2026 the AMF said the move from the Pacte regime to MiCA was over. It counted 31 French actors authorised, with some authorisations still being finalised, and 283 in the EU. It named France as the second-largest location of authorised PSCAs. It said it keeps monitoring wind-down plans, supervises PSCA conduct rules and keeps assessing applications.

What does a PSCA agrément require?

You need a legal person with its registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (MiCA Art 59(2)). A French company is the usual route for an AMF file. Capital is the higher of the class minimum and a quarter of the previous year's fixed overheads (Art 67).

Class Services Minimum capital
1 Order execution, placing, reception and transmission of orders, advice, portfolio management, transfers EUR 50,000
2 Class 1 plus custody and administration, exchange EUR 125,000
3 Class 2 plus operating a trading platform EUR 150,000

Directors need good repute, knowledge, skills and time, and qualifying shareholders are vetted (Art 68). The AMF lists the common rules: AML and counter-terrorist financing, cyber security, conduct, governance, conflicts of interest and custody. DORA applies to your ICT risk management.

How do you apply for the agrément PSCA?

  1. Choose the class from the services you will offer.
  2. Set up the French company, or plan the conversion of an existing one.
  3. Prepare the Art 62 file using the content rules in the technical standards the AMF's MiCA page cites. The AMF notes that first versions of files are often incomplete.
  4. File with the AMF. Its MiCA page gave an email mailbox for applications when we read it, so confirm the current channel before you send anything.
  5. The AMF acknowledges within 5 working days, checks completeness within 25 working days and decides within 40 working days of a complete application. It can pause the clock for up to 20 working days to ask questions (MiCA Art 63). The AMF says ESMA guidance allows up to four months for the review of a complete file.
  6. After the decision, the AMF sends an invoice for the EUR 10,000 contribution. The 2025 guide says to pay within six months of the grant in the first year.

The AMF's earlier simplified procedure for PSANs with enhanced registration was tied to the transition and has no use now.

What this means for you

If you were a PSAN and have no authorisation, you are out of time in France. Wind down in an orderly way, return client assets and speak to the AMF. Operating without authorisation carries criminal penalties.

If you are starting from scratch, decide between a French application and another home state. The capital classes and the Art 63 clock are the same everywhere. Fees, language and local substance rules differ, and the AMF's MiCA material is mainly in French. An AMF authorisation gives you the passport to every EEA state.

If you are a bank or investment firm, the Art 60 notification replaces the application, at least 40 working days before you start. An EMI can notify only for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues (Art 60(4)). If your firm is authorised in another EU state, you passport into France and need no AMF licence.

If you hold or transfer e-money tokens for clients, the activity can also touch payment licensing. See the payment institution licence in France.

PSAN to PSCA FAQs

What is a PSAN and what replaced it?

A PSAN was a digital-asset service provider under the French Pacte regime. MiCA replaced it, and the authorisation is now the PSCA.

Can I keep operating as a PSAN?

No. The transition ended on 1 July 2026 and PSANs without a MiCA authorisation were struck off on 2 July 2026.

Did a PSAN registration give an EU passport?

No. The AMF said prior registration or optional authorisation gave no European passport during the transition. A MiCA authorisation does.

How much does the agrément PSCA cost?

The AMF guide sets EUR 10,000 at authorisation and EUR 10,000 a year, plus 0.0094 per mille of assets under custody for custodians, with a EUR 1,500 minimum. Capital is EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000 by class.

How long does the AMF take to decide?

MiCA Art 63 gives 25 working days for completeness and 40 working days to decide on a complete file, with pauses for questions. The AMF says ESMA guidance allows up to four months for a complete file.

Which regulator grants the licence in France?

The AMF. The ACPR authorises asset-referenced and e-money token issuers.

How many crypto firms are authorised in France?

The AMF counted 31 on 6 July 2026. The ESMA register file read on 23 November 2026 lists 36 entries with France as home state, including Art 60 notifiers.
