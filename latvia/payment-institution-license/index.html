Payment Institution License in Latvia

A payment institution (PI) licence in Latvia lets you provide payment services such as money remittance, acquiring or payment initiation from a Latvian company. Latvijas Banka, the central bank, grants it under the Law on Payment Services and Electronic Money. Own funds run from EUR 20,000 to EUR 125,000 depending on the services, and the application fee is EUR 5,000.

Who needs a payment institution license in Latvia?

You need Latvijas Banka authorisation to provide payment services in Latvia on a professional basis, unless you are a credit institution, an EMI or another listed provider. Latvijas Banka's page counts an institution licensed in another member state that has started business in Latvia among the entities entitled to provide payment services.

Other routes may fit better:

  • Registered PI. It covers only issuing payment instruments and money remittance. There is no minimum capital, but average monthly payments, taken over the previous 12 months or the next 12 in your business plan, must stay at or below EUR 3 million. Users must be linked to Latvia, the board and legal address must be in Latvia, and you cannot serve customers outside Latvia or use international payment systems.
  • Account information only. The own funds requirement does not apply to a licence that covers only account information services.
  • A payment institution licensed in another EEA state, serving Latvia by notification.
  • An EMI licence if you hold a stored balance for customers.
  • Innovative services. Latvijas Banka lists a EUR 450 examination fee for an applicant offering only an innovative electronic payment service on the scale of Latvia.

Payment institution license requirements in Latvia

Capital. Latvijas Banka's page says own funds are EUR 20,000 to EUR 125,000 depending on the services selected (Section 12 of the Law), plus the Section 35 requirement, and does not list the amount per service. PSD2 Art 7 sets EUR 125,000 for most services, EUR 50,000 for payment initiation and EUR 20,000 for money remittance only.

Own funds. Own funds must stay at or above the higher of initial capital and the PSD2 Art 9 amount, using method A, B or C as the regulator decides. Latvijas Banka's page does not set the method.

Management and fit and proper. Management needs adequate education and experience (Section 20). Management and shareholders with 10% or more of the capital or voting rights need good repute and no criminal record (Section 21). You file CVs, education documents and criminal record statements for board members, the AML officer and the person managing payment services (Annex 14).

Substance. PSD2 Art 11(3) puts the head office in the same member state as the registered office. The licensed PI page sets no further board or office minimum. A registered PI needs its management board and legal address in Latvia.

AML. You file AML, counter-terrorism financing and sanctions rules, a risk assessment, Annex 13 and Latvijas Banka's AML checklist.

Safeguarding. The file describes how users' funds are protected (Section 38; Annex 6 of Regulation No 270), in line with PSD2 Art 10.

ICT and DORA. The file covers an information systems security policy (Annex 11), a business continuity plan (Annex 10), security incident handling and complaints procedures (Section 104.2; Annex 8) and sensitive payment data controls (Annex 12). Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 applies directly.

Insurance. If you provide payment initiation or account information services, you file a certified professional indemnity insurance policy or a comparable credit institution guarantee until the licence decision (PSD2 Art 5).

How to get a payment institution license in Latvia: process

  1. Book the free pre-licensing advice. Email info@bank.lv or call +371 6702 2300. Latvijas Banka responds within up to 30 days, depending on the volume and complexity of your questions.
  2. Prepare the file under Regulation No 270. It includes the Annex 1 application with the shareholder structure, the programme of operations (Annex 3), a business plan for at least three financial years with forecasts and draft financial statements (Annex 4), evidence of initial capital, safeguarding, internal control and risk management (Annex 7), AML documents, a close links list, management data, and policies on outsourcing (Section 29) and on agent and branch supervision (Section 27) where they apply.
  3. Language. The English page does not state a filing language. Confirm it in the advice meeting.
  4. Submit and pay EUR 5,000 at submission.
  5. Completeness check. Latvijas Banka checks the file within 15 working days.
  6. Examination. Latvijas Banka's page says it starts examining the application within 3 months from confirmed completeness, and it can extend that if information is insufficient. PSD2 Art 12 sets a three-month decision period from a complete application.
  7. Decision. Latvijas Banka grants or refuses the licence and lists the result on its website and in the EBA register of payment institutions.

The statutory periods cover the regulator's work. Preparing the file and answering questions come on top of them.

After you get the payment institution license

  • Register. Latvijas Banka lists the result on its website and in the EBA register. Registered institutions sit in a separate list.
  • Supervision. Latvijas Banka assigns a curator and supervises you on an ongoing basis. You file regular reports under Regulation No 244 and notify changes.
  • Fees. The annual supervision fee applies as shown in the cost table.
  • Passport. A licensed PI notifies Latvijas Banka before serving another EEA state (PSD2 Art 28). A registered PI cannot serve outside Latvia.
  • Change of control. PSD2 Art 6 requires prior notification of changes in qualifying holdings. Notify management and shareholder changes to Latvijas Banka too.

Payment institution in Latvia compared with other countries

Latvia publishes a EUR 5,000 examination fee and an own funds range of EUR 20,000 to EUR 125,000. Lithuania and Estonia license the same institution under their own regulators. In Latvia itself, the EMI licence covers e-money and the crypto page covers MiCA. For the EU rules, read the payment institution licence guide.

Payment institution in Latvia FAQs

How much capital does a payment institution need in Latvia?

Latvijas Banka's page says EUR 20,000 to EUR 125,000 depending on the services, plus the Section 35 requirement. PSD2 Art 7 sets EUR 125,000, EUR 50,000 for payment initiation and EUR 20,000 for money remittance only. Account information alone has no own funds requirement.

How long does Latvijas Banka take to decide on a PI licence?

It checks completeness within 15 working days. Its page says examination starts within 3 months of confirmed completeness, and it can extend that if information is insufficient. PSD2 Art 12 sets three months for the decision.

How much does a payment institution application cost in Latvia?

The examination fee is EUR 5,000, paid at submission. A licence limited to an innovative service costs EUR 450. Supervision is EUR 7,000 a year plus up to 1.4% of gross income, capped at EUR 100,000, or EUR 3,000 a year plus the variable part for payment initiation and account information only.

What is a registered payment institution in Latvia?

It is a tier limited to issuing payment instruments and money remittance. There is no minimum capital, but average monthly payments must stay at or below EUR 3 million, the management board and legal address must be in Latvia, and you cannot serve customers outside Latvia. The examination fee is EUR 2,500.

Can a Latvian payment institution serve other EU countries?

A licensed one can, by notification under PSD2 Art 28. A registered one cannot.

Do I need an office in Latvia?

The head office sits in the same state as the registered office (PSD2 Art 11(3)). A registered PI needs its board and legal address in Latvia. The licensed PI page sets no further minimum.

Do I need insurance?

For payment initiation or account information services, yes. You file professional indemnity insurance or a credit institution guarantee until the licence decision.

Can I register as an account information provider only?

An account information licence has no own funds requirement in Latvia, and Latvijas Banka lists a EUR 3,000 base supervision fee for it. PSD2 Art 33 also allows registration of account information service providers. Ask Latvijas Banka which route fits your model.
