Crypto License in Latvia

A crypto licence in Latvia is a MiCA authorisation from Latvijas Banka, the central bank, which the Law on Crypto-asset Services names as the competent authority. Latvijas Banka issued its first licence on 3 December 2025, and ESMA's interim register listed 12 entries with Latvia as the home state on 23 November 2026. The review fee is EUR 2,500.

Who needs a crypto license in Latvia?

Any firm providing crypto-asset services in Latvia on a professional basis needs MiCA authorisation from Latvijas Banka or another EEA supervisor. The services are custody, trading platforms, exchange, execution, placing, order reception, advice, portfolio management and transfers. See the MiCA licence guide for the full list. MiCA does not cover unique non-fungible tokens (Art 2(3)), and recital 22 leaves out services provided in a fully decentralised manner with no intermediary.

Routes that may fit better:

  • Another EEA authorisation. A CASP authorised elsewhere can serve Latvia after the Art 65 notification.
  • Art 60 route for firms Latvijas Banka already licenses. Latvijas Banka's page lists credit institutions, central securities depositories, investment firms, regulated market operators, EMIs (custody and transfer for their own e-money tokens), management companies and alternative investment fund managers. You file at least 40 business days before you start, and Latvijas Banka checks completeness within 20 business days. Articles 62, 63, 64, 67, 83 and 84 do not apply.
  • E-money token or asset-referenced token issuance. Latvijas Banka has separate pages for these.

Latvijas Banka's page lists 20 items for the file, from the programme of operations and prudential safeguards to governance, management CVs and a Punishment Register statement, qualifying shareholders, internal control, AML, business continuity, ICT, client asset segregation, complaints handling, articles of association, outsourcing and the service-specific policies for custody, trading platforms, exchange, execution, advice and transfers.

How to get a crypto license in Latvia: process

  1. Book a free pre-licensing consultation. Email info@bank.lv or call +371 6702 2300. Latvijas Banka answers within up to 30 days, and its news item of 4 December 2025 says there is no charge.
  2. Map your services and pick your capital class.
  3. Prepare the file. Use the 20-item list and the application form on Latvijas Banka's page.
  4. Language. The page does not state a filing language, and the application form it provides is in English. Confirm the rest in the consultation.
  5. Pay the EUR 2,500 review fee.
  6. Completeness. Latvijas Banka checks the file within 25 working days.
  7. Decision. It reviews within 40 working days after completeness is confirmed, and can extend the period for objective reasons if information is insufficient. MiCA Art 63 allows the clock to pause for up to 20 working days.
  8. Passport. Notify Latvijas Banka before you serve another state (MiCA Art 65).

The statutory periods cover the regulator's work. Preparing the file and answering questions come on top of them.

After authorisation

ESMA lists you in its register, and Latvijas Banka supervises you. You report to it, pay the annual supervision payment and notify it of changes in management and qualifying holdings (Latvijas Banka Regulation No 241 covers qualifying holdings). Passport notifications go through Latvijas Banka (Art 65). Services you add later need a new application or notification.

Latvia crypto license: status and market

We found no Latvian legacy crypto licence register to convert into MiCA authorisations. The Law on Crypto-asset Services came into force on 30 June 2024 and named Latvijas Banka as the competent authority. Its transitional rule let providers supervised by the State Revenue Service that had started by 30 December 2024 continue until 30 June 2025, or until a decision if they had applied by then. MiCA Art 143(3) sets 1 July 2026 as the latest end of any national transitional regime.

Latvijas Banka's news item of 4 December 2025 says that during 2025 it held 50 consultations on MiCA services, received 5 licence applications and had 12 companies in pre-licensing consultations.

Latvia vs Lithuania crypto license

Both countries are EEA states, so a CASP authorised in either one can serve the other by notification. Official sources show these differences:

Point Latvia Lithuania
Old regime No legacy licence register found. State Revenue Service supervised providers had until 30 June 2025 See the Lithuania page
ESMA register entries, home state, 23 November 2026 12 7 (4 Article 63 authorisations, 3 Article 60 notifications)
Regulator fee for a CASP application EUR 2,500 (Law Section 6) See the Lithuania page
Decision clock MiCA Art 63: 25 and 40 working days Same

The statutory decision periods are the same everywhere. Fees and language differ, so read Estonia too. In Latvia itself, the sibling licences are the EMI licence and the payment institution licence.

Crypto license in Latvia FAQs

How much does a crypto license cost in Latvia?

The review fee is EUR 2,500 for firms not already supervised by Latvijas Banka. Capital is EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000 by class, or a quarter of fixed overheads if higher. Annual supervision is up to 0.6% of gross crypto-asset income, minimum EUR 3,000.

Which regulator grants a crypto license in Latvia?

Latvijas Banka, designated in Section 4(1) of the Law on Crypto-asset Services.

How long does the Latvian crypto license take?

Latvijas Banka checks completeness within 25 working days and reviews within 40 working days after completeness is confirmed. It can extend the review for objective reasons, and MiCA Art 63 allows a pause of up to 20 working days.

When did Latvia issue its first MiCA licence?

On 3 December 2025, to BlockBen SIA.

How many crypto firms are authorised in Latvia?

ESMA's interim register listed 12 entries with Latvia as the home state on 23 November 2026, dated 3 December 2025 to 30 September 2026. The register does not split authorisations from Article 60 entries, and the count includes an investment firm.

Does Latvia have a small crypto licence tier?

No. MiCA sets no small CASP tier, and the capital classes start at EUR 50,000.

Is Latvia or Lithuania better for a crypto licence?

The MiCA clocks are the same. The ESMA register lists 12 Latvian and 7 Lithuanian home-state entries, and the fees differ. Compare them against your product and team.

Can I serve Latvian clients with a licence from another EU country?

Yes, after your home regulator forwards the Art 65 notification.
