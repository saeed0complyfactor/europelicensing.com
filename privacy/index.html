Privacy policy

This page explains what personal data Europe Licensing collects through this website and by email, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

Who is responsible for your data

The controller is ComplyFactor Ltd, registered at 167-169 Great Portland Street, 5th Floor, London W1W 5PF, United Kingdom, company number 16474559 (registered in England and Wales). In this policy, "we" and "us" mean that entity. Europe Licensing is the name we use on this website.

You can contact us about your data at info@europelicensing.com.

This policy applies the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the UK GDPR together with the Data Protection Act 2018.

What data we collect

Through the contact form and by email, we receive what you choose to send us. That is usually:

  • your name;
  • your business email address and phone number, if you give one;
  • your company name and role;
  • the licence or jurisdiction you are asking about, and the message you write.

We do not ask for special category data (such as health data or political opinions) or for identity documents through this website. Please do not send them. If you send us personal data about other people, such as directors or shareholders of your firm, make sure you are allowed to share it with us.

Our services are for businesses, and this website does not collect data from children on purpose.

Why we use your data and our lawful basis

Purpose Data used Lawful basis
Reply to your enquiry and, if you ask, prepare a proposal Contact details, company details, your message Art 6(1)(b) GDPR: steps you ask us to take before entering a contract
Keep a record of enquiries, run and secure the website and our email, and handle disputes Enquiry content, technical data, correspondence Art 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in running and protecting a business
Contact you again about a related enquiry you made Contact details, enquiry content Art 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in following up on your request

We do not send marketing emails unless you ask for them. If we add a newsletter later, we will ask for your consent first and you can withdraw it at any time under Art 7(3) GDPR.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, you can object under Art 21 GDPR (see "Your rights" below). We do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

You do not have to give us your data, but we cannot answer an enquiry without a way to contact you.

Who we share your data with

We share personal data only with providers that help us run the site and the business, and only for the purposes above. They act as processors under written terms that meet Art 28 GDPR.

We may also disclose data to professional advisers, to a regulator or court where the law requires it, or to a buyer if the business is sold, under duties of confidentiality. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep your data

  • Backups and logs are deleted on the provider's normal cycle.

If a dispute or legal claim is open, we keep the relevant records until it is resolved.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you (Art 15);
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art 16);
  • have data erased in certain cases (Art 17);
  • restrict how we use your data in certain cases (Art 18);
  • be told when we pass a correction, erasure or restriction on to others (Art 19);
  • receive your data in a portable format where we use it under a contract or with your consent (Art 20);
  • object to use based on legitimate interests (Art 21);
  • not be subject to a decision based only on automated processing that significantly affects you (Art 22).

To use any of these rights, write to info@europelicensing.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will reply within one month, as Art 12(3) GDPR requires, and tell you if we need more time. We do not charge a fee unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

If we process your data with your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect use before that point.

How to complain

You can complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art 77 GDPR). If you are in the EU or EEA, you can contact the authority in the country where you live or work, or where you think the problem happened. We are established in the UK and have no establishment in the EU, so no EU authority acts as our lead supervisory authority. You can go to the authority in your own country.

If you are in the UK, the authority is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), at ico.org.uk.

We would like the chance to fix the problem first, so please contact us before you complain.

Cookies and tracking

With your consent, we use two analytics tools: Google Analytics 4 from Google and Microsoft Clarity from Microsoft. They give us usage statistics, such as which pages are visited, how visitors arrive and how long they stay. Clarity also makes heatmaps and session recordings of clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, with anything typed into forms masked. We use this only to improve the site. Google's advertising storage and ad personalisation are switched off.

These tools set cookies and read data from your device, so they load only after you click Accept in the cookie banner. If you click Decline or make no choice, they do not load. The lawful basis is your consent (Art 6(1)(a) GDPR, together with the ePrivacy rules and PECR in the UK). Google and Microsoft may process this data outside the EEA and UK, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their privacy statements: Google and Microsoft.

You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie choices" button at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw consent, the tools are told to stop using cookies straight away and do not load on later pages. Cookies they have already set stay in your browser until they expire or you delete them. We remember your choice in your browser's local storage (an entry called site-consent that holds only yes or no); it is not sent to us.

If we add any other tool that sets cookies or reads data from your device, we will ask for consent where the law requires it and update this policy first.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our use of data changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change affects how we use data you have already given us, we will tell you directly where we can.

Questions about this policy or your data: info@europelicensing.com. Post: ComplyFactor Ltd, 167-169 Great Portland Street, 5th Floor, London W1W 5PF, United Kingdom.
