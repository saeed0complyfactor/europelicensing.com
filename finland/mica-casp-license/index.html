Crypto License in Finland

A MiCA crypto-asset service provider (CASP) licence for Finland comes from the Financial Supervisory Authority (FIN-FSA, Finanssivalvonta), under Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 and the Finnish Act on Crypto-Asset Providers and Crypto-Asset Markets (402/2024). The old national virtual currency registration ended on 30 June 2025, so a firm without a MiCA authorisation cannot operate in Finland. FIN-FSA's 2026 schedule lists EUR 8,700 for processing an authorisation.

Who needs a crypto license in Finland?

Providing crypto-asset services in the EU requires authorisation (MiCA Art 59). FIN-FSA says authorised providers are listed in a public FIN-FSA register. The ten crypto-asset services are defined in MiCA Art 3(1)(16), and they include custody, operating a trading platform, exchange for funds, order execution, advice, portfolio management and transfer services.

Other routes can fit better:

  • Art 60 notification. Credit institutions, investment firms, EMIs and certain other regulated firms can notify FIN-FSA at least 40 working days before they start (MiCA Art 60). FIN-FSA's schedule lists EUR 3,500 for processing an Art 60 notification. The scope follows your existing licence, and an EMI may only offer custody and transfer services for the e-money tokens it issues.
  • Authorisation in another member state, then a passport into Finland. FIN-FSA keeps a notification list of such providers.
  • An EMI licence is the base for issuing e-money tokens.

Crypto license requirements in Finland

Capital. Your minimum is EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000, depending on the services (MiCA Annex IV). Art 67 sets prudential safeguards at the higher of that minimum and one quarter of the previous year's fixed overheads.

Management and fit and proper. Directors need good repute, knowledge, skills, experience and time, and qualifying shareholders need good repute (MiCA Art 68).

Substance. You need a registered office in a member state, place of effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (MiCA Art 59(2)). The Finnish sources we read add no further local office rules, so ask FIN-FSA.

AML. AML/CFT controls are part of the application and the governance rules.

Safeguarding. Client crypto-assets are not used for your own account, and client funds go to a credit institution or central bank by the end of the next business day (MiCA Art 70).

ICT and DORA. Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 applies, and MiCA Art 68 requires ICT and continuity arrangements under it.

How to get a crypto license in Finland: process

  1. Choose the services and the capital class, or the Art 60 notification route if you hold another licence.
  2. Prepare the application under MiCA Art 62 and the delegated rules, with a programme of operations, governance, custody and complaints policies, and a financial plan.
  3. Apply to FIN-FSA on its CASP application form, with the separate fit and proper declaration form, and send the file to FIN-FSA's registry by email or post. FIN-FSA's page does not mention a pre-application meeting, so ask whether one is available.
  4. FIN-FSA acknowledges within 5 working days and checks completeness within 25 working days (MiCA Art 63).
  5. FIN-FSA decides within 40 working days of a complete application. Questions pause the clock for up to 20 working days.
  6. ESMA is informed within 2 working days of the decision, and the register is updated.

The statutory periods cover the regulator's assessment. Your preparation and your answers to questions come on top.

After you get the crypto license

  • FIN-FSA lists you in its register of supervised entities, and ESMA lists you in the interim MiCA register.
  • To serve other EEA states, notify FIN-FSA under MiCA Art 65. FIN-FSA forwards the notice within 10 working days, and you may start when the host receives it or on the 15th calendar day at the latest.
  • Report changes to the information in your application to FIN-FSA.
  • Pay the supervision fees FIN-FSA charges under the Act on Supervision Fees.
  • Check MiCA's qualifying holding rules and FIN-FSA's guidance before any share transfer.

Is the Finnish crypto transition over?

Yes. FIN-FSA said on 7 July 2025 that the national transition period for virtual currency providers ended on 30 June 2025. National law was repealed and replaced by MiCA, and a provider with an application still under review could actively offer services only after authorisation. ESMA's list gives Finland a 6-month grandfathering period under MiCA Art 143(3).

FIN-FSA's 30 June 2026 release adds that Finland had no transitional period running to 30 June 2026, as some other countries did, and that unauthorised third-country providers can serve Finnish clients only on the client's own initiative.

Crypto license in Finland FAQs

How much does a crypto license cost in Finland?

FIN-FSA's 2026 schedule lists EUR 8,700 for authorising a CASP and EUR 3,500 for an Art 60 notification. Capital is EUR 50,000, 125,000 or 150,000 by class, or a quarter of fixed overheads if higher (MiCA Art 67, Annex IV). Staff, custody, audit and legal work cost extra.

Is the Finnish VASP registration still valid?

No. The national transition ended on 30 June 2025, and MiCA replaced the national regime.

How long does FIN-FSA take to decide on a CASP application?

25 working days to check completeness and 40 working days to decide from a complete application, with up to 20 working days of pause for questions (MiCA Art 63).

What are the crypto license requirements in Finland?

A registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU, an EU-resident director, capital for your class, fit and proper directors and shareholders, AML/CFT controls, DORA-compliant ICT, and custody and complaints policies (MiCA Art 59, 67, 68, 70).

Can an EMI offer crypto services in Finland by notification?

Yes, through Art 60, at least 40 working days before starting, and only for custody and transfer services for the e-money tokens it issues.

Does a Finnish CASP licence work in the rest of the EU?

Yes. After a notification to FIN-FSA under MiCA Art 65, you can serve other EEA states.

How many CASPs are authorised in Finland?

The ESMA interim register lists five Finnish entries on 23 November 2026. Check the FIN-FSA register for the current list.

Which language do I file in?

The FIN-FSA sources we read do not say. Ask FIN-FSA before you prepare translations.
