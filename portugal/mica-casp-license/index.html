Crypto License in Portugal

A crypto licence in Portugal means authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider (CASP, or prestador de serviços de criptoativos) under MiCA. The Banco de Portugal grants it, working with the CMVM, the securities regulator, under Law 69/2025. Applications go through the Banco de Portugal's SIRES portal, and its page says neither the application nor the authorisation carries a cost.

Who needs a crypto license in Portugal?

You need MiCA authorisation to provide crypto-asset services as a business from Portugal or to Portuguese clients: custody, exchange, trading platforms, order execution, placing, transfer, advice, portfolio management, and reception and transmission of orders (MiCA Art 3(1)(16)). Only providers authorised in an EU member state may operate in Portugal.

Other routes can fit better.

  • You are a bank, investment firm, EMI or other listed financial entity. You can notify the Banco de Portugal under MiCA Art 60 at least 40 working days before starting, instead of applying. An EMI is limited to custody and transfer for the e-money tokens it issues. See the EMI licence in Portugal.
  • You are authorised in another EU state. You passport into Portugal and need no Portuguese licence.
  • You were on the old Portuguese virtual asset register. That registration let you continue only until 1 July 2026 or a decision, and it did not exempt you from the MiCA procedure (Banco de Portugal).

Crypto license requirements in Portugal

Capital by class

Class Services Minimum capital
1 Order execution, placing, reception and transmission of orders, advice, portfolio management, transfer EUR 50,000
2 Class 1 plus custody and administration, exchange for funds or for other crypto-assets EUR 125,000
3 Class 2 plus operating a trading platform EUR 150,000

Prudential safeguards are the higher of that capital and one quarter of the previous year's fixed overheads, with a forecast for firms under a year old (MiCA Art 67).

Substance and legal form

MiCA needs a registered office in a member state where you carry out part of your services, effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (Art 59(2)). We did not find a further Portuguese rule on local staff or premises.

Governance, ICT and AML

The management body needs good repute and the right skills, and qualifying shareholders are vetted (MiCA Art 68). ICT and continuity arrangements follow DORA, and AML/CFT controls are required (Art 68). Our MiCA CASP licence page lists each rule.

Client safeguards and conduct

Client funds go to a credit institution or central bank by the end of the next business day, and custody needs an agreement, a policy and segregation (MiCA Arts 70 and 75). The Banco de Portugal supervises Chapters 1, 4 and 5 of Title V and Articles 67 to 69, 73 and 74. The CMVM supervises Chapter 3, on specific services, and Articles 66 and 70 to 72, which cover conduct, safekeeping of client assets, complaints and conflicts of interest (Law 69/2025 Art 2).

How to apply for a crypto license in Portugal

  1. Choose the class from the services you will offer.
  2. Set up the legal entity and decide where management sits.
  3. Build the MiCA Art 62 file using Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/305 and Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/306, which the Banco de Portugal names as the source of the required content.
  4. File through SIRES, the Banco de Portugal's reporting system, using Citizen or Corporate access. A filing after 16:30 counts as delivered on the next business day. Send documents as searchable PDFs where possible.
  5. The Banco de Portugal checks completeness and can ask for more information, in coordination with the CMVM.
  6. The decision is notified within 40 working days of the complete application or of the extra information, and never later than 60 working days from the complete application (Banco de Portugal page; MiCA Art 63). Before a refusal or a conditional authorisation, you get a preliminary hearing.
  7. Start operating within 12 months of the decision. After that, the authorisation is revoked.

After the Banco de Portugal grants the license

  • Register. ESMA lists you in its interim MiCA register. Ask the Banco de Portugal how its own list of authorised entities will show you.
  • Reporting and supervision. The Banco de Portugal supervises authorisation, prudential and governance rules, and the CMVM supervises conduct duties.
  • Passport. You notify the Banco de Portugal of the services and states. It forwards the notice within 10 working days, and you may start on receipt of that communication or on the 15th calendar day (MiCA Art 65).
  • Changes. Acquisitions of a CASP and changes in management go to the Banco de Portugal.

Status of MiCA in Portugal

The transitional period has ended. A firm registered with the Banco de Portugal for virtual-asset activity at 30 December 2024 could continue until 1 July 2026 or a decision, and MiCA Art 143(3) set 1 July 2026 as the latest end in every member state. Law 69/2025 was published on 22 December 2025.

On 23 November 2026 the ESMA interim register, with entries up to 18 November 2026, listed two entities with Portugal as home state: Bison Bank, S.A. (19 May 2026) and Uphold Digital Assets Europe, Unipessoal Lda (11 November 2026). Both show the Banco de Portugal as competent authority. Check the ESMA file for later entries.

Portugal compared with other countries

For payment services, see the payment institution licence in Portugal, and for e-money the EMI licence in Portugal. For the same licence elsewhere, compare the MiCA licence in Spain, where the CNMV charges a fee under its own schedule, Lithuania and Malta. The EU baseline is on the MiCA CASP licence page.

Crypto license in Portugal FAQs

How much does a crypto license cost in Portugal?

The Banco de Portugal says neither the application nor the maintenance of the authorisation has associated costs. You need EUR 50,000, 125,000 or 150,000 of capital by class, or a quarter of fixed overheads if higher, plus the cost of running the firm. our fee range for CASP licence in Portugal

Who regulates crypto in Portugal?

The Banco de Portugal authorises CASPs and supervises prudential and governance rules. The CMVM gives input on applications and supervises conduct duties and safekeeping (Law 69/2025; Banco de Portugal page).

How long does the Banco de Portugal take to decide?

It notifies the decision within 40 working days of a complete application or of the extra information requested, and never later than 60 working days from the complete application. Your preparation time comes before that.

How do I apply for a crypto license in Portugal?

You file the MiCA Article 62 application through SIRES. The Banco de Portugal works with the CMVM on the assessment.

What is the SIRES portal?

SIRES is the Banco de Portugal's information system for supervised entities. Its CASP page says the application must be submitted through it.

Is the old Portuguese virtual asset registration still valid?

No. It allowed activity only until 1 July 2026 or a decision, and a prior registration did not exempt a firm from the MiCA procedure.

Does the application have to be in Portuguese?

We did not find a language rule in the material we could read. Ask the Banco de Portugal which documents it accepts in English.

Can a Portuguese CASP serve customers in other EU countries?

Yes. One authorisation is valid across the EU, with a passport notification under MiCA Art 65.
