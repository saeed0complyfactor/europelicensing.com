Payment Institution License in Portugal

A payment institution (PI, or instituição de pagamento, IP) licence from the Banco de Portugal lets a Portuguese company run payment accounts, execute transfers, acquire cards, send remittances or initiate payments, and passport those services across the EU and EEA. The Banco de Portugal decides within 3 months of a complete file, and never later than 12 months after the first filing. Portugal also has an exemption regime for small volumes, capped at an average of EUR 3 million of payments a month.

Who needs a payment institution license in Portugal?

You need authorisation before you provide payment services in Portugal as a business: payment accounts, execution of transfers and direct debits, card issuing or acquiring, money remittance or payment initiation (PSD2 Annex I; Art 11(1)).

Other routes can fit better.

  • You are small. Portugal's exemption regime under RJSPME Art 37 and Portaria 239/2019 allows a lighter file and EUR 50,000 of capital if your monthly payments average no more than EUR 3 million. The decree preamble says exempt firms must have their seat in Portugal, cannot passport, and cannot offer payment initiation, account information or money transfer services. Check which services qualify before you count on it.
  • You only provide account information. That is a registration with the Banco de Portugal, with no capital requirement, and an insurance requirement under PSD2 Art 5(3) and Art 33.
  • You hold stored value. An EMI licence in Portugal covers e-money and payment services together.
  • You are already authorised in another EEA state. Passport into Portugal under PSD2 Art 28.

Payment institution license requirements in Portugal

Capital and own funds

Money remittance only EUR 20,000 PSD2 Art 7(a)
Payment initiation only EUR 50,000 PSD2 Art 7(b)
Services 1 to 5 EUR 125,000 PSD2 Art 7(c)
Account information only None PSD2 Art 33
Exempt institution EUR 50,000 share capital Portaria 239/2019 Art 3(2)

Own funds then stay at or above the initial capital or the amount your Method A, B or C calculation gives, whichever is higher (PSD2 Arts 8 and 9).

Substance

The head office sits in the same state as the registered office (PSD2 Art 11(3)). Exempt institutions must have their seat in Portugal. We did not find a Banco de Portugal rule on resident directors.

Management and owners

Directors and qualifying shareholders are assessed for reputation and experience (PSD2 Art 5(1)). For the exemption, no manager may have a conviction for money laundering, terrorist financing or other financial crime (Portaria 239/2019 Art 2(2)(b)).

Safeguarding

Funds received for services 1 to 6 are segregated by the end of the next business day, or covered by insurance or a comparable guarantee (PSD2 Art 10(1)). Payment initiation and account information need professional indemnity insurance (PSD2 Art 5(2), (3)).

AML, governance and ICT

The Banco de Portugal asks for evidence of compliance with the AML/CFT rules that apply to institutions headquartered in Portugal. The file also covers governance, internal control, incident reporting, business continuity and ICT arrangements under DORA. See our AML and compliance programme.

How to apply for a payment institution license in Portugal

  1. Map each service to the PSD2 Annex I list. That sets your capital and whether the exemption can apply.
  2. Read the Banco de Portugal payment institution authorisation page and RJSPME Articles 18 to 23.
  3. Set up the Portuguese company and prove the capital.
  4. Build the Article 19 file. Exempt applicants skip most of the Article 19(2) items, except points a) to h), j), p) and q), and apply to the Banco de Portugal for the regime (Portaria 239/2019 Art 3(1)).
  5. File with the Banco de Portugal and answer its requests for information. The Banco de Portugal's crypto page names the SIRES portal for filing. We did not find the same statement on the payment institution page, so ask which channel applies.
  6. The 3-month period starts when the Banco de Portugal holds all the evidence and information it needs. The total cannot pass 12 months from the initial filing (RJSPME Art 23(1)).
  7. Authorisation is granted if the file meets Article 19 and the overall assessment is positive (RJSPME Art 23(2)).

An exempt institution that stops meeting the conditions has 30 calendar days to seek full authorisation (RJSPME Art 37(10)).

After the Banco de Portugal grants the license

  • Register. Authorised institutions appear on the Banco de Portugal's list of authorised institutions.
  • Reporting and supervision. The Banco de Portugal supervises payment institutions prudentially and for conduct. We did not find a published annual supervision fee.
  • Passport. You notify the Banco de Portugal of the states, services, agents and branches. It forwards the notice within 1 month and the host assesses it within 1 month (PSD2 Art 28(2)). Exempt institutions cannot passport.
  • Change of control. Changes in qualifying holdings go to the Banco de Portugal. Confirm the route before any share transfer.

Payment institution license in Portugal FAQs

How much does a payment institution license cost in Portugal?

We found no Banco de Portugal application fee in the material we could read. You need EUR 20,000, 50,000 or 125,000 of capital by service, and a Portuguese company with local management. our fee range for payment institution licence in Portugal

How much capital do you need for a payment institution in Portugal?

EUR 20,000 for money remittance only, EUR 50,000 for payment initiation only and EUR 125,000 for services 1 to 5 (PSD2 Art 7). Exempt institutions need EUR 50,000 of share capital (Portaria 239/2019).

How long does the Banco de Portugal take to decide?

3 months from the date it holds all the information it needs, and never more than 12 months from the initial application (RJSPME Art 23(1)).

What is the Portuguese exemption regime for small payment institutions?

Under RJSPME Art 37 and Portaria 239/2019, a Portuguese company whose payments average no more than EUR 3 million a month over the previous 12 months can apply for a lighter authorisation with EUR 50,000 of share capital. It cannot passport, and the preamble to Decree-Law 91/2018 excludes payment initiation, account information and money transfer services.

Can I passport a Portuguese payment institution licence?

Yes, if you are fully authorised (PSD2 Art 28). Exempt institutions cannot.

Do I need an office in Portugal?

Your head office must be in the same state as your registered office (PSD2 Art 11(3)), so a Portuguese authorisation means a Portuguese company with its head office there.

Does the application have to be in Portuguese?

We did not find a language rule in the material we could read. Ask the Banco de Portugal which documents it accepts in English.

Do I need a licence for account information services only?

Registration with the Banco de Portugal is enough, and no minimum capital applies. You do need professional indemnity insurance (PSD2 Arts 5(3) and 33).
