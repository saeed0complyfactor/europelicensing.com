EMI License in Portugal

An electronic money institution (EMI, or instituição de moeda eletrónica, IME) licence from the Banco de Portugal lets a Portuguese company issue e-money and provide payment services, and passport both across the EU and EEA. The Banco de Portugal decides within 3 months of a complete file, and never later than 12 months after the first filing. Initial capital is EUR 350,000.

Who needs an EMI license in Portugal?

You need one if you issue electronic money in Portugal on a professional basis, for example prepaid cards, wallets with a stored balance or accounts that hold funds redeemable at par (EMD2 Art 11). An EMI may also provide payment services, with or without e-money, so you do not need a second licence for them.

Other routes can fit better.

  • You only transmit money and hold no stored value. The payment institution licence in Portugal has lower capital.
  • You are already an EMI or payment institution in another EEA state. Passport into Portugal under PSD2 Art 28.
  • You only provide account information. That is a registration, not a licence.
  • You want to offer crypto services as well. An EMI can notify the Banco de Portugal under MiCA Art 60 for custody and transfer of the e-money tokens it issues. Wider services need a CASP authorisation, covered in the crypto licence in Portugal.

EMI license requirements in Portugal

Capital and own funds

EMIs with their head office in Portugal must hold share capital of at least EUR 350,000 at authorisation and at all times (RJSPME Art 55; EMD2 Art 4). The capital comes from the own-funds items in Article 26(1)(a) to (e) of Regulation (EU) 575/2013, according to the consolidated text. Own funds are then at least 2% of the average e-money outstanding (EMD2 Art 5(3)). If you also provide payment services, the payment services method applies to that part.

Substance and legal form

PSD2 Art 11(3) requires the head office to sit in the same state as the registered office, and a Portuguese authorisation means a company established in Portugal. We did not find a Banco de Portugal statement on how many directors must be resident, so confirm that when you scope the file.

Management and owners

The file covers the people who direct the business and every holder of a qualifying holding, who are assessed for reputation and experience (PSD2 Art 5(1); RJSPME Art 19). The Banco de Portugal charges no fee for qualifying holding assessments of EMIs, according to its page for them.

Safeguarding

Funds received for e-money are segregated, or covered by an insurance policy or comparable guarantee (EMD2 Art 7; PSD2 Art 10(1)).

AML, governance and ICT

The Banco de Portugal asks applicants to show compliance with the AML/CFT rules that apply to institutions headquartered in Portugal (RJSPME Arts 18(2)(i) and 19(2)(h)). The file also covers governance, internal control, business continuity, security policy and ICT arrangements under DORA (PSD2 Art 5(1)). See our AML and compliance programme.

How to apply for an EMI license in Portugal

  1. Define the business model and list every payment service you want, so the programme of operations is precise.
  2. Read the Banco de Portugal EMI authorisation page and the RJSPME articles it cites, Articles 18 to 23.
  3. Set up the Portuguese company and prove the EUR 350,000 capital.
  4. Build the Article 19 file, which follows the PSD2 Article 5 list and the EBA authorisation guidelines.
  5. Send the notification of intent with all required information. The Banco de Portugal assesses it, asks for more information where needed and then communicates its decision (Banco de Portugal EMI page). Its crypto page names the SIRES portal for filing. We did not find the same statement on the EMI page, so ask which channel to use.
  6. The 3-month period starts when the Banco de Portugal holds all the evidence and information it needs, and the total cannot pass 12 months from the initial filing (RJSPME Art 23(1); Banco de Portugal FAQ).
  7. Authorisation is granted if the file meets Article 19 and the overall assessment is positive (RJSPME Art 23(2)). Passport notifications follow.

We have not confirmed in the RJSPME text what happens if the clock runs out without a decision, so do not plan on a deemed approval.

EMI license cost in Portugal

We did not find an application or supervision fee on the Banco de Portugal pages we could read, and we do not quote one. The Banco de Portugal states that it charges no fee for analysing qualifying holdings in EMIs, and that its crypto authorisations carry no cost, but we have no equivalent statement for EMI authorisation. Ask the Banco de Portugal for its current fee position before you budget.

After the Banco de Portugal grants the license

  • Register. Authorised institutions are listed on the Banco de Portugal's list of authorised institutions.
  • Reporting and supervision. The Banco de Portugal supervises you prudentially and for conduct. We did not find a published annual supervision fee for EMIs.
  • Passport. You notify the Banco de Portugal of the states and services. It forwards the notice within 1 month, and the host state assesses it within 1 month (PSD2 Art 28(2)). E-money distribution abroad goes through distributors, not agents (EMD2 Art 3(4), (5)).
  • Change of control. A proposed acquisition or increase of a qualifying holding in an EMI goes to the Banco de Portugal for assessment.

Portugal compared with other countries

If you only provide payments, see the payment institution licence in Portugal. For crypto services, the Banco de Portugal also authorises CASPs, with a CMVM opinion, covered in the crypto licence in Portugal. For the same licence elsewhere, compare the EMI licence in Spain, where the regulator charges no fee and the clock ends in refusal, Lithuania and Ireland. The EU baseline is on the EMI licence page.

EMI license in Portugal FAQs

How much does an EMI license cost in Portugal?

We found no Banco de Portugal application fee for EMIs in the material we could read. You need EUR 350,000 of capital and a Portuguese company with local management. our fee range for EMI licence in Portugal

How much capital do you need for an EMI license in Portugal?

EUR 350,000 at authorisation and at all times (EMD2 Art 4; RJSPME Art 55). Own funds then stay at or above 2% of the average e-money outstanding (EMD2 Art 5(3)).

How long does the Banco de Portugal take to decide?

The authorisation process lasts 3 months from the date the Banco de Portugal has all the evidence and information it needs, and no more than 12 months from the initial application (RJSPME Art 23(1)). Your preparation time comes before that.

How do I get an EMI license in Portugal?

You set up a Portuguese company, prove the capital, file the Article 19 application with the Banco de Portugal and answer its questions until the decision.

Can a non-resident founder hold a Portuguese EMI?

The Portuguese company needs its head office in Portugal (PSD2 Art 11(3)). We found no nationality rule for shareholders. Directors and owners are assessed for reputation and experience.

Does the application have to be in Portuguese?

We did not find a language rule in the Banco de Portugal material we could read. Ask which documents it accepts in English before you file.

Can a Portuguese EMI serve customers in other EU countries?

Yes, by notification under PSD2 Art 28, applied by EMD2 Art 3(1).

Is there a small EMI licence in Portugal?

We found none for e-money. Portugal has an exemption regime for payment institutions under Article 37 of the RJSPME, covered on the payment institution page.
