Crypto License in Greece

A crypto licence in Greece means authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider (CASP) under MiCA, Regulation (EU) 2023/1114. The Hellenic Capital Market Commission (HCMC) grants it, and one authorisation covers every EU member state. Greece now has CASPs on the ESMA register, so you can plan on a working national procedure.

Who needs a MiCA licence in Greece?

You need CASP authorisation if you provide crypto-asset services to clients as a business: custody and administration, operating a trading platform, exchange, order execution, placing, order reception and transmission, advice, portfolio management or transfer services (MiCA Art 3(1)(16)). The applicant needs a registered office in a member state, its place of effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (Art 59(2)).

Other routes can fit better:

  • You are a bank supervised in Greece. You may notify the Bank of Greece at least 40 working days before starting, instead of applying (MiCA Art 60(1) and (7)). The ESMA list names the Bank of Greece for those notifications.
  • You are an EMI that only wants to offer custody and transfer services for e-money tokens you issue. Art 60(4) lets you notify for those services alone. Any other crypto service needs full authorisation.
  • You are a payment institution. Art 60 gives you no notification route, so you apply for full authorisation. The ESMA list names the Bank of Greece, not the HCMC, for listed Title V provisions when the applicant is a payment institution or EMI, including Art 59 (except paragraph 3) and Arts 62 to 65.
  • You already hold a MiCA authorisation in another member state. Use the passport (Art 65) and skip a Greek application.
  • You are a non-EU firm. ESMA's statement of 23 June 2026 says you cannot serve or solicit EU clients outside reverse solicitation.

Requirements for a MiCA licence in Greece

Capital and prudential safeguards

The minimum capital depends on the services you provide.

Class Minimum capital Services covered
Class 1 EUR 50,000 Order reception and transmission, advice, execution, placing, transfer services, portfolio management
Class 2 EUR 125,000 Class 1 plus custody and administration, and exchange for funds or other crypto-assets
Class 3 EUR 150,000 Class 2 plus operating a trading platform

You hold the higher of that amount or one quarter of the previous year's fixed overheads (Art 67(1)). A new firm uses projected overheads. The safeguards can be Common Equity Tier 1 own funds, an insurance policy or comparable guarantee, or both (Art 67(4)).

Governance and fit and proper

Directors need good repute, knowledge, skills, experience and enough time. Qualifying shareholders need good repute (Art 68). The HCMC provides the qualifying holding questionnaires in Annexes II and III of its decision.

Client assets, complaints and conflicts

Custody needs a client agreement, a custody policy, a register of positions and segregation (Art 75). Complaints handling is free for clients (Art 71), and conflicts are disclosed on your website (Art 72).

ICT and AML

Your ICT must meet DORA, Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, with business continuity plans (Art 68(7)). You also need AML/CFT controls (Art 68(8)). See our AML and compliance programme page.

Substance in Greece

MiCA requires a registered office in a member state and effective management in the EU (Art 59(2)). The HCMC decision sets no further local presence rule, so ask the HCMC what it expects at the pre-application meeting.

How to get a MiCA licence in Greece

  1. Decide on your services and class. The HCMC expects you to have confirmed that your activities fall inside MiCA and to be sure you will apply in Greece.
  2. Request a pre-application meeting. It is optional. Email crypto@cmc.gov.gr. The HCMC sends a one-hour online meeting invitation and a questionnaire (Annex I). You send a presentation with brief answers two days before the meeting. The feedback is general and does not replace the formal assessment (HCMC Decision 8/1059, Art 4(2)).
  3. Prepare the file under MiCA Art 62, Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/305 and Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/306.
  4. File. Complete the HCMC application form (Annex II) and the qualifying holding questionnaires (Annex III), and submit the file in electronic and physical form. The HCMC gives it a protocol number.
  5. Acknowledgement. Within 5 working days of filing (MiCA Art 63(1)).
  6. Completeness. Within 25 working days of receipt. If the file is incomplete, the HCMC sets a deadline to complete it. It tells you immediately once the file is complete.
  7. Decision. A reasoned decision within 40 working days of a complete application, notified within 5 working days of its date (MiCA Art 63(9)).

Preparation time and the HCMC's questions come on top of the statutory clocks.

After authorisation

  • Register: authorised CASPs appear in the ESMA register, which ESMA publishes as csv files updated weekly.
  • Extension of services: you apply to the HCMC to add services (MiCA Art 59(8)).
  • Passport: send a notification to the HCMC listing the states and services. The HCMC forwards it within 10 working days, and you can start on that communication or on the 15th calendar day at the latest (Art 65).
  • Records: keep records for five years, up to seven if the regulator asks (Art 68(9)).
  • Supervision fee and change of control: the HCMC decision does not cover them. Ask the HCMC, and plan for prior notice of acquisitions of qualifying holdings.

Status of the Greek regime on 23 November 2026

The MiCA transitional period for existing providers ended on 1 July 2026 at the latest (Art 143(3)). ESMA's list of grandfathering periods shows Greece at 18 months, with a note that some periods were communicated as expectations and may not be in national law. Whatever the Greek period was, MiCA capped it at 1 July 2026. ESMA's statement of 23 June 2026 says unauthorised CASPs must stop onboarding EU clients and wind down. We found no official statement from the HCMC on what happened to any pre-MiCA national register.

The ESMA register file we downloaded on 23 November 2026 lists four entries with Greece as home Member State. They are BCASH Societe Anonyme (30 June 2026), Xenios Blockchain Group S.A. and Capital Wallet Greece S.A. (both 22 July 2026), all authorised by the HCMC, and Piraeus Bank S.A. (7 August 2026), an Art 60 notification to the Bank of Greece, as dated in the file. Check the live register before you quote a figure.

The HCMC procedure, including the optional pre-application meeting, has run since Decision 8/1059 of 30 July 2025. Decision 9/1079 of 27 January 2026 restated Article 4 and removed the 20-day cap on the period the HCMC sets to complete an incomplete file. Two filing clocks apply: 25 working days to check completeness and 40 working days to decide.

Greece vs other countries

Greece has a designated authority and a published procedure. Compare the MiCA licence in Cyprus, the MiCA licence in Malta and the MiCA licence in Lithuania. In Greece, a bank may notify the Bank of Greece instead of applying, and an EMI may notify only for custody and transfer of its own e-money tokens. A payment institution applies in full, and the Bank of Greece handles the listed provisions. See the EMI licence in Greece and the payment institution licence in Greece.

Crypto license in Greece FAQs

Who grants a MiCA licence in Greece?

The Hellenic Capital Market Commission (HCMC). For credit institutions, EMIs and payment institutions, the Bank of Greece holds the listed provisions, including Art 60 notifications by credit institutions and EMIs and Arts 62 to 65 for applications.

How long does MiCA authorisation take in Greece?

The HCMC checks completeness within 25 working days of receipt and decides within 40 working days of a complete application (HCMC Decision Art 4(3); MiCA Art 63). Preparation and the HCMC's questions come on top, and we do not quote a timeline.

How much capital do you need for a crypto licence in Greece?

EUR 50,000, EUR 125,000 or EUR 150,000 depending on the services (MiCA Annex IV), or one quarter of fixed overheads if higher (Art 67).

How much does it cost to apply?

We found no application fee in the HCMC decisions and no published tariff. The HCMC's fee schedule sets any fee, so ask the HCMC.

Is there a pre-application stage?

Yes, optional. Email crypto@cmc.gov.gr. You get a one-hour online meeting and send a presentation two days before. The feedback is general.

Has the Greek MiCA transitional period ended?

Yes. No transitional period could run past 1 July 2026 (Art 143(3)). ESMA's statement of 23 June 2026 says unauthorised CASPs must stop onboarding EU clients and wind down.

How many CASPs does Greece have?

The ESMA register file downloaded on 23 November 2026 lists four entries with Greece as home Member State. Check the live register.

Can I file in English?

The HCMC decision states no language rule. Ask the HCMC at the pre-application meeting.
