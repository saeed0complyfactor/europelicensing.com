Payment Institution License in Greece

A Greek payment institution (PI) licence lets you provide payment services from Greece and passport them across the EU and EEA. The Bank of Greece grants it under Law 4537/2018, which transposes PSD2. The Bank's published procedure lists a 3-month processing time and a EUR 5,000 filing fee, and it runs on paper.

Who needs a payment institution licence in Greece?

You need Bank of Greece authorisation if you provide payment services as a business in Greece and are not a bank, an EMI or a licensed provider from another EEA state using its passport. The services are the eight in PSD2 Annex I, such as money remittance, card acquiring and issuing, payment initiation and account information.

Check these alternatives first:

  • You only read account data. Account information providers register with the Bank of Greece in the Article 14 register of Law 4537/2018 (Act 164/2/2019, Chapter II). You apply under the PI chapter only if you add other payment services.
  • You hold customer balances as stored value. That is e-money, and you need an EMI licence in Greece.
  • You hold a licence in another EEA state. Notify your home regulator and use the passport.
  • You want to offer crypto services next to payments. MiCA Art 60 gives payment institutions no notification route, so a Greek PI applies for full CASP authorisation. The ESMA list of competent authorities names the Bank of Greece, not the HCMC, for listed MiCA Title V provisions when the applicant is a payment institution, including Art 59 (except paragraph 3) and Arts 62 to 65. See our MiCA CASP page for Greece.
  • You hope for a small-institution waiver. PSD2 Art 32 lets states exempt small firms, and the Bank of Greece says its register records exempted payment institutions. Ask the Bank for the conditions and thresholds before you plan around one.

Requirements for a payment institution licence in Greece

Capital and own funds

PSD2 Art 7 sets EUR 125,000 for services 1 to 5 of Annex I, EUR 50,000 for payment initiation and EUR 20,000 for money remittance only. Article 7 of Law 4537/2018 transposes it, and we could not read the Greek wording, so check it with the Bank. Account information providers hold no initial capital (PSD2 Art 33).

The capital must be fully paid up and deposited with a credit institution in Greece, or one operating in Greece through a branch, that is not a related party. It stays deposited at least until the licence is granted. You show where each contributor's money came from, from an independent and reliable source. An existing company adds a statutory auditor's certificate that net equity is not below the minimum.

Legal form and substance

The Bank's published procedure is for a société anonyme (SA) or a European company (SE). The company must exist when the licence is granted, so one still in formation completes incorporation first. The file states the legal form, registered seat and head office address (Act 164/2/2019, Chapter I, 2.1).

Management and shareholders

Board members and senior managers fill in the suitability questionnaires (annex 16). Shareholders with qualifying holdings are identified in annex 15, and the Bank may ask for details on holders above 1% (Act 164/2/2019, 15.4).

Safeguarding

If you hold users' funds, you segregate them in a deposit or custody account at an unrelated credit institution in Greece, or cover them with insurance or a comparable guarantee from a provider outside your group (annex 7; PSD2 Art 10).

Insurance, ICT and AML

Payment initiation and account information services need professional indemnity insurance or a comparable guarantee, sized under Chapter V of Act 164/2/2019. The 19 annexes also cover security incident and complaints procedures, sensitive payment data, business continuity, a security policy and AML/CFT controls. Add your DORA (Regulation (EU) 2022/2554) documentation. See our AML and compliance programme page.

How to apply for a payment institution licence in Greece

  1. Set your services and legal form. Incorporate the SA or SE, or plan to finish incorporation before the decision. The Bank recommends its optional pre-application procedure, two meetings with Bank staff, before you file.
  2. Pay in the capital to a Greek credit institution and keep the evidence.
  3. Prepare the application and the 19 annexes listed on the Bank of Greece service page. The annex numbers stay fixed even when an annex does not apply to you.
  4. Sign. Two authorised natural persons sign the application, and their signatures are notarised. Certificates come from authorised persons, are legalised and are translated into Greek or English.
  5. File. Deliver a sealed paper file in person or by post to the Bank of Greece, Directorate of Supervision of Supervised Companies, 21 Eleftheriou Venizelou St., 102 50 Athens, with a USB or CD of PDF scans. There is no online filing. Pay the EUR 5,000 fee at submission.
  6. Completeness and questions. The Bank tells you in writing about gaps and confirms the date it received a complete application (Act 164/2/2019, Chapter IV). It may ask for more information at any stage.
  7. Decision. The service page lists 3 months as the processing time, and EU law sets a decision within 3 months of a complete application (PSD2 Art 12). The Credit and Insurance Committee decides, and you get the decision in person, by post and by email.
  8. Remedies. You can file an application for review under Article 24 of the Code of Administrative Procedure, and decisions can be challenged before the Council of State under Article 26 of Law 4537/2018.

EUR 20,000, EUR 50,000 or EUR 125,000 by service type (PSD2 Art 7), deposited in Greece before the licence is granted.

After authorisation

  • Register: the Bank of Greece publishes a list of payment institutions authorised in Greece and a list of EEA institutions that notified services in Greece. We could not open the list pages, so we give no count.
  • Reporting: semi-annual reports within 20 working days after the half-year ends, and annual financial statements within 5 working days after publication (Act 164/2/2019, Chapter XI, 3.1). Major incidents are reported under Executive Committee Act 157/3/2.4.2019.
  • Outsourcing: written notice at least 30 working days ahead (Chapter VIII, 4).
  • Qualifying holdings: prior notification with the Bank's questionnaires (Act 142/11.6.2018).
  • Changes: notify material changes immediately (Chapter XII).
  • Agents and branches: notify them with supporting documents (Chapter X, 3 and Chapter XIII). The register records agents and branches in other EEA states.
  • Passport: your authorisation is valid in all Member States (PSD2 Art 11(9)). You notify the Bank of the states, services, agents and branches. The home regulator forwards the notice within 1 month and the host assesses it within 1 month (PSD2 Art 28).
  • Secrecy: information you give the Bank is covered by professional secrecy (Law 4537/2018 Art 25).

Greece vs other countries

Greece asks for an SA or SE, a paper file and a Greek capital deposit. Compare the payment institution licence in Cyprus, the payment institution licence in Lithuania and the one in Malta. In Greece, the EMI licence needs EUR 350,000 and the MiCA CASP licence comes from the HCMC.

Payment institution in Greece FAQs

How much capital do you need for a payment institution licence in Greece?

PSD2 Art 7 sets EUR 125,000 for most services, EUR 50,000 for payment initiation and EUR 20,000 for money remittance only. Law 4537/2018 Art 7 transposes it. Account information providers hold none (PSD2 Art 33).

How much does the Bank of Greece charge?

EUR 5,000 at submission, under Executive Committee Act 238/2/10.01.2025 Chapter IV, as shown on the Bank's service page. We found no annual supervision fee figure.

How long does a payment institution licence take in Greece?

The service page lists 3 months, and EU law sets a decision within 3 months of a complete application (PSD2 Art 12). The Bank may ask for more information at any stage, and file preparation comes before the clock starts. We do not quote a timeline.

What legal form do you need?

The Bank's published procedure is for a société anonyme or a European company (SE). Incorporate before the licence is granted.

Can I apply online?

No. The service page says the filing is in person or by post in a sealed envelope, with a USB or CD of PDF scans.

Which language do I file in?

Greek or English. Certificates must be certified and translated into Greek or English.

Can I appeal a refusal?

Yes. You can file an application for review under Article 24 of the Code of Administrative Procedure, and you can challenge the decision before the Council of State under Article 26 of Law 4537/2018.

Is there a small payment institution tier in Greece?

PSD2 Art 32 leaves the choice to each state, and the Bank of Greece says its register records exempted payment institutions. We could not confirm the conditions, so ask the Bank.
