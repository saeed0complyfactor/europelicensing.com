What happens next

  1. We read your message and check which licence your product points to.
  2. On the call we map your payment or crypto flows to a licence and a capital class, and shortlist countries.
  3. You receive a document list, a capital estimate and a scoped fee estimate for the application.

Before you get in touch

What should I prepare for the scoping call?

A short description of your product, how money or crypto-assets move through it, the countries where your customers are, and who will own and run the company. A draft business plan helps but is not needed.

Is the first call free?

confirm whether the scoping call is free and how long it lasts

Do you work with firms outside the EU?

Yes. Many applicants are groups from outside the EEA setting up an EU or EEA entity. The regulator of the country where that entity is established grants the licence.

Can you guarantee that we get the licence?

No. The regulator decides, and it must give reasons if it refuses (PSD2 Art 12, MiCA Art 63(9)). We build a complete file and help you answer the regulator's questions.
