Crypto License in Lithuania

A crypto licence in Lithuania is an authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider (CASP) under MiCA, granted by the Bank of Lithuania. It is for exchanges, custodians, brokers, transfer services and other crypto firms that want an EU passport from Lithuania. Lithuania's 12-month MiCA transition ran out at the end of 2025, so the old VASP registration no longer lets you operate.

Who needs a crypto license in Lithuania?

You need CASP authorisation to provide any of the ten MiCA crypto-asset services as a business: custody, trading platform, exchange for funds or other crypto-assets, order execution, placing, order reception and transmission, advice, portfolio management and transfer services (MiCA Art 3(1)(16)). The applicant needs a registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (Art 59(2)).

Other routes can fit:

  • If you already hold an EMI licence, you may notify the Bank under MiCA Art 60 for custody and transfer of your own e-money tokens, at least 40 working days before you start. Wider services need CASP authorisation. See the EMI licence in Lithuania.
  • If you hold a bank, investment firm or other Art 60 licence, you may notify instead of applying for the services your licence covers.
  • If you are authorised in another member state, you passport into Lithuania under Art 65 and need no Lithuanian licence.
  • If you handle e-money tokens in ways that count as payment services, such as transfers on a client's behalf, you may also need payment institution authorisation. The EBA advised national authorities in June 2025 to require it from 2 March 2026. See the payment institution licence in Lithuania.

Lithuania crypto license requirements

Capital by class

Class Minimum capital Services
Class 1 EUR 50,000 Order reception and transmission, advice, execution, placing, transfer, portfolio management
Class 2 EUR 125,000 Class 1 plus custody and exchange services
Class 3 EUR 150,000 Class 2 plus operating a trading platform

You hold the higher of the class minimum and one quarter of the preceding year's fixed overheads, using projected overheads in your first year (MiCA Art 67(1), (2)). Safeguards can be CET1 own funds, insurance or both (Art 67(4)).

Management and fit and proper

Directors need good repute, knowledge, skills, experience and enough time. Qualifying shareholders need good repute (MiCA Art 68(1), (2)). The regulator must refuse if they fail the test (Art 63(10)).

Substance

MiCA requires effective management in the EU and an EU-resident director (Art 59(2)). We could not retrieve the Bank's written view on local staff or office, so confirm local expectations before you apply.

AML officer

Art 68(8) requires AML/CFT controls. Plan for a named AML compliance officer and a business-wide risk assessment. See our AML and compliance programme.

Client assets and custody

Client funds go to a credit institution or central bank by the end of the next business day, in separate accounts (MiCA Art 70). Custody needs a client agreement, a custody policy, a position register and statements at least quarterly (Art 75).

ICT and DORA

Your ICT systems must meet Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, with continuity and recovery plans (MiCA Art 68(7)).

How to get a crypto license in Lithuania

  1. Map your services to the ten crypto-asset services to fix your capital class.
  2. Contact the Bank of Lithuania before you apply, and read its CASP authorisation page.
  3. Set up a legal entity with the capital and an EU-resident director.
  4. Build the MiCA Art 62(2) file: programme of operations, governance, fit and proper evidence, AML/CFT, ICT, custody and complaints policies.
  5. Submit in Lithuanian or English. The Bank may ask for Lithuanian translations.
  6. The Bank acknowledges receipt within 5 working days and checks completeness within 25 working days (MiCA Art 63(1), (2)).
  7. It decides within 40 working days of a complete application. Questions raised by the 20th working day pause the clock for up to 20 working days (Art 63(9), (12)).
  8. Within 2 working days of a grant, ESMA is informed for its register (Art 63(13)).

These are statutory limits. Preparation sits outside them.

After you get the crypto license

  • The Bank enters you in its register, and ESMA lists you in its MiCA register.
  • You meet the ongoing MiCA rules on capital, governance, custody, complaints and conflicts of interest, and pay supervision fees to the Bank.
  • To serve other EU states, you notify the Bank, which forwards the notice within 10 working days. You may start on receipt of that notice or on the 15th calendar day at the latest (MiCA Art 65).
  • Adding services later needs an extension of authorisation (Art 59(8)).
  • A change in qualifying holders needs regulator approval.

Is the Lithuanian crypto transition over?

Yes. Lithuania chose a 12-month transitional period under MiCA Art 143(3), counted from 30 December 2024, according to ESMA's list of national grandfathering periods. It ran out at the end of 2025, and a provider without a MiCA licence no longer has the right to operate in Lithuania. The old Lithuanian VASP registration no longer authorises crypto-asset services. EU-wide, the MiCA transition ended no later than 1 July 2026 (MiCA Art 143(3)).

Lithuania crypto license vs other countries

Lithuania has a short list of authorised firms and a regulator that decides inside the MiCA clocks. See the MiCA licence in Malta, Cyprus and Ireland. In Lithuania, the sibling pages are the EMI licence and the payment institution licence. The EU-wide rules sit in our MiCA CASP licence guide.

Crypto license in Lithuania FAQs

How much does a crypto license cost in Lithuania?

Capital of EUR 50,000, 125,000 or 150,000 by class, or a quarter of fixed overheads if higher (MiCA Art 67, Annex IV). The Bank of Lithuania's fee schedule sets the regulator fee, and we could not confirm an official amount. Running costs come on top.

What are the Lithuania crypto license requirements?

A registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU, an EU-resident director, minimum capital for your class, fit and proper directors and shareholders, AML/CFT controls, DORA-compliant ICT, custody and complaints policies (MiCA Art 59, 62, 67, 68).

How long does a MiCA license take in Lithuania?

The Bank checks completeness within 25 working days and decides within 40 working days of a complete application, with up to 20 working days of pause for questions (MiCA Art 63). Preparation is extra.

What is the difference between a VASP and a CASP in Lithuania?

A VASP was a national AML registration. A CASP is authorised under MiCA with capital, governance, conduct and custody rules and an EU passport. The VASP route ended when the transition ran out at the end of 2025.

How many CASPs are licensed in Lithuania?

On 23 November 2026, the ESMA interim register lists 4 Lithuanian CASPs authorised under Art 63 and 3 Art 60 notifications.

Can I operate in Lithuania with another EU state's MiCA licence?

Yes, after your home regulator forwards your Art 65 notification, or on the 15th calendar day at the latest.

Can a non-EU company get a Lithuanian MiCA licence?

Through an EU entity with a registered office in a member state, effective management in the EU and an EU-resident director (MiCA Art 59(2)).

Can I file in English?

Yes. The Bank accepts Lithuanian or English and may ask for Lithuanian translations.
