Crypto License in Ireland

A crypto licence in Ireland is an authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider (CASP) from the Central Bank of Ireland under MiCA. It lets you offer custody, exchange, trading platform, transfer and similar services in Ireland and, by passport, across the EU.

Ireland's own transition period for former VASPs ended on 29 December 2025, so a new applicant must be authorised before it serves clients.

Who needs a crypto licence in Ireland?

You need CASP authorisation to provide crypto-asset services as a business to clients in the EU, unless you are authorised under another regime and use the notification route. The services and their classes are in MiCA Annex IV.

Other routes:

  • MiCA Article 60 notification. Credit institutions, EMIs, investment firms and some other regulated firms can notify instead of applying, at least 40 working days before starting. The Central Bank has a Notification Form on its MiCAR page and asks you to contact your supervision team first. An EMI is limited to custody and transfer services for the e-money tokens it issues.
  • Authorisation in another member state, then a passport into Ireland. If you do not need an Irish home regulator, this avoids the Irish queue.
  • A payment institution licence or a partnership with a payment provider, if you transfer e-money tokens. See the payment institution licence in Ireland.

Requirements for a CASP licence in Ireland

Capital

The class 1 minimum is EUR 50,000, class 2 is EUR 125,000 and class 3, which includes operating a trading platform, is EUR 150,000. You hold the higher of that figure and one quarter of the previous year's fixed overheads (Art 67(1)). A firm in its first year uses projected overheads (Art 67(2)).

Substance and management

The applicant needs a registered office in a member state where it carries on part of its services, place of effective management in the EU and at least one EU-resident director (Art 59(2)). Directors must be of good repute with the necessary knowledge, skills, experience and time (Art 68(1)). The Central Bank treats every CASP application as a new authorisation, including from registered VASPs.

Conduct and custody

Client crypto-assets cannot be used for your own account (Art 70(1)). Client funds go to a credit institution or central bank by the end of the next business day (Art 70). Custody needs an agreement, a custody policy and segregation (Art 75). Complaints (Art 71), conflicts of interest (Art 72) and outsourcing (Art 73) each have their own rules.

AML and ICT

You need AML/CFT controls (Art 68(8)) and ICT arrangements under Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (Art 68(7)). Records are kept five years, or seven on request (Art 68(9)).

How to apply for a CASP licence in Ireland

The Central Bank publishes five stages.

  1. Initial engagement. Email CASPAuthorisations@centralbank.ie. Submit a presentation on your proposal at least 10 working days before the meeting.
  2. Key Facts Document (KFD) stage. The Bank assesses your KFD and expects continuous engagement. It gives no timeframe for this stage.
  3. Formal application. Since 2 April 2026, all application documents go through the Central Bank Portal. The Bank reviews completeness within 25 working days and can refuse to review applications that stay incomplete after the deadline it sets (Art 63(3)).
  4. Assessment. The Bank has 40 working days to assess the complete application, with one suspension of up to 20 working days if it requests more information.
  5. Decision. The Bank communicates the outcome within 5 working days of deciding.

The statutory clocks are in MiCA Art 63. They start only when the file is complete. In its 2025 report the Central Bank gives an average of 171 calendar days for CASP authorisations, measured across the KFD and formal application stages.

After authorisation

The Central Bank passes your details to ESMA, which publishes them in its register. You pay the annual levy and meet the Art 68 to 75 requirements on an ongoing basis. For passporting, give the Bank the target states, services and start date, and it forwards them to host states, ESMA and the EBA within 10 working days (Art 65). Adding services needs an extension from the Bank (Art 59(8)). The Central Bank held a CASP supervisory industry briefing in March 2026.

Status of the Irish crypto regime

  • Ireland set a 12-month transitional period for firms registered as VASPs before 30 December 2024. The Central Bank states it ended on 29 December 2025. EU-wide, no transitional period can run past 1 July 2026 (MiCA Art 143(3)).
  • A VASP that had not applied, or whose application was refused, had to stop operating. Firms not registered and operating by 30 December 2024 cannot use the transition and cannot operate as a CASP until authorised. VASP registration does not simplify a CASP assessment.
  • The Central Bank's 2025 report counts 11 CASP applications and 10 authorisations in 2025, eight of them previously registered VASPs, and expects a similar number in 2026.
  • The ESMA interim MiCA register file, downloaded on 23 November 2026, lists 12 CASP entries with Ireland as home member state.

Ireland compared with other CASP jurisdictions

Ireland authorised ten CASPs in 2025 and publishes its stage timings. Compare the crypto licence in Lithuania and the crypto licence in Malta. In Ireland, the EMI licence and payment institution licence cover stored value and payments.

Crypto license in Ireland FAQs

How do I get a crypto licence in Ireland?

Apply to the Central Bank of Ireland for CASP authorisation under MiCA. Email CASPAuthorisations@centralbank.ie, present your proposal, complete the KFD, then submit the formal application through the Central Bank Portal.

How much capital does a CASP need in Ireland?

EUR 50,000, 125,000 or 150,000 depending on the services, or one quarter of fixed overheads if higher (MiCA Art 67, Annex IV).

How long does CASP authorisation take in Ireland?

MiCA gives 25 working days for completeness and 40 working days to decide, with one pause of up to 20 working days (Art 63). The Central Bank's 2025 report gives an average of 171 calendar days overall.

What does it cost?

The Central Bank's CASP page does not state an application fee. The annual levy is EUR 30,000 for a full year in the 2026 guide. Capital and your own set-up costs come on top.

Is the MiCA transition period over in Ireland?

Yes. The Central Bank says Ireland's period ended on 29 December 2025, and no member state's period could run past 1 July 2026.

Does my VASP registration let me skip the CASP process?

No. The Central Bank says every CASP application is a new authorisation and VASP registration does not give a simplified assessment.

Can an EMI or bank offer crypto services in Ireland without applying?

Some can notify under MiCA Art 60 at least 40 working days before starting, using the Central Bank's Notification Form.

Do I need an Irish office?

You need a registered office in a member state where you carry out part of your services, effective management in the EU and an EU-resident director (Art 59(2)). The Central Bank's page does not set an Irish-specific rule beyond that.
